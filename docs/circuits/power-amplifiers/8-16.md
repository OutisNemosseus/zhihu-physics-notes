# 8.16 — class-A BJT 输出范围

来源：Donald A. Neamen, *Microelectronics: Circuit Analysis and Design*, 4th ed.，教材页 607（PDF 第 630 页）。

## 题目

Consider the class-A emitter-follower circuit shown in Figure P8.16. Assume all transistors are matched with VBE(on) = 0.7 V, VCE(sat) = 0.2 V, and VA = ∞. Neglect base currents. Determine the maximum and minimum values of output voltage and the corresponding input voltages for the circuit to operate in the linear region.
