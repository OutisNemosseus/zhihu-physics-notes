## Weeks 1–3

### Week 1 - Simulation Assignment #1 - SPICE Installation
**Type:** Assignment, 10 points. **Guide:** Install LTspice or the instructor-approved SPICE program, open a test circuit, run it, and submit a screenshot showing the circuit and the simulation result. Include the program version and operating system.

### Homework #1: Sinusoids and Phasors
**Type:** Homework, 20 points. **Guide:** For each sinusoid, identify amplitude, angular frequency, frequency, phase, and RMS value; convert between time-domain and phasor form; show units and the reference convention.

### Week 2 - Lab 0: Lab Kit Inventory
**Type:** Lab, 10 points. **Guide:** Check every item against the provided inventory. Record manufacturer/model and quantity, photograph missing or damaged items, and submit the completed inventory table.

### Homework #2: Impedance
**Type:** Homework, 30 points. **Guide:** Convert each resistor, capacitor, and inductor to impedance at the stated frequency. Combine series and parallel impedances, keeping complex arithmetic visible. Report magnitude and phase with units.

### Week 2 - Lab 1: Installing the Analog Devices ADALM2000 SW-FW-Drivers
**Type:** Lab, 10 points. **Guide:** Install the software and firmware, connect the ADALM2000, verify that the device is detected, and submit the requested screenshots and version information.

### Week 3 Lab - Lab 2: Characterizing Sinusoidal Signals
**Type:** Lab, 20 points. **Guide:** Capture the specified sinusoid with the ADALM2000. Report frequency, period, peak-to-peak value, RMS value, offset, and phase. Include screenshots with scales visible and compare measured values with theory.

### Week 3 - Lab: Get to know your lab partners
**Type:** Assignment, 5 points. **Guide:** Record the required partner names, contact information, roles, and agreed communication method. Use the actual group information.

### Homework #3: Circuit Analysis with Phasors and Impedance (Node Voltage and Mesh Current)
**Type:** Homework, 35 points. **Guide:** Redraw the AC circuit, label reference nodes and currents, replace components by impedances, write KCL or KVL equations, solve the complex linear system, and convert requested phasors to magnitude-phase form.

**Type:** Group report, 0 points. **Guide:** The group leader submits one report containing objective, method, shared data, plots, error discussion, and member contributions.

**Type:** Assignment, 4 points. **Guide:** No student submission is normally required. Check Canvas for the recorded participation score.

### Homework #4: Instantaneous and Average Power Calculations
**Type:** Homework, 20 points. **Guide:** Write voltage and current in consistent sinusoidal conventions. Compute instantaneous power p(t), average power, and any requested RMS quantities. Show the trigonometric identity used.

### Homework #5: Complex Power
**Type:** Homework, 20 points. **Guide:** Use RMS phasors and S = V I*; report real power P, reactive power Q, apparent power |S|, and power factor with leading/lagging sign. Check conservation of complex power.
