# About

This website collects Tong Su's personal notes on physics, mathematics, semiconductors, and computational research. Its purpose is to keep technical explanations and research directions in a clear, durable, and searchable form.

Several notes were originally published as Chinese answers on Zhihu and were later translated or lightly edited into English. Each such article retains its original Chinese title and a link to the corresponding Zhihu source.

A link to the author's Zhihu profile can be added here later.
