Input/output · mass per unit length · 01

Estimate cable mass from a short sample

A uniform cable sample has mass m in grams and length ell in centimetres. Read positive m,ell and target cable length L in metres. Calculate linear density in kg/m and target mass in kg and grams. Test m = 18, ell = 75, L = 4.5. Print three decimals with units. Assume the target cable has the same construction and no connectors.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\lambda=(m/1000)/(\ell/100)\), \(M_{kg}=\lambda L\), \(M_g=1000M_{kg}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先把样品质量和长度都换成与 kg/m 匹配的单位，再按目标长度放大。

Composite geometry · face area and cut length · 02

A triangular sheet with a centered round opening

An equilateral triangular sheet of side a cm has a circular opening of radius r cm centered at its incenter. Read a,r, thickness t in mm, and density rho in g/cm³, with a > 0, 0 < r < a√3/6, t > 0, rho > 0. Calculate remaining face area in cm², total cut-boundary length (all three outside edges plus the circle) in cm, and mass in grams. Test a = 18, r = 2, t = 2, rho = 7.85. Print three decimals; ignore cutting losses.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=\sqrt3\,a^2/4-\pi r^2\), \(P=3a+2\pi r\), \(m=\rho A(t/10)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

面积减去圆孔，边界长度则把圆孔周长加上；给定半径限制保证圆孔完全落在三角形内部。

Inverse geometry · litres and centimetres · 03

Infer cone dimensions from its capacity

An ideal right circular cone has internal capacity V in litres and perpendicular height h in centimetres. Read V > 0 and h > 0. Calculate base radius, slant length and curved surface area (excluding the circular base). Test V = 0.300 and h = 12. Print three decimals in cm or cm² as appropriate. Ignore wall thickness and use the supplied geometric formulas.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V_{cm^3}=1000V\), \(r=\sqrt{3V_{cm^3}/(\pi h)}\), \(s=\sqrt{r^2+h^2}\), \(A_{curved}=\pi rs\).
中文提示（不含解答）

容量先换成 cm³，再从体积公式解出半径；垂直高度与斜高不能混用。

A supplied vector model · coordinates and direction · 04

Add a vehicle velocity to a moving medium

In a planar model, east is +x and north is +y. Read a vehicle velocity (vx,vy) relative to a moving medium and the medium velocity (ux,uy) relative to the ground, all in m/s. The supplied model adds these vectors. Calculate ground-velocity components, speed and counterclockwise direction from east using atan2. Test (vx,vy)=(4,0), (ux,uy)=(−1,3). Print three decimals. Assume the resulting ground velocity is nonzero; report direction in (−180°,180°].

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(g_x=v_x+u_x\), \(g_y=v_y+u_y\), \(s=\sqrt{g_x^2+g_y^2}\), \(\theta=\operatorname{degrees}(\operatorname{atan2}(g_y,g_x))\). If the function gives −180°, report +180°.
中文提示（不含解答）

速度分量先按同一坐标轴相加；方向使用 atan2，不能只对两个分量的商使用 atan。

Pressure and area · kPa and millimetres · 05

Share a uniform pressure load among bolts

A circular cover of diameter d mm carries a uniform pressure difference p kPa. Read p > 0, d > 0 and positive integer bolt count n. Under a supplied equal-sharing model, calculate cover area in m², total force in N and force per bolt in N. Test p = 120, d = 80, n = 6. Print area to six decimals and forces to three. Ignore bolt pretension, deformation and all other loads.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=\pi(d/1000)^2/4\), \(F=(1000p)A\), \(F_b=F/n\). One pascal is one newton per square metre.
中文提示（不含解答）

压强换成 Pa，直径换成 m 后才能得到牛顿；最后才把总力均分。

A supplied energy balance · percentages · 06

Infer efficiency from useful output and operating time

A device draws constant input power P watts for t minutes and provides useful output energy Eout joules. Read P > 0, t > 0 and Eout with 0 < Eout ≤ 60Pt. Calculate input energy, useful-energy efficiency as a percentage, and energy not counted as useful output. Test P = 80, t = 2.5, Eout = 9000. Print three decimals and units. Use only the supplied energy-accounting model.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(E_{in}=60Pt\), \(\eta=100E_{out}/E_{in}\), \(E_{other}=E_{in}-E_{out}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

瓦特是每秒焦耳；先换时间，再用有效输出除以输入。

Given algebraic model · speed conversion and two stages · 07

Evaluate an ideal stopping-distance formula

An ideal mathematical model has a constant-speed reaction stage followed by constant-magnitude deceleration to rest. Read initial speed u in km/h, reaction time tr in seconds and deceleration magnitude a in m/s², with u > 0, tr ≥ 0, a > 0. Calculate speed in m/s, braking time, braking distance and total distance including the reaction stage. Test u = 54, tr = 0.8, a = 3. Print three decimals. This exercise evaluates the supplied ideal model, without road or vehicle effects.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(v=u/3.6\), \(t_b=v/a\), \(d_b=v^2/(2a)\), \(d_{total}=vt_r+d_b\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先换成 m/s；反应阶段仍以初速度运动，制动阶段才使用减速度公式。

math.log10 · inverse formula without premature rounding · 08

Evaluate a supplied amplitude-ratio level

A supplied amplitude-ratio level is G = 20 log10(Vout/Vin) in dB. Read positive Vin,Vout in volts. Compute G to three decimals and reconstruct Vout from the unrounded G to six decimals. Test Vin = 8 and Vout = 2. Also calculate the change in G when Vout is halved while Vin stays fixed. Do not round the logarithm before using the inverse formula.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(G=20\log_{10}(V_{out}/V_{in})\), \(V_{out}=V_{in}10^{G/20}\). Use math.log10.
中文提示（不含解答）

对数输入是相同单位量的比值；小于 1 的比值会给出负的对数。

Debugging · inverse units and f-string formatting · 09

Repair a frequency report from a millisecond period

The program should read a positive period in milliseconds and report frequency in Hz and angular frequency in rad/s to three decimals. Repair all errors and test T_ms = 2.5. Explain why replacing 2π by 360 gives a different angular unit.

import math
T_ms = float(input("Period (ms): "))
f = 1 / T_ms
omega = 360 * f
print("f={f:.3f} Hz, omega={omega:.3f} deg/s")
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(T_s=T_{ms}/1000\), \(f=1/T_s\), \(\omega=2\pi f\).
中文提示（不含解答）

毫秒周期需先换成秒；输出插值要用 f-string，并把弧度与角度单位区分开。

Supplied geometry · slant height and total area · 10

A solid frustum with square parallel faces

A solid square-pyramid frustum has parallel, centered square faces of side a and b cm, with b > a > 0, and perpendicular height h > 0 cm. Read a,b,h and density rho > 0 in g/cm³. Use the supplied formulas to calculate the slant height of a trapezoidal side face, volume, total outside area including both square faces, and mass. Test a = 4, b = 10, h = 4, rho = 1.25. Print three decimals.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(s=\sqrt{h^2+[(b-a)/2]^2}\), \(V=h(a^2+ab+b^2)/3\), \(A=2(a+b)s+a^2+b^2\), \(m=\rho V\).
中文提示（不含解答）

侧面的斜高由垂直高度和两底面边长差的一半组成直角三角形；总面积还需要两块正方形底面。
