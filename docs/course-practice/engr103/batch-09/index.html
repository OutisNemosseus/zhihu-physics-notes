Input/output · inches to SI area · 01

Convert a drawing into a sheet area

A rectangular sheet is specified by length L and width W in inches. Read positive L and W, and calculate the two dimensions in millimetres and the sheet area in square metres. Use exactly 1 inch = 25.4 mm. Test L = 18 and W = 12. Print dimensions to two decimals and area to six decimals. Explain why multiplying the area in square inches by 25.4 only once would give the wrong units.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(L_{mm}=25.4L\), \(W_{mm}=25.4W\), \(A_{m^2}=L_{mm}W_{mm}/10^6\).
中文提示（不含解答）

面积换算要把长度换算系数平方；先分别转换长宽也可以。

A supplied volume formula · similar sections · 02

Capacity of a rectangular pyramidal hopper

A hopper is the portion of a rectangular pyramid between two parallel cuts. The top rectangle is a by b, the bottom rectangle is c by d, and vertical height is h; all dimensions are metres. The cuts are similar and aligned, so a/c = b/d > 1. Read positive a,b,c,d,h satisfying that assumption; validation code is not required. Calculate capacity in cubic metres and litres using the supplied formula. Test a = 1.2, b = 0.8, c = 0.6, d = 0.4, h = 0.9. Print three decimals. Do not include wall thickness.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A_1=ab,\ A_2=cd,\ V=\frac h3(A_1+A_2+\sqrt{A_1A_2})\). 1 m³ = 1000 L.
中文提示（不含解答）

这是相似矩形截面的棱台；先算上下截面积，平方根作用在两个面积的乘积上。

Geometry · exclude the base and top · 03

Line the curved inside wall of a tank

An open cylindrical tank has inner diameter D in metres and wall height H in metres. Only its curved inside wall will be covered with a thin liner; neither the base nor any top surface is covered. Read D > 0, H > 0, and liner thickness t > 0 in millimetres. Under a supplied thin-sheet model, calculate wall area in m² and liner material volume in litres as area times thickness. Test D = 1.6, H = 2.5, t = 1.2. Print three decimals. State that this is a thin-sheet estimate rather than the exact volume of a thick cylindrical shell.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=\pi DH\), \(V=A(t/1000)\;\mathrm{m^3}\). 1 m³ = 1000 L.
中文提示（不含解答）

展开后的曲面是周长乘高度的矩形；厚度先从 mm 换成 m。

Radians · revolutions and rim distance · 04

Rotation through a specified time interval

A wheel of radius r centimetres turns at constant speed n revolutions per minute. Read positive r,n and elapsed time t seconds. Calculate angular speed in rad/s, number of revolutions during t, angle turned in radians, and distance travelled by a point on the rim along its circular path in metres. Test r = 15, n = 120, t = 7.5. Print three decimals; the accumulated angle may exceed one revolution.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\omega=2\pi n/60\), \(N=nt/60\), \(\theta=\omega t\), \(s=(r/100)\theta\).
中文提示（不含解答）

不要把累计角度取成一圈以内；圆周路径长度等于半径乘累计弧度。

Cosine law · included angle · 05

Find an inaccessible side from two ranges

Two straight ranges from the same observation point have lengths a and b in metres and included angle theta_deg in degrees. Their far endpoints cannot be joined directly for measurement. Read positive a,b and 0 < theta_deg < 180. Calculate the straight-line distance between the far endpoints using the cosine law. Test a = 35, b = 55, theta_deg = 60. Print three decimals. Also calculate the triangle area from the same inputs, in m².

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(c=\sqrt{a^2+b^2-2ab\cos\theta}\), \(A=ab\sin\theta/2\).
中文提示（不含解答）

夹角是在共同观测点；计算 cos 和 sin 前，统一换成弧度。

Units · a supplied hydrostatic model · 06

Pressure difference between two submerged sensors

Two pressure sensors are in the same stationary liquid, at depths h1 and h2 measured downward from the free surface. Read h1,h2 in centimetres with h2 > h1 ≥ 0 and density rho in kg/m³ with rho > 0. With g = 9.81 m/s², calculate the pressure at the deeper sensor minus that at the shallower sensor in Pa and kPa. Test h1 = 25, h2 = 85, rho = 1030. Print three decimals. Explain why a common surface pressure cancels from this difference.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\Delta p=\rho g(h_2-h_1)\) with depths in metres.
中文提示（不含解答）

只需要深度差；厘米先变成米，Pa 再除以 1000 变成 kPa。

Exponential model · preserve time units · 07

Predict a sample temperature and its change

A cooling model is supplied as T(t) = Ta + (T0 − Ta) exp(−kt), with time in minutes and k in min⁻¹. Read T0, Ta, k and t with T0 > Ta, k > 0 and t ≥ 0. Calculate T(t) and the temperature drop T0 − T(t), both to three decimals in °C. Test T0 = 92, Ta = 20, k = 0.08, t = 15. Also check t = 0. State why entering 900 for t while leaving k unchanged would not represent a 15-minute experiment.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(T(t)=T_a+(T_0-T_a)e^{-kt}\), \(\Delta T=T_0-T(t)\). Use math.exp.
中文提示（不含解答）

指数中的 kt 必须无单位；输入时间单位要与给定 k 配套。

Integer arithmetic · quotient and remainder · 08

Convert total seconds into a clock-style duration

Read a nonnegative integer S representing a duration in whole seconds. Print hours, remaining whole minutes, and remaining seconds as three integer values. Hours may exceed 23; this is a duration, not a time of day. Test S = 7384, then test S = 59 and S = 3600. Do not use date/time libraries or loops. Explain why ordinary / is unsuitable when extracting whole hours.

Reference / 必要约定与公式In Python, // is floor division and % gives a remainder for these nonnegative integers. \(h=S//3600\), \(r=S\%3600\), \(m=r//60\), \(s=r\%60\).
中文提示（不含解答）

整除得到完整单位数，余数留给下一级；不需要把小时对 24 取余。

Debugging · diameters, powers and labels · 09

Repair an annular washer report

A flat washer has outer diameter D and hole diameter d in millimetres, with D > d > 0. The program must report its face area in mm² and total boundary length (outer plus inner circle) in mm. Repair all errors in this code and test D = 50, d = 20. Use three decimals.

import math
D = input("Outer diameter (mm): ")
d = float(input("Hole diameter (mm): "))
area = math.pi * (D**2 - d**2)
boundary = math.pi * (D - d)
print(f"Area: {area:.3f} mm")
print(f"Boundary: {boundary:.3f} mm²")
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=\pi(D^2-d^2)/4\), \(B=\pi(D+d)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

输入应是数值；半径是直径的一半，两个边界长度则要相加。

Length, area and volume scale factors · 10

Scale a solid model and estimate its mass

A geometrically similar solid model is built at a scale of 1:s relative to the full-size object, where s > 1. The full-size object has volume V in m³ and surface area A in m². Read positive V,A,s and model-material density rho in kg/m³. Calculate model volume in cm³, model surface area in cm², and model mass in grams. Test V = 0.8, A = 6.4, s = 20, rho = 1200. Print three decimals. Assume the model is solid with uniform density.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V_m=V/s^3\), \(A_m=A/s^2\), \(m_m=\rho V_m\) in SI; 1 m³ = 10⁶ cm³, 1 m² = 10⁴ cm², 1 kg = 1000 g.
中文提示（不含解答）

线性尺寸缩小 s 倍，面积缩小 s² 倍，体积缩小 s³ 倍；最后再转换显示单位。
