Input/output · rates and filling time · 01

Combine two filling rates stated in different units

Two pumps fill an initially empty container at constant rates. Read q1 > 0 in L/min, q2 > 0 in mL/s, and target volume V > 0 in litres. Calculate the combined rate in L/min and m³/h, and the time to collect V in minutes. Test q1 = 2.4, q2 = 35, V = 45. Print three decimals. Assume both pumps run together with no losses or outflow.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(q=q_1+60q_2/1000\), \(q_{m^3/h}=60q/1000\), \(t=V/q\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先把两个流量换成同一种单位才能相加；时间用目标体积除以合计流量。

Composite geometry · cut boundary and mass · 02

A round sheet with a centered square opening

A circular metal sheet of diameter D has a centered square opening of side a, both in cm. Read D,a, thickness t in mm and density rho in g/cm³, with D > 0, 0 < a < D/√2, t > 0 and rho > 0. Calculate remaining face area in cm², the total cut-boundary length (outer circle plus square opening) in cm, and remaining mass in grams. Test D = 18, a = 6, t = 2, rho = 2.7. Print three decimals; neglect cutting losses.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=\pi D^2/4-a^2\), \(P=\pi D+4a\), \(m=\rho A(t/10)\). The bound on a makes the whole square fit inside the disk.
中文提示（不含解答）

面积要减去方孔，但切割边界长度要把外圈和孔边相加；厚度需换成 cm。

Coordinates · linear interpolation and distance · 03

Locate a marker partway along a surveyed segment

Read endpoints P=(x1,y1), Q=(x2,y2) in metres and a fraction alpha with 0 < alpha < 1. The marker is alpha of the way from P toward Q along the straight segment. Calculate marker coordinates, full segment length and distance from P to the marker. Test P = (−5,4), Q = (15,−2), alpha = 0.35. Print three decimals and units. The endpoints must be distinct.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(M=(x_1+\alpha(x_2-x_1),\ y_1+\alpha(y_2-y_1))\), \(d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}\), \(d_{PM}=\alpha d\).
中文提示（不含解答）

分数表示从起点出发的比例；先求坐标差，再加回起点。

math.cos · milliseconds, hertz and degrees · 04

Evaluate a periodic position with mixed angle and time units

A supplied position model is x(t)=A cos(2πft+phi). Read A > 0 in cm, f > 0 in Hz, phase phi_deg in degrees, and t_ms ≥ 0 in milliseconds. Calculate the period in ms and the positions at t=0 and at the supplied time in cm. Test A = 12, f = 2.5, phi_deg = 60, t_ms = 100. Print three decimals. Convert units before calling math.cos; no calculus is needed.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\phi=\phi_{deg}\pi/180\), \(t=t_{ms}/1000\), \(T_{ms}=1000/f\), \(x=A\cos(2\pi ft+\phi)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

math.cos 的角度必须是弧度；Hz 配合以秒表示的时间。

A given engineering model · rpm and period · 05

Use a supplied gear-ratio model to find speed

Two ideal external gears mesh. Gear 1 has Z1 teeth and rotates at positive speed n1 rpm; gear 2 has Z2 teeth and pitch radius r in mm. Read positive integer Z1,Z2 and positive n1,r. Under the supplied no-slip model, calculate gear 2 speed magnitude in rpm, its rotation period in seconds, and its pitch-circle speed magnitude in m/s. Test Z1 = 24, Z2 = 60, n1 = 900, r = 40. Print three decimals. The model states that the gears rotate in opposite directions; report that direction relationship separately.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(n_2=n_1Z_1/Z_2\), \(T=60/n_2\), \(v=2\pi(r/1000)n_2/60\).
中文提示（不含解答）

转速比例使用主动轮齿数除以从动轮齿数；周期与角速度计算时都要处理一分钟的 60 秒。

Percentages · two denominators for one water mass · 06

State which mass a percentage is based on

A sample has wet mass mw and dry mass md, both in grams, with mw > md > 0. Assume drying removes only water. Read mw,md and calculate removed water mass, water percentage on a wet-mass basis, and water percentage on a dry-mass basis. Test mw = 125 and md = 100. Print two decimals with labels that identify the basis. Explain why the two percentages differ although the water mass is the same.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(m_w^{water}=m_{wet}-m_{dry}\), \(P_{wet}=100(m_{wet}-m_{dry})/m_{wet}\), \(P_{dry}=100(m_{wet}-m_{dry})/m_{dry}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

两个百分数的分子相同，但分母不同；输出标签必须写清基准。

A supplied algebraic model · weighted temperature · 07

Balance heat between materials with different heat capacities

Two ideal samples reach a common temperature without heat loss or phase change. Read masses m1,m2 in kg, specific heat capacities c1,c2 in J/(kg·°C), and initial temperatures T1 > T2 in °C, with all masses and capacities positive. Calculate final temperature and the positive heat released by sample 1 and absorbed by sample 2. Test m1 = 0.20, c1 = 900, T1 = 90, m2 = 0.30, c2 = 4200, T2 = 20. Print three decimals. Use the given constant-capacity model.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(T_f=(m_1c_1T_1+m_2c_2T_2)/(m_1c_1+m_2c_2)\), \(Q_1=m_1c_1(T_1-T_f)\), \(Q_2=m_2c_2(T_f-T_2)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

温度平均值的权重是质量乘比热容，不只是质量；两侧热量应在未舍入数据下相等。

Natural logarithm · supplied exponential formula · 08

Infer a time constant from one charging observation

A supplied model is V(t)=Vs[1−exp(−t/tau)], with volts and seconds. Read Vs > 0, observation time t1 > 0, observed voltage V1 with 0 < V1 < Vs, and prediction time t2 ≥ 0. Calculate tau and predicted V(t2). Test Vs = 12, t1 = 3, V1 = 7.2, t2 = 6. Print tau and predicted voltage to three decimals. Substitute the unrounded tau at t1 to check the observation. No differential equation is required.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\tau=-t_1/\ln(1-V_1/V_s)\), \(V(t_2)=V_s[1-e^{-t_2/\tau}]\). Use math.log and math.exp.
中文提示（不含解答）

先隔离指数项，再取自然对数；1−V1/Vs 位于 0 与 1 之间，因此其对数为负。

Debugging · float inputs and true division · 09

Repair a decimal travel-speed calculator

The program should read positive trip distance d in km and elapsed time t in minutes, then report average speed in km/h to three decimals. Repair every error and test d = 12.5, t = 18. Explain why floor division is inappropriate for this result.

d = int(input("Distance (km): "))
t = float(input("Time (min): "))
speed = d // t
print(f"Average speed: {speed:.0f} km/min")
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(v_{km/h}=60d/t_{min}\). In Python, / is true division and // is floor division.
中文提示（不含解答）

距离输入可能有小数；普通除法保留小数结果，再把每分钟换成每小时。

Supplied volume formulas · fractional powers · 10

Replace an ellipsoid by an equal-volume sphere

A solid ellipsoid has positive semi-axis lengths a,b,c in centimetres and uniform density rho > 0 in g/cm³. Read all four inputs and calculate its volume, mass, and the radius of a sphere with the same volume. Test a = 3, b = 4, c = 6, rho = 0.50. Print three decimals. The inputs are semi-axis lengths, not full end-to-end dimensions.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V=4\pi abc/3\), \(m=\rho V\), \(r_{eq}=(3V/(4\pi))^{1/3}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

体积使用三个半轴长度；等体积球的半径由立方根得到，而不是三个半轴的算术平均。
