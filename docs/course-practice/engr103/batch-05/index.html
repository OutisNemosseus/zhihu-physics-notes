Input/output · geometry and density · 01

A solid hexagonal spacer

A solid spacer is a right prism with a regular hexagonal cross section. Read hexagon side length a in mm, prism thickness h in mm, and uniform density rho in g/cm³, all positive. Calculate one hexagonal face area in mm², volume in cm³, and mass in grams. Test a = 8, h = 5, rho = 7.8. Print area, volume, and mass with three decimal places. Assume there is no hole.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}a^2\), \(V=Ah\), \(m=\rho V\) with compatible units; 1 cm³ = 1000 mm³.
中文提示（不含解答）

密度所用的 cm³ 与几何输入的 mm³ 不同；先换体积单位再乘密度。

acos · square root · a supplied formula · 02

Overlap of two circular light spots

Two equal circular spots of radius r cm have centres separated by d cm, where 0 < d < 2r. Read r and d. Use the supplied exact formula to print the area of their overlap in cm² and as a percentage of one full spot area. Test r = 5 and d = 6. Keep inverse-cosine angles in radians for the formula and print three decimal places.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A_{overlap}=2r^2\arccos(d/(2r))-\frac d2\sqrt{4r^2-d^2}\), \(P=100A_{overlap}/(\pi r^2)\). math.acos returns radians.
中文提示（不含解答）

重叠面积是一块透镜形区域；分母是一个圆的面积，不是两个圆面积的总和。

Law of sines · angle units · 03

Distances from a surveyed baseline

Two survey stations A and B are 40 m apart. A target C lies on one side of the baseline. Read baseline length c = AB, angle alpha at A, and angle beta at B. Length is in metres and angles are in degrees. Assume c > 0, alpha > 0, beta > 0, and alpha + beta < 180°. Calculate gamma at C, distances AC and BC, and triangle area. Test c = 40, alpha = 35°, beta = 65°. Print lengths to three decimals and area to two decimals.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\gamma=180^\circ-\alpha-\beta\), \(AC=c\sin\beta/\sin\gamma\), \(BC=c\sin\alpha/\sin\gamma\), \(K=\tfrac12(AC)(BC)\sin\gamma\). Convert degrees before math.sin.
中文提示（不含解答）

边长与其对角对应；AC 对应 B 角，BC 对应 A 角。

Square root · feet to SI · 04

A simple pendulum calculator

For a small-angle ideal pendulum, read length L in feet and use the supplied model to compute period T in seconds and frequency f in Hz. Assume L > 0 and g = 9.81 m/s². Test L = 3.20 ft. Then repeat with four times the length and predict both output ratios. Ignore air resistance and the size of the bob.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(T=2\pi\sqrt{L_m/g}\), \(f=1/T\); 1 ft = 0.3048 m.
中文提示（不含解答）

先把长度转为米；本题用给定的小角度模型，不需要微分方程。

Revolutions · rate and time · 05

Travel of a rotating lead screw

A lead screw advances a carriage by p millimetres per revolution. It rotates at N revolutions per minute without slip or backlash. Read p, N, and desired travel distance d in centimetres, all positive. Calculate carriage speed in mm/s, required number of revolutions, and travel time in seconds. Test p = 2.5, N = 360, d = 18.0. Cross-check the time by dividing travel distance by speed.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(v=pN/60\), \(n=10d/p\), \(t=60n/N\); 1 cm = 10 mm.
中文提示（不含解答）

每转位移与每分钟转数相乘后仍是每分钟速度；目标位移也要用毫米。

Composite solid · which length is given? · 06

A capsule-shaped container

A closed capsule consists of a straight circular cylinder of length ell and radius r, capped by one hemisphere at each end. The given ell excludes both caps. Read ell and r in cm, both positive. Print total end-to-end length in cm, enclosed volume in litres, and outside surface area in cm². Test ell = 20 and r = 4. Ignore wall thickness; the circular joins are internal.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(L=\ell+2r\), \(V=\pi r^2\ell+4\pi r^3/3\), \(S=2\pi r\ell+4\pi r^2\); 1 L = 1000 cm³.
中文提示（不含解答）

两个半球合起来是一个球；外表面积不含内部接合的圆面。

Weighted mean · temperature units · 07

Mixing two samples of the same liquid

Two samples of the same liquid have masses m1, m2 in kg and temperatures T1, T2 in °C. Read all four values, assuming both masses are positive. Under the supplied ideal model, no heat is lost, specific heat is the same for both samples, and there is no phase change. Calculate final equilibrium temperature in °C and °F. Test m1 = 1.5, T1 = 18, m2 = 0.50, T2 = 82. Print two decimals and explain why the result must lie between the initial temperatures.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(T_f=(m_1T_1+m_2T_2)/(m_1+m_2)\), \(T_F=9T_f/5+32\).
中文提示（不含解答）

质量不相等时不能直接平均两个温度；相同比热在能量平衡中约去。

Engineering geometry · allowed input range · 08

Volume of a spherical liquid cap

Liquid occupies a spherical cap of height h at the bottom of a spherical vessel of internal radius R. Read R and h in centimetres, assuming R > 0 and 0 ≤ h ≤ 2R. Calculate liquid volume in litres and percentage of the full spherical vessel volume. Test R = 15 and h = 6. Also test h = 0, h = R, and h = 2R, and use the expected empty, half-full, and full cases to check your program.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V_{cap}=\pi h^2(R-h/3)\), \(V_{sphere}=4\pi R^3/3\), \(P=100V_{cap}/V_{sphere}\); 1 L = 1000 cm³.
中文提示（不含解答）

液位高度从球底量起；h 可以大于半径，但不超过直径。

Python arithmetic · precedence and units · 09

Repair a three-dimensional distance program

The program should read a point (x, y, z) in millimetres and print its distance from the origin in centimetres. Repair every error affecting that goal. Test x = 20, y = −30, z = 60. Explain why taking a square root of the sum of squared coordinates is different from adding absolute coordinate values.

import math
x = float(input("x (mm): "))
y = input("y (mm): ")
z = float(input("z (mm): "))
distance_mm = x ** 2 + y ** 2 + z ** 2 ** 0.5
distance_cm = distance_mm * 10
print("Distance:", distance_cm, "mm")
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(d=\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}\); 1 cm = 10 mm. Use math.sqrt or parentheses around the full sum before **0.5.
中文提示（不含解答）

检查 y 的类型、开方作用范围、毫米到厘米的方向与最后的标签。

Angular speed · efficiency as a fraction · 10

Mechanical power from torque and rotation

A rotating shaft carries torque tau in N·m at N revolutions per minute. A drive of efficiency eta_percent supplies this shaft output. Read all three values, assuming tau > 0, N > 0, and 0 < eta_percent ≤ 100. Calculate shaft angular speed in rad/s, mechanical output power in W and kW, and required input power in kW. Test tau = 12, N = 1500, eta_percent = 88. Use three decimal places and state which power must be larger when efficiency is below 100%.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\omega=2\pi N/60\), \(P_{out}=\tau\omega\), \(\eta=\eta_{percent}/100\), \(P_{in}=P_{out}/\eta\); 1 kW = 1000 W.
中文提示（不含解答）

先把转/分钟转为 rad/s；效率关系的分子是输出功率。
