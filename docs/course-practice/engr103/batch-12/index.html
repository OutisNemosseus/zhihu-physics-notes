Input/output · flow and area conversions · 01

Average water speed in a circular pipe

Water fills a circular pipe and flows at volume rate Q in L/s. Read Q > 0 and internal diameter d > 0 in mm. Calculate cross-sectional area in m² and average speed in m/s. Test Q = 1.8 and d = 30. Print the area to six decimals and speed to three decimals, without rounding intermediate results. Assume uniform steady flow.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=\pi(d/1000)^2/4\), \(v=(Q/1000)/A\).
中文提示（不含解答）

流量换成 m³/s，直径换成 m 后再计算面积；速度等于每秒体积除以面积。

Composite area · outer boundary only · 02

A sign made from a rectangle and a semicircle

A sign consists of a rectangle of width w and height h, topped by a semicircle whose diameter equals w. Read positive w,h in cm. Calculate total face area and the length of the outside boundary. Test w = 16 and h = 20. Print three decimals. Do not count the shared horizontal edge between the rectangle and semicircle as an outside edge.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(r=w/2\), \(A=wh+\pi r^2/2\), \(P=w+2h+\pi r\).
中文提示（不含解答）

外边界包括底边、两条竖边和半圆弧；拼接处的直线在内部。

Coordinates · math.atan2 and degrees · 03

A displacement with a quadrant-aware direction

A robot moves from (x1,y1) to (x2,y2), in metres. Read four coordinates for distinct points. Calculate displacement components, distance, and direction measured counterclockwise from the positive x-axis in the range (−180°,180°]. Test start (4,−2) and finish (−2,6). Print three decimals. Use atan2 so the reported direction is in the correct quadrant.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\Delta x=x_2-x_1\), \(\Delta y=y_2-y_1\), \(d=\sqrt{\Delta x^2+\Delta y^2}\), \(\theta=\operatorname{degrees}(\operatorname{atan2}(\Delta y,\Delta x))\). If atan2 yields −180°, report +180°.
中文提示（不含解答）

方向由终点减起点得到；负的横向位移需要保留，不能只对商使用 atan。

Supplied torus geometry · area, volume and mass · 04

Material in a circular ring-shaped solid

A solid torus is made by revolving a circular disk around an axis in the same plane, outside the disk. Its major radius R is the distance from the axis to the disk center; its tube radius r is the disk radius. Read R,r in cm and material density rho in g/cm³, with R > r > 0 and rho > 0. Use the supplied formulas to calculate outside surface area, material volume, and mass. Test R = 6, r = 1.5, rho = 1.2. Print three decimals. The circular tube is solid, not hollow.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=4\pi^2Rr\), \(V=2\pi^2Rr^2\), \(m=\rho V\).
中文提示（不含解答）

大半径是旋转轴到管截面中心的距离；小半径是圆形截面的半径。按给定公式分别计算面积、体积与质量。

Given energy model · mixed units · 05

Convert spring compression into an ideal launch speed

An ideal spring launches a mass horizontally without losses. Read spring constant k in N/cm, compression x in mm, and mass m in kg, all positive. Calculate stored energy in joules and launch speed in m/s using the supplied energy model. Test k = 2.4, x = 50, m = 0.30. Print three decimals. No differential equation or friction model is needed.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(k_{SI}=100k\), \(x_{SI}=x/1000\), \(E=k_{SI}x_{SI}^2/2\), \(v=\sqrt{2E/m}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

每厘米的力常数要换成每米；压缩距离也要换成 m，才能得到焦耳。

Powers · inverse exponential calculation · 06

Predict a concentration from a supplied half-life model

A supplied model is c(t) = c0 × 2^(−t/H), with concentration in mg/L and time in hours. Read c0 > 0, half-life H > 0, time t ≥ 0 and target concentration cstar with 0 < cstar < c0. Calculate c(t) and the time to reach cstar. Test c0 = 48, H = 3, t = 4.5, cstar = 6. Print three decimals. Use unrounded values internally.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(c(t)=c_0\,2^{-t/H}\), \(t_*=H\ln(c_0/c_*)/\ln2\). Use math.log for the natural logarithm.
中文提示（不含解答）

预测时用实数指数；反算时间时，对数的输入是浓度比值。

Affine conversion · absolute values versus differences · 07

Convert a temperature and its change separately

Read initial and final temperatures F1,F2 in degrees Fahrenheit, both above −459.67 °F. Convert each to degrees Celsius and kelvin. Also calculate the signed change in °F and in °C. Test F1 = 14 and F2 = 77. Print two decimals with units. Explain why the −32 offset is applied to each temperature but not to their difference.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(C=(F-32)5/9\), \(K=C+273.15\), \(\Delta F=F_2-F_1\), \(\Delta C=(5/9)\Delta F\).
中文提示（不含解答）

温差换算只需要比例因子；绝对温度换算还需要平移。

Three-dimensional distance · unit consistency · 08

A straight brace across a rectangular crate

A rectangular crate has internal length a, width b and height c in centimetres. A straight brace joins two opposite corners through the interior. Read all three positive dimensions. Calculate the floor diagonal, brace length in cm, and brace length in metres. Test a = 24, b = 18, c = 40. Print three decimals. The brace is an ideal line segment with negligible thickness.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(d_{floor}=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\), \(d_{brace}=\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}\), \(d_m=d_{brace}/100\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先计算底面直角三角形，再把高度与底面对角线组成另一个直角三角形。

Debugging · millimetres and litres · 09

Repair a rainwater collection calculation

The program should read roof area A in m² and rainfall depth h in mm, both positive, then report collected water in litres to one decimal. Assume 100% collection and no losses. Repair the program and test A = 72 and h = 12.5. Explain each conversion and why input values must be numeric.

A = input("Roof area (m²): ")
h = input("Rainfall (mm): ")
volume = A * h * 1000
print(f"Collected: {volume:.1f} m³")
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V_{m^3}=A(h/1000)\), \(V_L=1000V_{m^3}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

毫米要先换成米得到 m³，再换成升；检查这两个换算因子是否相消。

Supplied formulas · curved and total area · 10

A spherical cap with a flat circular base

A spherical cap is cut from a sphere of radius R. The cap height is h, measured perpendicular to its flat circular base, with 0 < h < R. Read R,h in cm and calculate the base radius, cap volume, and total area including the flat base. Test R = 10 and h = 2. Print three decimals. Use the supplied formulas; no calculus is required.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(a=\sqrt{2Rh-h^2}\), \(V=\pi h^2(R-h/3)\), \(A_{curved}=2\pi Rh\), \(A_{total}=A_{curved}+\pi a^2\).
中文提示（不含解答）

曲面面积不包括底面的圆；总表面积需要另加底面积。
