Input/output · cubed units · 01

Material in a spherical shell

A hollow spherical enclosure has outer radius R and inner radius r, both in millimetres. Read R and r, assuming R > r > 0. Calculate material volume in mm³ and cm³, and mass in grams if the uniform density is rho g/cm³. Test R = 35, r = 32, rho = 2.70. Print all results with three decimal places and units. Ignore openings and fittings.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V=\frac{4\pi}{3}(R^3-r^3)\), \(m=\rho V\) with compatible volume units; 1 cm = 10 mm.
中文提示（不含解答）

密度使用 cm³；立方单位的转换因子需要立方。

Angles · area and boundary · 02

A circular sector cut from a sheet

Read a sector radius r in cm and its central angle theta in degrees, with r > 0 and 0 < theta < 360. Print its area in cm² and m², curved arc length in cm, and total boundary length in cm. Test r = 18 and theta = 125. Include the two straight radial sides in the boundary.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\theta_r=\pi\theta/180\), \(A=r^2\theta_r/2\), \(s=r\theta_r\), \(P=2r+s\); 1 m² = 10000 cm².
中文提示（不含解答）

圆弧长度与完整边界不同；给定的公式用弧度。

Coordinates · math.atan2 · 03

Distance and navigation bearing

A surveyor travels from (x1, y1) = (14, −8) m to (x2, y2) = (−10, 24) m. Positive x means east and positive y means north. Write a program that reads both points and prints distance in metres and bearing in degrees, measured clockwise from north in [0°, 360°). Assume the two points differ. Print three decimal places.

Use atan2 rather than atan of a ratio, and explain why the order of its arguments differs from the usual angle measured counterclockwise from the x-axis.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(d=\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2}\); bearing = (math.degrees(math.atan2(dx, dy)) + 360) % 360.
中文提示（不含解答）

本题方位角的起点是北方；先计算两个坐标差。

Engineering units · a supplied model · 04

Pressure below two liquid layers

An open tank contains an upper oil layer of thickness ho cm over a water layer. A sensor lies hw cm below the oil–water interface. Read ho, hw, oil density rho_o, and water density rho_w, with thicknesses positive and densities in kg/m³. Calculate sensor gauge pressure and absolute pressure in kPa. Use atmospheric pressure 101.3 kPa and g = 9.81 m/s². Test ho = 28, hw = 65, rho_o = 850, rho_w = 998.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(p_g=g(\rho_o h_o+\rho_w h_w)\) with heights in metres; \(p_{abs}=p_g+p_{atm}\); 1 kPa = 1000 Pa.
中文提示（不含解答）

各层贡献相加；表压不包含大气压，绝对压强包含。

Cosine law · inverse cosine · 05

Determine an included angle

A triangle has side lengths a, b, and c in cm; gamma is the angle opposite c and between a and b. Read the three sides and calculate gamma in degrees and triangle area in cm². Assume a valid nondegenerate triangle. Test a = 9, b = 12, c = 15. Explain why acos returns radians and why the argument must lie in [−1, 1].

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\cos\gamma=(a^2+b^2-c^2)/(2ab)\), \(A=ab\sin\gamma/2\); math.acos returns radians.
中文提示（不含解答）

先由余弦定理求角，再把角转为所需输出单位；求面积时仍用弧度角。

Diameter · mixed length units · 06

A wire resistance calculator

A uniform round wire has length ell in metres, diameter d in millimetres, and resistivity rho in Ω·m. Read all three positive values. Calculate cross-sectional area in m² and electrical resistance in Ω. Test ell = 12, d = 0.80, rho = 1.68e−8. Display area in scientific notation and resistance with six decimal places. Then double the diameter without changing other inputs and predict the resistance ratio.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=\pi(d_m/2)^2\), \(R=\rho\ell/A\), \(d_m=d/1000\).
中文提示（不含解答）

输入是直径，公式需要半径；横截面积在换算后计算。

Composite geometry · external boundary · 07

A panel with a semicircular end

A flat panel consists of a rectangle of length L and width W, with one semicircle of diameter W attached along one short side. Their shared diameter is internal and must not be counted in the outer boundary. Read L and W in cm, both positive. Print total area in cm² and outer perimeter in cm. Test L = 22 and W = 14. Ignore thickness.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(r=W/2\), \(A=LW+\pi r^2/2\), \(P=2L+W+\pi r\).
中文提示（不含解答）

矩形有一条短边被半圆接缝替代；总面积不重叠。

Logarithms · model inversion · 08

Time to reach a cooling target

A model predicts T(t) = Ta + (T0 − Ta) exp(−kt), where t is in minutes and k is in min⁻¹. Write a program reading Ta, T0, Ttarget, and k. Assume k > 0 and T0 > Ttarget > Ta. Calculate time in minutes and seconds, then substitute that time back into the model to check the target. Test Ta = 22, T0 = 82, Ttarget = 37, k = 0.060. Print time to two decimal places.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(t=-\ln[(T_{target}-T_a)/(T_0-T_a)]/k\). Use math.exp and math.log.
中文提示（不含解答）

math.log 是自然对数；代回检查时使用未提前取整的时间。

Debugging · dimensional tracing · 09

Repair a cylindrical tank calculation

The following program should read tank diameter D in cm and height h in cm, then print capacity in litres. Identify and fix every error affecting that goal. Assume D > 0 and h > 0; test D = 40 and h = 75.

import math
D = float(input("Diameter (cm): "))
h = input("Height (cm): ")
volume = math.pi * D ** 2 * h
litres = volume / 100
print("Capacity:", litres, "cm2")
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V=\pi(D/2)^2h\); 1 L = 1000 cm³.
中文提示（不含解答）

检查输入类型、直径与半径、立方单位换算和输出标签。

Signed error · percentage formatting · 10

Compare an estimate with a reference

Read a positive reference distance d_ref and a measured estimate d_est, both in metres. Print signed error in cm, absolute error in cm, and absolute relative error as a percentage. Test d_ref = 2.400 and d_est = 2.368. Use three decimal places for cm and two for percent. Repeat with d_est = 2.432 and explain which outputs change sign and which remain the same.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(e=d_{est}-d_{ref}\), \(e_{abs}=|e|\), \(e_{rel,\%}=100|e|/d_{ref}\); 1 m = 100 cm.
中文提示（不含解答）

相对误差的分母是参考值；百分号前的数字需要乘 100。
