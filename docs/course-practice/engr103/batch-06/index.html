Input/output · area and volume · 01

Capacity of a trapezoidal channel

An open channel has a trapezoidal inside cross section: bottom width b, top width a, and vertical depth h, all in centimetres. Its straight length L is in metres. Read the four positive values, with a ≥ b. Calculate cross-sectional area in cm² and full capacity in litres. Ignore wall thickness. Test a = 50, b = 30, h = 20, L = 2.5. Print both results to two decimal places.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=(a+b)h/2\), \(V=A L_{cm}\); 1 m = 100 cm, 1 L = 1000 cm³.
中文提示（不含解答）

截面积用垂直深度，不用斜边长度；长度 L 要先转换为厘米。

Geometry · semiaxes and density · 02

An elliptical plate with a circular opening

A thin uniform plate has an elliptical outer edge and a central circular opening. Read ellipse semiaxes a and b, hole radius r, and thickness t in millimetres, plus material density rho in g/cm³. Assume a ≥ b > r > 0, t > 0, and rho > 0. Calculate remaining face area, material volume, and mass. Test a = 30, b = 20, r = 5, t = 2, rho = 2.70. Print area in mm², volume in cm³, and mass in grams, each to three decimals.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=\pi ab-\pi r^2\), \(V=At\), \(m=\rho V\) in compatible units; 1000 mm³ = 1 cm³.
中文提示（不含解答）

a 和 b 是半轴，不是整个长轴和短轴；挖去的部分在整个厚度上贯穿。

Square root · inverse tangent · 03

Length and angle of a support cable

A straight support cable runs from the top of a vertical pole of height h to a ground anchor at horizontal distance d from the pole base. Read h and d in metres, both positive. Calculate cable length and the acute angle the cable makes with the horizontal ground. Test h = 4.5 and d = 6.0. Report length to three decimals and angle in degrees to two decimals. Do not use an angle measured from the vertical.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\ell=\sqrt{h^2+d^2}\), \(\theta=\arctan(h/d)\); use math.sqrt, math.atan, and math.degrees.
中文提示（不含解答）

直角三角形中，地面夹角的对边是高度 h；反正切函数输出弧度。

Engineering formula · small unit changes · 04

Thermal expansion of a straight rod

Under a supplied constant-coefficient model, a rod with initial length L0 at temperature T0 expands when heated to T1. Read L0 in metres, T0 and T1 in °C, and alpha_micro in units of 10⁻⁶ per °C. Assume L0 > 0, alpha_micro > 0 and T1 ≥ T0. Print the length increase in millimetres and final length in metres. Test L0 = 1.20, T0 = 15, T1 = 75, alpha_micro = 23. Use three decimals for the increase and six for final length.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\alpha=\alpha_{micro}10^{-6}\), \(\Delta L=\alpha L_0(T_1-T_0)\), \(L_1=L_0+\Delta L\); 1 m = 1000 mm.
中文提示（不含解答）

微量系数要乘 10 的负六次方；温差用摄氏度差即可，不需要加 273.15。

Circle area · rate conversion · 05

Water flow through a round pipe

Water moves at uniform speed v through a full round pipe of inside diameter d. Read d in millimetres and v in m/s, both positive. Calculate flow rate in m³/s and litres/minute, then the time in seconds to collect a read-in positive target volume V in litres. Test d = 20, v = 1.5, V = 12. Print flow in m³/s to six decimal places, litres/minute to three, and time to two. Ignore losses and variation across the pipe.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=\pi(d_m/2)^2\), \(Q=Av\), \(t=V_{m^3}/Q\); 1 m³ = 1000 L, 1 min = 60 s.
中文提示（不含解答）

面积计算先把直径换成米并除以 2；流量乘时间得到体积。

math.tan · integer input · 06

A regular polygon from its side length

Read the number of sides n of a regular polygon as an integer and side length s in centimetres as a float. Assume n ≥ 3 and s > 0; no input-validation code is required. Use the supplied formula to calculate perimeter and area. Test an octagon with n = 8, s = 4. Then test n = 4, s = 4 and compare its area with the familiar square formula. Print perimeter and area to three decimals.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(P=ns\), \(A=ns^2/[4\tan(\pi/n)]\). Python math.tan expects radians.
中文提示（不含解答）

公式中的 π/n 是弧度；这里 n 通过 int(input(...)) 读取，不要将它当作角度。

Inverse formula · square root and powers · 07

Recover sphere dimensions from surface area

Read the outside surface area S of a solid sphere in cm², assuming S > 0. Calculate its radius in cm, diameter in mm, and volume in cm³. Test S = 314.1592653589793 cm². Print each result to three decimal places. Recompute surface area from your calculated radius and explain why rounding the radius early can spoil this check.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(S=4\pi r^2\), \(r=\sqrt{S/(4\pi)}\), \(V=4\pi r^3/3\); 1 cm = 10 mm.
中文提示（不含解答）

先解出半径再计算其他量；内部计算保留浮点精度，只在最终显示时格式化。

Exponentials · rate units · 08

A supplied half-life model

A harmless simulated tracer follows the mathematical model below. Read initial amount M0 in grams, half-life H in hours, and elapsed time t in minutes. Assume M0 > 0, H > 0, t ≥ 0. Print remaining amount and percentage of the initial amount, both to three decimals. Test M0 = 80, H = 2.5, t = 225. Also test t = 0 and t = 60H and explain the two checks. This is a programming model, not a laboratory procedure.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(M=M_0(1/2)^{t_{hours}/H}\), \(P=100M/M_0\); \(t_{hours}=t/60\).
中文提示（不含解答）

指数必须无量纲；先让经过时间与半衰期使用相同单位。

Type conversion · parentheses · geometry · 09

Repair a cone surface-area program

A closed right circular cone has radius r and vertical height h, both entered in centimetres. The goal is to print total outside surface area in cm², including the base. Repair the program and test r = 3, h = 4. Explain what the slant length represents.

import math
r = input("radius (cm): ")
h = float(input("height (cm): "))
l = math.sqrt(r ** 2) + h ** 2
area = math.pi * r * l
print(f"Total area: {area:.2f} cm")
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\ell=\sqrt{r^2+h^2}\), \(A_{total}=\pi r\ell+\pi r^2\).
中文提示（不含解答）

检查输入类型、根号作用范围、底面是否遗漏和面积单位标签。

Percentages · rounding whole packages · 10

Order material with a stated allowance

A rectangular panel has length L and width W in metres. Material is sold in packages covering exactly c square metres each. Read L, W, c and a waste allowance p in percent, assuming L,W,c > 0 and p ≥ 0. Calculate bare area, target area including allowance, and the smallest whole number of packages whose combined coverage is at least the target. Print the spare coverage beyond that target. Test L = 4.2, W = 3.5, c = 2.0, p = 8. No prices are involved. Explain why ordinary rounding of the package count may under-order.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A=LW\), \(A_t=A(1+p/100)\), \(N=\lceil A_t/c\rceil\), \(A_{spare}=Nc-A_t\). Python math.ceil rounds upward to an integer.
中文提示（不含解答）

先给面积增加百分比，再将包数向上取整；余量与含损耗的目标面积比较。
