HW 1 · period and phase · 01

Zero crossings and a sine wave

Let \(v(t)=8\sin(250\pi t-\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\), with t in seconds.

  1. Find peak amplitude, angular frequency, ordinary frequency, and period in ms.
  2. Rewrite it using a positive-amplitude cosine and give its cosine-reference phase in degrees.
  3. Find the first upward zero crossing at or after t = 0, and the first positive maximum.
  4. Plot two periods beginning at t = 0 with time in ms; label both events.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\omega=2\pi f\), \(T=1/f\); \(\sin\theta=\cos(\theta-\pi/2)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

向上过零需要同时满足电压为零和斜率为正。

HW 1 · canonical phasors · 02

Negative amplitude and a time advance

Let \(x(t)=-3\cos(600t+5\pi/6)\;\mathrm A\). Use positive peak amplitudes and cosine-reference phases in (−180°, 180°].

  1. Write the canonical phasor X.
  2. For y(t) = x(t + 0.0015), determine Y and a time-domain cosine expression.
  3. State whether y is advanced or delayed and compute the phase change in radians and degrees.
  4. Check your original and rewritten x(t) at t = 0.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(-\cos\theta=\cos(\theta+\pi)\); advancing by \(t_a\) adds phase \(\omega t_a\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先把负幅值吸收到相位中，再处理时间平移。

HW 1 · complex forms · 03

Conjugates and phasor arithmetic

Let \(z_1=-6-8j\) and \(z_2=4\angle(-30^\circ)\). For each item, state rectangular and polar form, with principal phase in (−180°, 180°].

  1. \(z_1\) and its complex conjugate \(\overline{z_1}\)
  2. \(z_1\overline{z_1}\)
  3. \(3z_2-z_1\)
  4. \(z_1/z_2\)

Use quadrant-aware angle calculation. Keep at least four significant digits internally, and round only final reported values.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\overline{a+jb}=a-jb\), \(z\overline z=|z|^2\), \(j^2=-1\).
中文提示（不含解答）

共轭改变虚部符号；复数相除可用极坐标交叉核验。

HW 1 · add and independently check · 04

Two quadrature components

Combine \(v(t)=5\cos(800t+\pi/2)+12\cos(800t)\;\mathrm V\) into one positive-amplitude cosine.

  1. Show both phasors and their rectangular sum.
  2. Find the magnitude and principal phase, then write the single time-domain cosine.
  3. Verify both representations at t = 0 and t = π/1600 s.
  4. Draw the two component vectors and their resultant on equal-scale real/imaginary voltage axes.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A\angle\phi=A(\cos\phi+j\sin\phi)\); peak-amplitude, cosine-reference phasors.
中文提示（不含解答）

相差 90° 的两个相量可以用几何结果校对复数运算。

HW 1 · phasor subtraction · 05

Recover a missing sinusoid

Two voltages have angular frequency \(\omega=1200\;\mathrm{rad/s}\). Their sum is \(v_T(t)=9\cos(1200t)\;\mathrm V\), and one component is \(v_2(t)=4\cos(1200t+\pi/2)\;\mathrm V\).

  1. Find the missing component v1(t), given v1 + v2 = vT.
  2. Give V1 in rectangular and polar form and draw V1, V2, and VT as a tip-to-tail sum.
  3. Check v1(0) + v2(0), then calculate the maximum magnitude of v1(t). Explain why that amplitude may exceed the amplitude of the sum.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V_1=V_T-V_2\); all phasors use peak cosine amplitudes.
中文提示（不含解答）

不能直接把两个正弦的幅值相减；先做复平面向量减法。

HW 2 · impedances and source convention · 06

A capacitive series RLC circuit

The circuit has \(v_s(t)=24\cos(500t-\pi/9)\;\mathrm V\), R = 18 Ω, L = 40 mH, and C = 25 μF. Define i(t) clockwise, leaving the positive source terminal.

  1. Redraw the source and components in the phasor domain.
  2. Calculate total impedance and current phasor in rectangular and polar form.
  3. Write steady-state i(t), and state whether current leads or lags the source.
  4. Calculate all component voltage phasors and verify KVL. If simulating, convert the cosine to the simulator’s sine-source convention and compare at two phases after transients settle.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\ Z_L=j\omega L,\ Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\); \(I=V_s/(Z_R+Z_L+Z_C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

容性总阻抗的虚部为负；不要由元件名称直接猜总相位。

HW 2 · equivalent impedance · 07

A series resistor with a parallel RC load

Find the input impedance between A and B at \(\omega=1000\;\mathrm{rad/s}\). A 10 Ω resistor R₁ is in series with a parallel combination of R₂ = 30 Ω and C = 20 μF, as shown.

  1. Find ZC, then reduce the parallel pair before adding R₁.
  2. Give the input impedance in rectangular and polar form.
  3. State whether the network is net capacitive or inductive at this frequency.
  4. Check your parallel impedance by adding branch admittances independently.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_a\parallel Z_b=Z_aZ_b/(Z_a+Z_b)\), \(Y_{parallel}=1/Z_a+1/Z_b\).
中文提示（不含解答）

R₁ 不在并联支路内；等效并联的两端要对应同一对节点。

HW 2 · node voltage and KCL · 08

A current source and inductive branches

An ideal source \(i_s(t)=0.008\cos(1000t+2\pi/9)\;\mathrm A\) injects current from ground into node A. R = 2.0 kΩ and L = 1.0 H each connect from A to ground. Define vA positive at A, with resistor and inductor currents directed downward.

  1. Draw the phasor-domain circuit and write KCL at A.
  2. Find VA in rectangular and polar form and write vA(t).
  3. Find both branch-current phasors and verify their sum equals Is. Keep units consistent between amperes and milliamperes.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(I_s=(1/R+1/(j\omega L))V_A\), \(I_R=V_A/R\), \(I_L=V_A/(j\omega L)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

电感导纳为 1/(jωL)，不是 jωL；留意其虚部符号。

HW 2 · output polarity and divider · 09

Measure across the resistor

A source \(v_s(t)=10\cos(1000t-\pi/12)\;\mathrm V\) drives a series R = 15 Ω and L = 20 mH. Define clockwise current i(t). The output vR is measured across R, with positive reference on the source side and negative reference on the inductor side, as drawn.

  1. Find the total impedance, current phasor, and resistor-voltage phasor.
  2. Write vR(t) and state its phase difference relative to the source.
  3. Find the inductor-voltage phasor and verify VR + VL = Vs. Explain why replacing the divider numerator with ZL would answer a different question.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V_R=V_sR/(R+j\omega L)\), \(V_L=j\omega LI\).
中文提示（不含解答）

输出是电阻两端的差分电压，不能把上方任意节点对地电压当作 vR。

HW 2 · synthesis with a capacitor load · 10

Loaded output and two-point verification

The source is \(v_s(t)=10\cos(500t+\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\). It feeds R₁ = 50 Ω in series. At node A, R₂ = 100 Ω and C = 20 μF connect in parallel to ground. Define vo = vA relative to ground and source current through R₁ toward A.

  1. Reduce the load, then find input impedance and source current phasor.
  2. Find Vo in rectangular and polar form and write vo(t).
  3. Compute resistor and capacitor branch currents and verify node KCL.
  4. Predict vo at t = 0 and t = π/1000 s. For optional transient simulation, compare at matching phases in a late cycle after startup has settled, rather than comparing t = 0 startup to a steady-state formula.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=R_2\parallel Z_C\), \(V_o=V_sZ_p/(R_1+Z_p)\), \(Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

负载阻抗必须包括 R₂ 与 C；时域校验的时间单位为秒。
