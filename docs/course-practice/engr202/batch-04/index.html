HW 1 · frequency and phase from a plot · 01

Reconstruct a waveform from peak times

A zero-offset sinusoidal voltage has maximum +6 V and minimum −6 V. Consecutive positive maxima occur at t = 1.0 ms and t = 9.0 ms. Assume this pattern continues for all real t.

  1. Find amplitude, period, frequency, and angular frequency.
  2. Write one positive-amplitude cosine expression with t in seconds and principal phase in (−180°, 180°].
  3. Find v(0), the first downward zero crossing at or after zero, and the first negative minimum at or after t = 0.
  4. Sketch two periods beginning at zero and label the measured peaks and calculated events.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(v(t)=A\cos(\omega t+\phi)\), \(T=1/f\), \(\omega=2\pi f\); positive maxima occur at phase \(2\pi k\).
中文提示（不含解答）

相邻同类峰值间隔是一整个周期；把毫秒换为秒后再写 ωt。

HW 1 · sine/cosine and relative phase · 02

Compare phase after a time delay

Let \(x(t)=6\cos(500t+\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\) and \(y(t)=3\sin[500(t-0.002)+\pi/3]\;\mathrm V\), with t in seconds.

  1. Write both cosine-reference peak phasors using principal phases in (−180°, 180°].
  2. Calculate the principal phase difference φy − φx in degrees. Which waveform leads in phase?
  3. Give the positive time separation smaller than half a period corresponding to that phase difference. Compare the equal-phase features of the two waves, ignoring their different amplitudes.
  4. Check y(0) from both its original and cosine forms.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\sin\theta=\cos(\theta-\pi/2)\), \(\Delta t=|\Delta\phi|/\omega\) with phase in radians.
中文提示（不含解答）

y 内部的 0.002 是秒；先展开时间平移，再转换正弦基准。

HW 1 · complex expression audit · 03

Reciprocals before conversion to polar form

Evaluate \(z=(3-4j)/(1+2j)+1/(-j)\).

  1. Show the rectangular value of each term before adding.
  2. Give z in rectangular and polar form.
  3. Find 1/z and z² in both forms.
  4. Verify z(1/z) = 1, and identify the sign error caused by incorrectly using 1/j = j. Use principal phases in (−180°, 180°].
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(j^2=-1\), \(1/(a+jb)=(a-jb)/(a^2+b^2)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

复数倒数需要有理化分母；分数算完再相加。

HW 1 · phasor addition and plotting · 04

Three terms with different sign conventions

Combine \(i(t)=5\cos(900t)-4\sin(900t)-2\cos(900t+\pi)\;\mathrm A\) into one positive-amplitude cosine.

  1. Give the three individual cosine-reference phasors and sum them in rectangular form.
  2. Find the resultant magnitude and principal phase and write i(t).
  3. Verify your result at t = 0 and t = π/1800 s.
  4. Draw a tip-to-tail diagram with equal scales, real/imaginary current axes, and all vector directions marked. Explain why the last term cannot be treated as a phasor of magnitude −2.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(A\cos(\omega t+\phi)\leftrightarrow A\angle\phi\), with \(A\ge0\); \(\Re[(a+jb)e^{j\omega t}]=a\cos\omega t-b\sin\omega t\).
中文提示（不含解答）

负系数要由相位表示；括号中的 π 也必须计入。

HW 1 · rectangular components from data · 05

Recover a phasor from quarter-period samples

A zero-offset sinusoidal voltage has frequency 250 Hz. Its measured samples are v(0) = −4 V and v(1.00 ms) = −3 V. Measurements are ideal and exact. Use a positive-amplitude cosine representation.

  1. Find the period and explain why these samples determine the real and imaginary phasor components.
  2. Recover V in rectangular and polar form, then write v(t) with t in seconds.
  3. Predict v(2.00 ms) and v(3.00 ms).
  4. Draw one full cycle with the four sample locations and an equal-scale phasor plot. Label amplitude, time units, and voltage units.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V=a+jb\Rightarrow v(t)=a\cos\omega t-b\sin\omega t\); \(\omega=2\pi f\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先确认 1 ms 在周期中所占比例；取相量实部回到时域时，虚部乘的是负正弦。

HW 2 · impedance and component voltages · 06

A series RLC circuit with cancelling reactances

The source is \(v_s(t)=10\sin(500t+\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\). In series are R = 20 Ω, L = 80 mH, and C = 50 μF. Current i(t) is clockwise, leaving the positive source terminal.

  1. Draw the cosine-reference phasor-domain circuit and calculate each impedance and the total.
  2. Find I and write steady-state i(t) as a cosine.
  3. Calculate VR, VL, and VC with passive voltage references and check their sum against Vs.
  4. Can the peak voltage of an individual reactive component exceed the source peak in this circuit? Justify using the computed phasors rather than adding magnitudes.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\ Z_L=j\omega L,\ Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\), \(I=V_s/Z_{total}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

源给的是正弦；相量必须先统一为余弦基准。

HW 2 · topology and equivalent impedance · 07

A capacitor in series with a parallel RL pair

At \(\omega=1000\;\mathrm{rad/s}\), the input path from A passes through C = 25 μF, then reaches two parallel branches to B: R = 40 Ω and L = 40 mH. Find the impedance between A and B.

  1. Calculate the capacitor and inductor impedances.
  2. Reduce the parallel pair, then add the series capacitor. Give ZAB in rectangular and polar form.
  3. Check the parallel pair by adding admittances. State whether the input network is net capacitive or inductive.
  4. Explain why adding all three component impedances directly would use the wrong topology.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_a\parallel Z_b=Z_aZ_b/(Z_a+Z_b)\); \(Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先认清 R 和 L 的公共两端；输入电容不属于并联部分。

HW 2 · node voltage and KCL · 08

A source feeding resistive and series-RC branches

The current source \(i_s(t)=0.010\cos(1000t-\pi/4)\;\mathrm A\) points from ground upward into node A. Branch 1 from A to ground contains R₁ = 400 Ω in series with C = 2.5 μF. Branch 2 contains R₂ = 800 Ω. Define vA positive at A and both passive branch currents downward.

  1. Draw the phasor circuit and calculate the total admittance.
  2. Find VA in rectangular and polar form and write vA(t).
  3. Find both branch current phasors and verify KCL.
  4. Find the capacitor-voltage phasor, positive at its upper plate, and check KVL within the series RC branch.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Y=1/(R_1+Z_C)+1/R_2\), \(V_A=I_s/Y\), \(V_C=I_1Z_C\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先对 R₁ 与 C 的串联阻抗求倒数，再与另一支路导纳相加。

HW 2 · divider numerator and output definition · 09

Voltage across two series reactive elements

The source \(v_s(t)=5\cos(1000t+\pi/18)\;\mathrm V\) drives the series path R = 10 Ω, L = 30 mH, C = 100 μF. Node A is between R and L. The bottom plate of C is grounded. Define vo = vA relative to ground, so the output spans both L and C. Current is clockwise.

  1. Find the total impedance and current phasor.
  2. Use an impedance divider to find Vo and write vo(t).
  3. Find VL and VC separately and verify Vo = VL + VC.
  4. Determine the phase of Vo relative to Vs. Explain how measuring only the capacitor would change the divider numerator.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V_o=V_s(Z_L+Z_C)/(R+Z_L+Z_C)\), \(V_L=IZ_L\), \(V_C=IZ_C\).
中文提示（不含解答）

输出跨越 L 与 C 整个串联组合；分压分子需要与测量端点一致。

HW 2 · full network and two-point check · 10

A loaded output larger than the source peak

The source \(v_s(t)=8\cos(500t-\pi/3)\;\mathrm V\) feeds a series capacitor C = 100 μF. At node A, R = 40 Ω and L = 80 mH connect in parallel to ground. Define output vo from A to ground and source current from C into A.

  1. Reduce the load, then find the input impedance and source current phasor.
  2. Find Vo in rectangular and polar form and write vo(t).
  3. Calculate both load currents and check KCL. Also calculate the series-capacitor voltage, positive on its source side, and verify KVL.
  4. Predict vo at t = 0 and t = π/1000 s. For optional LTspice verification, give the source frequency and equivalent sine-source phase, and compare those same two phases in a late cycle after transients settle.
  5. Explain how the output peak can exceed the source peak without violating the phasor KVL relation.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=R\parallel j\omega L\), \(I_s=V_s/(Z_C+Z_p)\), \(V_o=I_sZ_p\), \(V_C=I_sZ_C\).
中文提示（不含解答）

KVL 对复数电压求和，不是对幅值求和；检查仿真电流的参考方向。
