1. AC power and power-factor correction

A 120 V RMS, 60 Hz ideal sinusoidal source supplies a load with impedance Z = 12 + j9 Ω.

中文推导

(a) |Z| = √(12² + 9²) = 15 Ω，PF = R/|Z| = 0.800。正电抗表示感性负载，电流滞后。

(b) IRMS = 120/15 = 8 A，所以 P = IRMS²R = 8² × 12 = 768 W。

(c) QL = 8² × 9 = 576 var。并联电容两端同为 120 V RMS，QC = −2πfCVRMS²。令 QC + QL = 0，得 C = 576/[2π × 60 × 120²] ≈ 106 μF。

2. RMS of a periodic current

The current below repeats every 4 ms. Calculate its RMS value over one full period.

Time interval (ms)Current (A)
0 ≤ t < 10
1 ≤ t < 3+6
3 ≤ t < 4−3
中文推导

每段电流先平方，再按该段持续时间加权。IRMS = √[(0² × 1 + 6² × 2 + (−3)² × 1)/4] = √20.25 = 4.50 A。

3. Parallel R–L load

A source with phasor voltage 120∠0° V RMS drives a 30 Ω resistor in parallel with an ideal inductor whose reactance at the source frequency is 40 Ω. Use the voltage as the phase reference.

中文推导

(a) IR = 120/30 = 4∠0° A。

(b) IL = 120/(j40) = −j3 = 3∠−90° A。

(c) 用直角坐标相加，I = 4 − j3 A，幅值为 5 A，相角为 arctan(−3/4) ≈ −36.9°。
