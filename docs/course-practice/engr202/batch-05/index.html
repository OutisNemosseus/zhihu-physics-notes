HW 1 · interpret angular-frequency units · 01

A time axis is labeled in milliseconds

A waveform is written as \(v(t_{ms})=9\cos(0.8t_{ms}-\pi/3)\;\mathrm V\), where \(t_{ms}\) is the numerical time in milliseconds and the phase is in radians.

  1. Rewrite v using t in seconds. State angular frequency in rad/s, frequency in Hz, and period in ms.
  2. Give the cosine-reference peak phasor and calculate v(0).
  3. Find the first positive maximum and first upward zero crossing at or after t = 0.
  4. Sketch one period with a time axis in ms. Explain the frequency error if someone treats 0.8 as rad/s while leaving the numerical phase expression unchanged.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(t_{ms}=1000t\), \(\omega=2\pi f\), \(T=2\pi/\omega\).
中文提示（不含解答）

毫秒变量与秒变量相差 1000 倍；角频率必须配合横轴单位。

HW 1 · phase wrapping and a delay · 02

Which expressions are the same waveform?

At \(\omega=700\;\mathrm{rad/s}\), consider these three expressions, each in volts: \(x_1(t)=8\cos(700t+5\pi/2)\), \(x_2(t)=-8\sin(700t)\), and \(x_3(t)=8\sin(700t)\).

  1. Write each as a positive-amplitude cosine with principal phase in (−180°, 180°]. Identify all equivalent pairs.
  2. Let T be the period and define y(t) = x1(t − T/2). Find Y and state which of x1, x2, x3 equals y for every t.
  3. Check the three original expressions at t = 0 and t = π/1400 s. Explain why checking only t = 0 would not establish equivalence.
  4. Draw the three phasors with labels on equal-scale axes.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\sin\theta=\cos(\theta-\pi/2)\), \(-\sin\theta=\cos(\theta+\pi/2)\), \(T=2\pi/\omega\).
中文提示（不含解答）

相位可以加减整圈；相差半圈的波形在某些取样点也可能都为零。

HW 1 · sum, difference and vector scale · 03

Add opposing phasors without losing direction

Let \(A=6\angle140^\circ\;\mathrm V\) and \(B=10\angle(-40^\circ)\;\mathrm V\). Both describe 500 rad/s cosine-reference signals.

  1. Give A and B in rectangular form and show their angular separation.
  2. Find S = A + B and D = A − B in rectangular and polar form, keeping positive magnitudes.
  3. Write the time-domain waveforms represented by S and D.
  4. Draw both operations tip-to-tail with a common voltage scale. Explain why reporting S as a negative magnitude at 140° is not the requested canonical form.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(M\angle\phi=M(\cos\phi+j\sin\phi)\); use principal phases in (−180°, 180°].
中文提示（不含解答）

两个相量方向相反；做差时还需要把被减向量翻转。

HW 1 · compound complex arithmetic · 04

Multiply, divide, and normalize the phase

Evaluate \(z=\dfrac{(-3+3j)(-2+2j)}{1-j\sqrt3}\).

  1. Compute the numerator and denominator in rectangular and polar form.
  2. Obtain z by rectangular arithmetic and independently by magnitude/phase arithmetic.
  3. Give z in rectangular and principal polar form, then find its conjugate and magnitude squared.
  4. Verify z(1 − j√3) reproduces the numerator. Show explicitly where phase wrapping is needed when multiplying the numerator phasors.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(j^2=-1\), \(z\overline z=|z|^2\), \((M_1\angle\phi_1)/(M_2\angle\phi_2)=(M_1/M_2)\angle(\phi_1-\phi_2)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先保留乘积的完整实部与虚部；乘除相位可以超出主值范围，最后再化回。

HW 1 · a constrained phasor sum · 05

Choose an amplitude to cancel the quadrature part

Two currents are \(i_1(t)=a\cos(600t+\pi/3)\;\mathrm A\) and \(i_2(t)=6\cos(600t-\pi/6)\;\mathrm A\), where a is a positive real amplitude to be chosen. The desired total current must have phase 0° and a positive cosine amplitude.

  1. Express the total phasor as a real part plus j times an imaginary part in terms of a.
  2. Determine a and the resulting total peak amplitude. Check that the real part is positive.
  3. Write the total time-domain current and verify the sum at t = 0 and t = π/1200 s.
  4. Draw the two components and resultant on equal-scale current axes. Explain why cancelling the imaginary part alone would not guarantee a 0° phase if the real part were negative.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(I_T=a\angle60^\circ+6\angle(-30^\circ)\); all phasors use peak amplitudes.
中文提示（不含解答）

同相于正余弦要求虚部为零且实部为正；先用前一条件求未知幅值。

HW 2 · recompute impedance for each frequency · 06

The same components at two source frequencies

The series circuit has R = 12 Ω, L = 40 mH, and C = 100 μF. In two separate experiments the source is \(v_s(t)=12\cos(\omega t+\pi/9)\;\mathrm V\): experiment A uses ω = 500 rad/s, and experiment B uses ω = 1000 rad/s. Current is clockwise from the positive source terminal. Treat each experiment independently.

  1. Draw and label the impedance circuit for each experiment.
  2. For each, find total impedance, current phasor, and steady-state i(t).
  3. Find the resistor, inductor, and capacitor voltage phasors for each, and verify KVL separately.
  4. Compare the current amplitudes and source-relative phases. Explain why the current phasors from the two experiments cannot simply be added as if they shared one angular frequency.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\ Z_L=j\omega L,\ Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\), \(I=V_s/(Z_R+Z_L+Z_C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

频率改变时电感和电容阻抗都必须重算；每次试验内部才使用同一个 ω。

HW 2 · reduce a two-level network · 07

Parallel capacitor and a series RL branch

At ω = 500 rad/s, R₁ = 5 Ω is in series with two parallel branches between the next node and B. Branch 1 is C = 200 μF. Branch 2 is R₂ = 10 Ω in series with L = 20 mH. Input terminal A is before R₁. Find ZAB.

  1. Calculate ZC and the complete series-RL branch impedance.
  2. Reduce the parallel pair, then include R₁. Give the input impedance in rectangular and polar form.
  3. Check the parallel result by summing branch admittances.
  4. Identify the input network’s net reactance sign. Explain why having an inductor in one branch does not by itself determine that sign.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=Z_C(R_2+Z_L)/(Z_C+R_2+Z_L)\), \(Z_{AB}=R_1+Z_p\).
中文提示（不含解答）

R₂ 与 L 串联，整个支路再与 C 并联；不要将 R₁ 加进支路导纳。

HW 2 · admittance and three-current KCL · 08

A current source with three parallel branches

The source \(i_s(t)=0.005\cos(500t-\pi/6)\;\mathrm A\) injects current upward into node A. R = 1.0 kΩ, L = 2.0 H, and C = 1.0 μF each connect directly from A to ground. Define vA positive at A and all three branch currents downward.

  1. Draw the phasor circuit and calculate total admittance.
  2. Find VA in rectangular and polar form and write vA(t).
  3. Compute IR, IL, and IC and verify IR + IL + IC = Is.
  4. Compare the inductor and capacitor current phases and amplitudes. Explain why their individual current magnitudes should not be added as ordinary positive numbers when checking KCL.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Y=1/R+1/(j\omega L)+j\omega C\), \(V_A=I_s/Y\), \(I_R=V_A/R\), \(I_L=V_A/(j\omega L)\), \(I_C=j\omega CV_A\).
中文提示（不含解答）

并联支路电压相同；电感导纳与电容导纳的虚部符号相反。

HW 2 · divider and voltage polarity · 09

A deliberately reversed output reference

The source \(v_s(t)=20\cos(2000t+\pi/12)\;\mathrm V\) drives R = 300 Ω in series with C = 1.25 μF. Node A is the capacitor’s upper plate; its lower plate is grounded. Define the requested output as vo = vground − vA, so the positive output reference is at ground and the negative reference is at A. Current is clockwise.

  1. Find total impedance and current phasor.
  2. First find VA relative to ground, then find the requested Vo. Give both in rectangular and polar form.
  3. Write vo(t), and check it at t = 0 against −vA(0).
  4. Find the resistor voltage with the passive reference and verify VR + VA = Vs. Explain why substituting Vo for VA in that KVL sum would use the wrong sign.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V_A=V_sZ_C/(R+Z_C)\), \(V_o=-V_A\), \(Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

输出箭头或正负号决定所求电压；先计算常规节点电压，再按定义转换。

HW 2 · full steady-state calculation and check · 10

A parallel LC load at a specified frequency

The source \(v_s(t)=10\cos(1000t-\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\) drives a series R = 10 Ω, followed by node A. From A to ground, L = 20 mH and C = 100 μF are in parallel. Define vo from A to ground and source current through R toward A. Use only the stated frequency.

  1. Compute the parallel-load admittance and impedance, then the total input impedance.
  2. Find the source-current and output-voltage phasors in rectangular and polar form, and write vo(t).
  3. Find the inductor and capacitor currents. Verify their phasor sum equals the source current, even if an individual branch-current amplitude exceeds the source-current amplitude.
  4. Check KVL using the resistor voltage and Vo. Predict vo at t = 0 and t = π/2000 s.
  5. For optional LTspice verification, state the source frequency in Hz and the equivalent sine-source phase. Compare the two predicted phases in a late cycle after transients settle, using explicitly matching current directions.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Y_p=1/(j\omega L)+j\omega C\), \(Z_p=1/Y_p\), \(I_s=V_s/(R+Z_p)\), \(V_o=I_sZ_p\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先加并联导纳再求倒数；本题给定频率下导纳不为零。
