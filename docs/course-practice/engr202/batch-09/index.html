HW 1 · crossing direction and phase · 01

Recover a sinusoid from opposite zero crossings

A zero-offset voltage ranges from −8 V to +8 V. It crosses zero upward at t = 0.50 ms and next crosses zero downward at t = 2.50 ms. Use seconds in the final time-domain expression.

  1. Find its period, frequency and angular frequency.
  2. Write a positive-amplitude cosine with principal phase in (−180°,180°], and give its peak phasor.
  3. Find v(0), the first positive maximum and first minimum at or after zero.
  4. Sketch one cycle with time in ms, voltage in V, and both crossing directions marked.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(T=2\Delta t\), \(\omega=2\pi/T\), \(d[A\cos(\omega t+\phi)]/dt=-A\omega\sin(\omega t+\phi)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

相邻的反向过零点相隔半个周期；向上过零时，余弦的导数必须为正。

HW 1 · time delay and equivalent reference · 02

Delay a shifted sine until its cosine phase vanishes

Let \(x(t)=6\sin(400t+5\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\), and \(y(t)=x(t-\pi/1200\;\mathrm s)\).

  1. Express x and y as positive-amplitude cosines and give their peak phasors.
  2. Find the delay in ms, its fraction of a period, and the phase change in degrees.
  3. Compare x and y at t = 0 and t = T/4.
  4. Draw both phasors on equally scaled axes and identify which waveform is delayed.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\sin\theta=\cos(\theta-\pi/2)\), \(T=2\pi/\omega\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先把正弦相位改成余弦参考；时间延迟给相位减去 ωΔt。

HW 1 · rectangular multiplication and division · 03

A quotient lands exactly on the original divisor

Let \(z=-3-j4\) and \(w=1-j2\).

  1. Calculate z + w, zw, and z/w in rectangular form.
  2. Convert z, w, zw and z/w into polar form with positive magnitudes and principal phases.
  3. Check (z/w)w = z and explain the relationship between z and w².
  4. Plot z, w and the quotient. Label real and imaginary axes and state each quadrant.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(j^2=-1\), \(1/(a+jb)=(a-jb)/(a^2+b^2)\), \(\arg z=\operatorname{atan2}(\Im z,\Re z)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

共轭法能直接给出商的实部虚部；极角要按象限整理到主值范围。

HW 1 · a phase of pi is a sign reversal · 04

Combine a reversed cosine with two quadrature terms

At a common angular frequency, \(v(t)=4\cos(900t)-3\sin(900t)+2\cos(900t+\pi)\;\mathrm V\).

  1. Write all three peak phasors in rectangular form and add them.
  2. Convert the total to polar form and write one positive-amplitude cosine for v(t).
  3. Verify both expressions at t = 0 and t = π/1800 s.
  4. Draw a scaled tip-to-tail plot. Explain why the third term reduces the real component while the negative sine adds a positive imaginary component.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\cos(\theta+\pi)=-\cos\theta\), \(-\sin\theta=\cos(\theta+\pi/2)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先处理负号和 π 的相位，三项都写成同一个余弦参考后再相加。

HW 1 · target minus known phasors · 05

Complete a three-vector target without changing frequency

Two voltages are \(v_1(t)=6\cos(600t)\;\mathrm V\) and \(v_2(t)=4\cos(600t+\pi/2)\;\mathrm V\). The required total has cosine-reference peak phasor \(V_{target}=3+j4\;\mathrm V\).

  1. Find the additional phasor V3 so that V1 + V2 + V3 = Vtarget.
  2. Write v3(t) as a positive-amplitude cosine with principal phase.
  3. Write the target waveform in both cosine/sine-component form and single-cosine form.
  4. Draw all vectors to scale and verify the original three-waveform sum at t = 0 and t = π/1200 s.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V_3=V_{target}-V_1-V_2\); \(u+jw\leftrightarrow u\cos\omega t-w\sin\omega t\).
中文提示（不含解答）

缺少的相量等于目标减去已知两个相量；负的实数用正幅值加 180°相位表达。

HW 2 · consistent cosine reference · 06

Series RLC with a sine source and capacitive total

The source \(v_s(t)=15\sin(1000t+\pi/2)\;\mathrm V\) drives R = 12 Ω, L = 10 mH and C = 50 μF in series. Current is clockwise from the source positive terminal. Use passive element voltage drops along this current.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit including the cosine-reference source phasor.
  2. Find total impedance and current in rectangular and polar form, then write i(t).
  3. Calculate VR, VL and VC and verify KVL. State whether current leads or lags source voltage.
  4. Calculate i(0) and i(π/2000 s), and check the sign of each sample using the time-domain expression.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\ Z_L=j\omega L,\ Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\), \(I=V_s/Z_{total}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先把源的正弦写成余弦；电感和电容的虚部符号相反。

HW 2 · admittances of reactive branches · 07

A series resistor before a parallel LC pair

At ω = 1000 rad/s, terminal A connects through R = 6 Ω to node X. L = 20 mH and C = 25 μF each connect from X to terminal B. No other connections exist. Define input current from A toward X and both reactive-branch currents from X to B.

  1. Find ZL and ZC and combine them using parallel admittance.
  2. Find ZAB in rectangular and polar form and classify its net reactance.
  3. For VAB = 10∠0° V peak, find input current and VX relative to B.
  4. Find each reactive branch current and verify their sum equals the input current.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=(1/Z_L+1/Z_C)^{-1}\), \(Z_{AB}=R+Z_p\), \(V_X=IZ_p\).
中文提示（不含解答）

两个电抗先并联；电感与电容的导纳符号相反，但本题并未刚好抵消。

HW 2 · branch current and node voltage · 08

A current source drives a resistor and series RL branch

The source \(i_s(t)=0.50\cos(500t)\;\mathrm A\) injects current upward into node A. R₁ = 20 Ω connects from A to ground. A second branch consists of R₂ = 10 Ω followed by L = 20 mH from A to ground. Define vA positive at A and both branch currents downward.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit and compute the total admittance.
  2. Find VA in rectangular and polar form and write vA(t).
  3. Find both branch currents and verify KCL.
  4. Predict vA(0) and vA(π/1000 s). Explain why the resistor-only branch current is in phase with node voltage, not necessarily with source current.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Y=1/R_1+1/(R_2+j\omega L)\), \(V_A=I_s/Y\), \(I_1=V_A/R_1\), \(I_2=V_A/(R_2+j\omega L)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先合并第二支路的串联 R 与 L；节点电压是源电流除以总导纳。

HW 2 · divider sign and output polarity · 09

Measure a series capacitor with reversed probes

The source \(v_s(t)=12\cos(2000t+\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\) drives R = 6 Ω followed by C = 125 μF. The lower capacitor plate and source negative terminal are grounded. Node A is between R and C. Define current positive from the source through R toward A. The output probes are reversed: vo = V(ground) − V(A), so output is positive at ground and negative at A.

  1. Find the source phasor, total impedance and current phasor.
  2. First find VA using the ordinary capacitor divider; then find Vo using the stated polarity.
  3. Give Vo in rectangular/polar form and write vo(t).
  4. Check Vs = VR + VA and compute vo(0) and vo(π/4000 s). Explain why changing the output reference does not change the physical circuit current.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\), \(V_A=V_sZ_C/(R+Z_C)\), \(V_o=-V_A\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先按通常上正下负算出电容电压，再根据输出探针方向改变符号。

HW 2 · loaded divider and late-cycle checks · 10

A capacitor feeds a parallel RL output

The source \(v_s(t)=18\cos(1000t)\;\mathrm V\) connects through C = 100 μF to node A. R = 20 Ω and L = 20 mH each connect from A to ground. Output vo is positive at A. Source current travels through C toward A; load currents are downward.

  1. Find the parallel-load impedance and total input impedance.
  2. Find the source-current and output-voltage phasors in rectangular and polar form, and write vo(t).
  3. Calculate the two load currents and capacitor voltage drop from source to A. Verify KCL and KVL.
  4. Predict vo at t = 0 and t = π/2000 s. For optional LTspice, specify frequency in Hz and equivalent sine-source phase; compare these two positions in a late steady-state cycle, after transients settle. Include axis units and consistent current signs.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=(1/R+1/(j\omega L))^{-1}\), \(I_s=V_s/(Z_C+Z_p)\), \(V_o=I_sZ_p\), \(I_R=V_o/R\), \(I_L=V_o/(j\omega L)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

电容与并联负载整体串联；不能把三个元件的阻抗直接相加。
