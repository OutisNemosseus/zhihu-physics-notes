HW 1 · frequency and event timing · 01

Translate a waveform whose time input is in milliseconds

A voltage is specified by \(v=7\cos(0.4\pi t_{ms}-\pi/5)\;\mathrm V\), where \(t_{ms}\) is the numerical time measured in milliseconds.

  1. Rewrite the equation using time t in seconds, and find peak amplitude, phase in degrees, frequency and period.
  2. Find v(0), the first positive maximum, and the first downward zero crossing at or after t = 0. Give event times in ms.
  3. Draw one period with correctly labeled time and voltage axes and mark the two events.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(t_{ms}=1000t\), \(\omega=2\pi f\), \(T=1/f\). A cosine maximum has phase \(2k\pi\); downward zero crossings have phase \(\pi/2+2k\pi\).
中文提示（不含解答）

t_ms 与以秒计的 t 相差 1000 倍；不要直接把毫秒表达式的系数当作 rad/s。

HW 1 · sign, reference and time shift · 02

A negative sine is advanced by a quarter period

Let \(x(t)=-5\sin(600t-\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\). Define \(y(t)=x(t+\pi/1200\;\mathrm s)\).

  1. Write positive-amplitude cosine expressions and peak phasors for x and y, using principal phases in (−180°,180°].
  2. Find the time advance in ms and as a fraction of a period. Explain its effect on the cosine phase.
  3. Find x(0), y(0), x(T/4), and y(T/4), then sketch the relative displacement of the two waveforms.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(-\sin\theta=\cos(\theta+\pi/2)\), \(T=2\pi/\omega\).
中文提示（不含解答）

负的正弦可以先改写为余弦；提前相当于给相位增加 ωΔt。

HW 1 · products, quotients and phase signs · 03

Use a conjugate pair to simplify a ratio

Let \(a=4+j3\) and \(b=4-j3\).

  1. Find a + b, a − b, ab and a/b in rectangular form.
  2. Convert a, b, a − b and a/b to polar form with positive magnitude and principal phase.
  3. Check that the magnitude of a/b is one, verify (a/b)b = a, and draw a and b on equally scaled complex axes.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(j^2=-1\), \(|u+jv|=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}\), \(\arg z=\operatorname{atan2}(\Im z,\Re z)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

共轭的幅值相同、相位相反；商的幅值不能靠实部单独判断。

HW 1 · two-term phasor subtraction · 04

Subtract a cosine that is already shifted

At a common angular frequency, \(v(t)=10\cos(800t)-6\cos(800t+\pi/3)\;\mathrm V\).

  1. Write the two unsigned component phasors and subtract them to obtain V in rectangular and polar form.
  2. Express v(t) as one positive-amplitude cosine. Verify it against the original expression at t = 0 and t = π/1600 s.
  3. Draw the subtraction as tip-to-tail addition of the first phasor and the negative of the second. Label real/imaginary axes in V with the same scale.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(M\angle\phi=M(\cos\phi+j\sin\phi)\), \(V=V_1-V_2\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先写第二项本来的相量，再整体取负；不能只减幅值而保留原相位。

HW 1 · rectangular components and sampled checks · 05

A sine coefficient fixes the direction of a phasor

A waveform has the form \(v(t)=a\cos(1200t)+b\sin(1200t)\;\mathrm V\). Its peak magnitude is 13 V, v(0) = 5 V, and v(T/4) is negative, where T is the period.

  1. Find a and b, then give the cosine-reference peak phasor in rectangular and polar form.
  2. Write one positive-amplitude cosine and predict v(T/4), v(T/2), and v(3T/4).
  3. Draw the phasor in the correct quadrant and explain why the sign of v(T/4) resolves the two possible phases.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(M=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\), \(V=a-jb\), \(T=2\pi/\omega\).
中文提示（不含解答）

v(0) 给出余弦系数；幅值平方等于两个系数平方和，另一采样点的信息用于选择正负号。

HW 2 · series impedance and passive voltage drops · 06

The source is ninety degrees ahead of a cosine

The source \(v_s(t)=20\cos(1000t+\pi/2)\;\mathrm V\) drives series R = 16 Ω, L = 12 mH, C = 250 μF. Current is clockwise from the source positive terminal, and all element voltages are passive drops along that current.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit and find the source, total-impedance and current phasors in rectangular and polar form.
  2. Write i(t), calculate VR, VL and VC, and verify KVL. State the phase difference between current and source voltage.
  3. Find i(0) and i(π/2000 s). Check them using your time-domain current.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\ Z_L=j\omega L,\ Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\), \(I=V_s/(R+Z_L+Z_C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

源相位和阻抗相位分别处理；求电流相位时减去阻抗相位。

HW 2 · two-stage network reduction · 07

Combine a resistor with a parallel RC section

At ω = 1000 rad/s, terminal A connects through R₁ = 4 Ω to node X. R₂ = 8 Ω and C = 125 μF each connect from X to terminal B. Define input current from A toward X and both branch currents from X toward B. Apply VAB = 20∠0° V peak, with B the reference node.

  1. Find the parallel equivalent ZXB and total input impedance ZAB in rectangular and polar form.
  2. Find input current and the node-X voltage relative to B.
  3. Find resistor and capacitor branch currents and verify KCL at X. Check the source voltage by adding the R₁ drop and node voltage.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=(1/R_2+1/Z_C)^{-1}\), \(Z_{AB}=R_1+Z_p\), \(I=V_{AB}/Z_{AB}\), \(V_X=IZ_p\).
中文提示（不含解答）

电容只与第二个电阻并联；第一个电阻最后与并联等效相加。

HW 2 · admittance and phase quadrant · 08

A shifted current source supplies three parallel elements

The source \(i_s(t)=2\cos(1000t-\pi/2)\;\mathrm A\) injects current upward into node A. R = 10 Ω, L = 10 mH and C = 200 μF each connect directly from A to ground. Define vA positive at A and all three element currents downward.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit, convert the source to its peak phasor, and find total admittance and equivalent impedance.
  2. Find VA in rectangular/polar form and write vA(t). Calculate all three branch-current phasors and verify their sum equals Is.
  3. Evaluate vA(0) and vA(π/2000 s). Explain why the negative real part of VA does not mean its peak magnitude is negative.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Y=1/R+1/(j\omega L)+j\omega C\), \(V_A=I_s/Y\), \(I_R=V_A/R\), \(I_L=V_A/(j\omega L)\), \(I_C=j\omega CV_A\).
中文提示（不含解答）

并联时先加导纳；源本身的 −90°也必须带入计算，最后检查电压所处象限。

HW 2 · voltage division in an RL loop · 09

An inductor output can have a simple phase

The source \(v_s(t)=20\sqrt2\cos(2000t+\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\) drives R = 20 Ω followed by L = 10 mH in a series loop. The lower end of L is grounded. Output vo is measured from node A between R and L to ground, with A positive. Current flows from the source through R toward A.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit. Find total impedance and current in rectangular/polar form.
  2. Find Vo using voltage division, write vo(t), and check Vs = VR + Vo.
  3. Compute vo(0) and vo(π/4000 s). Compare the phases of source voltage, current, and output voltage.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V_o=V_s(j\omega L)/(R+j\omega L)\), \(I=V_s/(R+j\omega L)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

输出跨在电感上，因此分压分子用电感阻抗；三个量的相位不必相同。

HW 2 · complete steady-state check · 10

Keep a sine source reference through a loaded RC divider

The source \(v_s(t)=15\sin(1000t)\;\mathrm V\) connects through R₁ = 10 Ω to node A. R₂ = 20 Ω and C = 50 μF each connect from A to ground. Output vo is positive at A. Source current flows through R₁ toward A, with both load currents downward.

  1. Find the cosine-reference source phasor, parallel-load impedance, total input impedance, and source-current phasor.
  2. Find Vo in rectangular/polar form and write vo(t). Calculate both load currents and the R₁ voltage drop; verify KCL and KVL.
  3. Predict vo(0) and vo(π/2000 s). For optional LTspice, state source frequency in Hz and its sine-source phase. Compare these two positions in a late cycle after transients settle, with labeled units and matching reference directions.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=(1/R_2+j\omega C)^{-1}\), \(I_s=V_s/(R_1+Z_p)\), \(V_o=I_sZ_p\), \(I_R=V_o/R_2\), \(I_C=j\omega CV_o\).
中文提示（不含解答）

题目给的是正弦源；相量计算按余弦参考，SPICE 的正弦源设置则按它自己的参考。
