HW 1 · reconstruct a zero-offset sinusoid · 01

Read a waveform from two measured peaks

A zero-offset sinusoidal voltage has peak value +5 V and minimum −5 V. Two consecutive positive maxima occur at t = 0.50 ms and t = 4.50 ms. Use t in seconds in the final equation.

  1. Find the period, frequency and angular frequency.
  2. Write v(t) as a positive-amplitude cosine with principal phase in (−180°,180°], and state its peak phasor.
  3. Find v(0), the first minimum at or after zero, and the first downward zero crossing at or after zero.
  4. Sketch one period starting at t = 0 with time in ms and voltage in V.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(T=t_{peak,2}-t_{peak,1}\), \(f=1/T\), \(\omega=2\pi/T\). A positive cosine maximum has phase \(2k\pi\).
中文提示（不含解答）

相邻同类极值的时间差才是一个周期；将已知峰值时刻代入余弦相位条件。

HW 1 · time shift and reference conversion · 02

Advance a sine and compare the phases

Let \(x(t)=4\sin(300t-\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\), and define \(y(t)=x(t+\pi/900\;\mathrm s)\).

  1. Give positive-amplitude cosine forms and peak phasors for x and y.
  2. Calculate the time advance in ms and the phase change in radians and degrees. Express the advance as a fraction of one period.
  3. Evaluate x and y at t = 0 and t = T/4, where T is their common period.
  4. Draw X and Y on equal-scale complex axes and explain the rotation caused by the advance.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\sin\theta=\cos(\theta-\pi/2)\), \(T=2\pi/\omega\).
中文提示（不含解答）

正弦转余弦减去 90°；时间提前会给相位增加 ωΔt。

HW 1 · complex arithmetic in the upper half-plane · 03

Track signs through a product and quotient

Let \(a=-6+j8\) and \(b=2+j\).

  1. Find a + b, ab and a/b in rectangular form.
  2. Convert a, b, ab and a/b to polar form with positive magnitudes and principal phases in (−180°,180°].
  3. Verify (a/b)b = a by rectangular arithmetic, and verify the magnitude and phase rules for the quotient.
  4. State the quadrant of every rectangular result and explain why using arctan(imaginary/real) without a quadrant check can fail.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(j^2=-1\), \(1/(u+jv)=(u-jv)/(u^2+v^2)\), \(\phi=\operatorname{atan2}(\Im z,\Re z)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

乘除后要保留实部和虚部的符号；atan2 比直接取两个数的比值更可靠。

HW 1 · addition in rectangular form · 04

Combine a shifted cosine with an unshifted sine

The total voltage is \(v(t)=9\cos(700t+\pi/3)+3\sqrt3\sin(700t)\;\mathrm V\).

  1. Write the two peak phasors using a cosine reference.
  2. Find the total phasor in rectangular and polar form, then express v as one positive-amplitude cosine.
  3. Check the original sum and combined form at t = 0 and t = π/1400 s.
  4. Draw a tip-to-tail phasor plot on equal-scale voltage axes. Explain why the second term reduces, rather than increases, the imaginary part of the first phasor.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\sin\theta=\cos(\theta-\pi/2)\); \(M\angle\phi=M(\cos\phi+j\sin\phi)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

正的正弦对应负 90°的余弦相位；先统一参考再相加。

HW 1 · cosine and sine coefficients · 05

Recover a third-quadrant phasor from samples

A zero-offset waveform of angular frequency ω = 800 rad/s has the form \(v(t)=a\cos(800t)+b\sin(800t)\;\mathrm V\), with real a and b. Measurements give v(0) = −3 V and v(T/4) = +4 V, where T is the period.

  1. Find a and b and write the cosine-reference phasor in rectangular form.
  2. Give its positive magnitude and principal phase, then write one positive-amplitude cosine for v(t).
  3. Predict v(T/2) and v(3T/4), and use all four samples to sketch one cycle.
  4. Plot the phasor and explain why b is the negative of its imaginary coefficient.
Reference / 必要约定与公式For \(V=u+jw\), \(v(t)=u\cos\omega t-w\sin\omega t\); \(T=2\pi/\omega\).
中文提示（不含解答）

四分之一周期时余弦为零、正弦为一；最后转换成相量时注意虚部符号。

HW 2 · RLC impedance and KVL · 06

Series voltages when the current phase is negative

The source \(v_s(t)=10\sqrt2\cos(1000t-\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\) drives R = 10 Ω, L = 30 mH and C = 50 μF in series. Current is clockwise from the positive source terminal. Element voltages are passive drops along the current.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit and state the source phasor.
  2. Find total impedance and current in rectangular and polar form; write i(t).
  3. Find VR, VL and VC and verify their sum equals Vs.
  4. Find i(0) and i(π/2000 s). State how current phase compares with source phase.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\ Z_L=j\omega L,\ Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\), \(I=V_s/(Z_R+Z_L+Z_C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

源相位和阻抗相位都要计入；负相位不表示电流幅值为负。

HW 2 · equivalent impedance with two stages · 07

A capacitor before a parallel RL pair

At ω = 2000 rad/s, terminal A connects through C = 125 μF to node X. From X to terminal B are R = 12 Ω and L = 6 mH in parallel. There are no other connections.

  1. Calculate each component impedance and the parallel equivalent ZXB.
  2. Find ZAB in rectangular and polar form. State whether its net reactance is inductive or capacitive.
  3. Verify ZXB by adding branch admittances.
  4. If a peak voltage of 10∠0° V is applied from A to B, find the input current in rectangular and polar form.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=(1/R+1/(j\omega L))^{-1}\), \(Z_{AB}=Z_C+Z_p\), \(I=V_{AB}/Z_{AB}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

电容与整个并联等效串联；不要把电容直接并入 R 和 L 的导纳。

HW 2 · nodal admittance and current check · 08

A current source feeding a resistor and a series RC branch

The source \(i_s(t)=0.40\cos(1000t)\;\mathrm A\) injects current upward into node A. One branch from A to ground is R₁ = 30 Ω. The other branch is R₂ = 10 Ω in series with C = 50 μF. Define vA positive at A and both branch currents downward.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit and compute total admittance.
  2. Find VA in rectangular and polar form and write vA(t).
  3. Find I1 and I2 and verify I1 + I2 = Is.
  4. Predict vA(0) and vA(π/2000 s). Explain why the capacitor shares the second branch current rather than the source current.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Y=1/R_1+1/(R_2+Z_C)\), \(V_A=I_s/Y\), \(I_1=V_A/R_1\), \(I_2=V_A/(R_2+Z_C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先把 R₂ 与 C 串联相加，再求整条支路的导纳。

HW 2 · two-element output division · 09

Output across a resistor and capacitor together

The source \(v_s(t)=9\cos(500t)\;\mathrm V\) drives a series loop containing L = 40 mH, R = 12 Ω and C = 250 μF, in that order from the positive source terminal. The lower capacitor plate is grounded. Define vo from node A between L and R to ground, so output spans R and C. Current follows the loop from the source through L, R and C.

  1. Calculate total input impedance and the combined output impedance.
  2. Use voltage division to find Vo in rectangular and polar form and write vo(t).
  3. Find I and each passive element voltage. Verify Vo = VR + VC and Vs = VL + Vo.
  4. Predict vo(0) and vo(π/1000 s). Explain why the inductor impedance belongs in the divider denominator but not its output numerator.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V_o=V_s(R+Z_C)/(Z_L+R+Z_C)\), \(Z_L=j\omega L\), \(Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先明确输出端跨越了哪两个元件，再写分压公式的分子。

HW 2 · full steady-state verification · 10

A loaded RL divider with a real source phasor

The source \(v_s(t)=20\cos(2000t)\;\mathrm V\) connects through R₁ = 8 Ω to node A. R₂ = 24 Ω and L = 12 mH each connect from A to ground. Output vo is positive at A. Source current flows through R₁ toward A, and load currents are downward.

  1. Find the parallel-load and total input impedances.
  2. Find source-current and output-voltage phasors in rectangular and polar form, then write vo(t).
  3. Calculate resistor and inductor load currents and verify KCL. Check KVL using the voltage across R₁ and Vo.
  4. Predict vo at t = 0 and t = π/4000 s. For optional LTspice checking, give source frequency in Hz and the equivalent sine-source phase, then compare those two positions in a late cycle after transients settle. Label axes with units and match current directions.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=(1/R_2+1/(j\omega L))^{-1}\), \(I_s=V_s/(R_1+Z_p)\), \(V_o=I_sZ_p\), \(I_R=V_o/R_2\), \(I_L=V_o/(j\omega L)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

并联支路的电压相同；电感电流相对于支路电压滞后 90°。
