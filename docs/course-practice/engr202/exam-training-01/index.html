Sinusoids · 01

Convert a sinusoid to a cosine-reference phasor

Given \(x(t)=-12\sin(400t+\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\):
  1. Rewrite x(t) as a cosine with positive amplitude and phase in (−180°,180°].
  2. Find the peak phasor X in polar and rectangular form.
  3. Find f, T, x(0), and x(T/4).
  4. Sketch X on equal-scale real and imaginary axes.
中文提示

先把 sin 改写成 cos，再用相位加 180° 消掉负振幅。

查看答案与步骤
\[-12\sin(400t+\pi/6)=12\cos(400t+2\pi/3).\]\[X=12\angle120^\circ=-6+j6\sqrt3\;\mathrm V.\]\[f=400/(2\pi)=63.662\;\mathrm{Hz},\quad T=\pi/200=15.708\;\mathrm{ms}.\]\[x(0)=-6\;\mathrm V,\quad x(T/4)=-6\sqrt3=-10.392\;\mathrm V.\]相量箭头终点为 (−6, 10.392)，在第二象限。
Phasor addition · 02

Add two sinusoids at one frequency

Let \(v_1(t)=10\cos(500t+\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\) and \(v_2(t)=8\sin(500t+\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\).
  1. Convert both to peak cosine-reference phasors.
  2. Find the phasor of v(t)=v₁(t)+v₂(t), in rectangular and polar form.
  3. Write v(t) as one cosine.
  4. Check your result at t=0.
中文提示

sin 的相位先减 90°；相加用直角坐标。

查看答案与步骤
\[V_1=10\angle30^\circ,\quad V_2=8\angle(-60^\circ).\]\[V=12.66 − j1.9282\;\mathrm V=12.806∠-8.6598°\;\mathrm V.\]\[v(t)=12.806248\cos(500t+(-0.151142))\;\mathrm V.\]直接代入 t=0：10cos30°+8sin30°=12.660254 V，等于 V 的实部。
Phase and delay · 03

Relate a time delay to a phasor rotation

\(x(t)=8\cos(400t+\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\), and \(z(t)=x(t-\pi/1600)\).
  1. Find T and express the delay Δt as a fraction of T.
  2. Find X and Z in polar form. Does z lead or lag x?
  3. Evaluate x(0), z(0), x(T/4), and z(T/4).
  4. Sketch X and Z on the same equal-scale complex axes.
中文提示

延迟产生相位 −ωΔt；相量 Z 相对于 X 顺时针转。

查看答案与步骤
\[T=2\pi/400=\pi/200,\quad\Delta t=\pi/1600=T/8=1.9635\;\mathrm{ms}.\]\[X=8\angle30^\circ,\quad Z=Xe^{-j\pi/4}=8\angle(-15^\circ)\;\mathrm V.\]z 落后 x 45°。\[x(0)=4\sqrt3=6.9282,\ z(0)=8\cos15^\circ=7.7274\;\mathrm V,\]\[x(T/4)=-4,\ z(T/4)=8\sin15^\circ=2.0706\;\mathrm V.\]箭头终点分别为 (6.9282,4) 与 (7.7274,−2.0706)。
Impedance · 04

Find impedance and resonance in a series RLC circuit

A resistor R=30 Ω, inductor L=10 mH, and capacitor C=20 μF are connected in series.
  1. At ω=2000 rad/s, find ZR, ZL, ZC and total Z in rectangular and polar form.
  2. State whether the circuit is net inductive or capacitive.
  3. Find the resonant angular frequency ω₀, resonant frequency f₀, and Z at resonance.
中文提示

ZL=jωL，ZC=−j/(ωC)；串联谐振时两个虚部抵消。

查看答案与步骤
\[Z_R=30,\quad Z_L=j20,\quad Z_C=-j25\;\Omega.\]\[Z=30-j5=30.4138\angle(-9.4623^\circ)\;\Omega.\]虚部为负，净容性。\[\omega_0=1/\sqrt{LC}=2236.068\;\mathrm{rad/s},\quad f_0=355.881\;\mathrm{Hz}.\]谐振时 Z=R=30 Ω。
Series RLC · 05

Solve the current and element voltages in a series loop

In the series loop below, R=30 Ω, L=20 mH, C=6.25 μF and \(v_s(t)=100\cos(2000t+20\pi/180)\;\mathrm V\). Current i flows from the positive source terminal through R, L, and C. Element voltages follow the passive sign convention.
  1. Find total Z and the peak current phasor I.
  2. Write i(t).
  3. Find VR, VL and VC and verify KVL using rectangular phasors.
  4. Find the average power absorbed by R.
中文提示

先算出 ZL=j40、ZC=−j80，再用 I=Vs/Z。元件电压要按复数相加。

查看答案与步骤
\[Z=30+j40-j80=30-j40=50\angle(-53.1301^\circ)\;\Omega.\]\[I=2∠73.13°\;\mathrm A,\quad i(t)=2\cos(2000t+1.276361)\;\mathrm A.\]\[V_R=17.412 + j57.418,\quad V_L=-76.557 + j23.216,\quad V_C=153.11 − j46.432\;\mathrm V.\]三者相加为 93.969 + j34.202 V = 100∠20° V。\[P_R=\tfrac12|I|^2R=60\;\mathrm W.\]
Parallel RLC · 06

Use KCL for a current-fed parallel network

An ideal current source \(i_s(t)=5\cos(1000t-\pi/6)\;\mathrm A\) injects current upward from ground into node A. Three parallel branches connect A to ground: R=20 Ω, L=40 mH and C=50 μF. V=VA relative to ground; IR, IL and IC are directed downward.
  1. Find the total admittance Y and equivalent impedance Z.
  2. Find V in rectangular and polar form, then write vA(t).
  3. Find IR, IL and IC and verify KCL.
  4. State whether the source current leads or lags the node voltage.
中文提示

并联先加导纳。电源向节点注入，三个支路向地流出，因此 Is=IR+IL+IC。

查看答案与步骤
\[Y=0.05+j0.025\;\mathrm S,\quad Z=16-j8\;\Omega.\]\[V=49.282 − j74.641=89.443∠-56.565°\;\mathrm V.\]\[v_A(t)=89.442719\cos(1000t+(-0.987246))\;\mathrm V.\]\[I_R=2.4641 − j3.7321,\quad I_L=-1.866 − j1.2321,\quad I_C=3.7321 + j2.4641\;\mathrm A.\]相加得到 4.3301 − j2.5 A = 5∠(−30°) A。Y 的相角为 +26.565°，所以电源电流超前节点电压 26.565°。
Loaded RC divider · 07

Analyze a loaded voltage divider

A source with peak phasor Vs=60∠(−10°) V at ω=1000 rad/s drives a 10 Ω series resistor. The output node A has a 20 Ω resistor and a 50 μF capacitor connected in parallel to ground. Vo is measured from A to ground.
  1. Find the load impedance Zp.
  2. Find H=Vo/Vs in rectangular and polar form.
  3. Find Vo and write vo(t).
  4. Compare |H| with the result if the 20 Ω load resistor were removed.
中文提示

电容与 20 Ω 并联构成负载；不能直接把三个元件当成串联。

查看答案与步骤
\[Z_p=20\parallel(-j20)=10-j10\;\Omega.\]\[H=\frac{10-j10}{20-j10}=0.6-j0.2=0.63246∠-18.435°.\]\[V_o=37.947∠-28.435°\;\mathrm V,\quad v_o(t)=37.947332\cos(1000t+(-0.496283))\;\mathrm V.\]移除 20 Ω 后：\(H_0=(-j20)/(10-j20)=0.8-j0.4\)，\(|H_0|=0.894427\)。带载时 |H|=0.632456，幅值更小。
Node-voltage analysis · 08

Solve a two-node AC network

All impedances below are specified at ω=1000 rad/s. Node A connects to ground through 10 Ω and −j20 Ω. A 20 Ω resistor connects A to B. Node B connects to ground through j10 Ω. An ideal current source Is=2∠0° A injects current from ground into A.
  1. Write the two node-voltage equations using ground as the reference.
  2. Solve for VA and VB in rectangular and polar form.
  3. Find the current IAB from A to B.
  4. Verify KCL at B.
中文提示

A 节点流出电流之和等于 2；B 节点没有独立电流源。连接 A、B 的电流为 (VA−VB)/20。

查看答案与步骤
\[\frac{V_A}{10}+\frac{V_A}{-j20}+\frac{V_A-V_B}{20}=2,\quad\frac{V_B-V_A}{20}+\frac{V_B}{j10}=0.\]\[(0.15+j0.05)V_A-0.05V_B=2,\quad-0.05V_A+(0.05-j0.1)V_B=0.\]\[V_A=13.659 − j2.9268=13.969∠-12.095°\;\mathrm V,\]\[V_B=3.9024 + j4.878=6.247∠51.34°\;\mathrm V.\]\[I_{AB}=0.4878 − j0.39024\;\mathrm A.\]B 节点：\((V_B-V_A)/20=-0.4878 + j0.39024\) A，\(V_B/(j10)=0.4878 − j0.39024\) A，两者之和为 0。
Mesh-current analysis · 09

Solve coupled mesh-current equations

Two adjacent planar meshes share a 4 Ω resistor. Let I1 and I2 be clockwise peak mesh currents. In mesh 1, the unshared elements are a 6 Ω resistor, j10 Ω inductor impedance and a voltage source Vs=20∠0° V that is a voltage rise along I1. Mesh 2 has unshared elements 8 Ω and −j8 Ω and no source. The shared-branch reference current is I1−I2 in the direction of I1.
  1. Write both mesh equations.
  2. Find I1, I2 and the shared-branch current.
  3. Find the average power in the shared 4 Ω resistor.
  4. Verify both mesh equations numerically.
中文提示

公共电阻左网孔压降为 4(I1−I2)，右网孔为 4(I2−I1)。

查看答案与步骤
\[(10+j10)I_1-4I_2=20,\quad-4I_1+(12-j8)I_2=0.\]\[I_1=1.065 − j1.1011=1.5319∠-45.955°\;\mathrm A,\]\[I_2=0.41516 − j0.090253=0.42486∠-12.265°\;\mathrm A.\]\[I_1-I_2=0.64982 − j1.0108=1.2017∠-57.265°\;\mathrm A.\]\[P_4=\tfrac12|I_1-I_2|^2(4)=2.888087\;\mathrm W.\]把这些复数代回，两式左边分别为 20+j0 V 与 0+j0 V；相量为峰值，所以平均功率要乘 1/2。
AC power · 10

Find instantaneous and average power

A load has voltage \(v(t)=120\sqrt2\cos(377t+\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\) and current \(i(t)=5\sqrt2\cos(377t-\pi/6)\;\mathrm A\). Current enters the positive voltage-reference terminal.
  1. Find Vrms, Irms and the phase difference θv−θi.
  2. Derive p(t)=v(t)i(t) using a product-to-sum identity.
  3. Find average real power P and the power factor. State leading or lagging.
  4. Find the load impedance in rectangular form.
  5. Find the minimum and maximum instantaneous power and explain a negative p(t).
中文提示

峰值分别是 120√2 与 5√2，不是 RMS。相位差是 60°，两相位之和是 0。

查看答案与步骤
\[V_{\mathrm{rms}}=120\;\mathrm V,\quad I_{\mathrm{rms}}=5\;\mathrm A,\quad\theta=60^\circ.\]\[p(t)=600[\cos60^\circ+\cos(754t)]=300+600\cos(754t)\;\mathrm W.\]\[P=300\;\mathrm W,\quad\mathrm{pf}=\cos60^\circ=0.5\text{ lagging}.\]\[Z=24\angle60^\circ=12+j12\sqrt3=12+j20.7846\;\Omega.\]\[p_{\min}=-300\;\mathrm W,\quad p_{\max}=900\;\mathrm W.\]负的瞬时功率表示负载暂时向电源返还能量；平均功率仍为正。
