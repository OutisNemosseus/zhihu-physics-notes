Series RLC Resonance and Half-Power Bandwidth

A variable-frequency ideal source with voltage Vₛ = 10.0∠0° V RMS drives the series RLC circuit shown. The source magnitude stays constant as its frequency changes. All components are ideal and the resistor shown is the entire series resistance.

Show your work. Write Z(jω) and find resonance by cancelling its imaginary part. Derive the half-power condition from resistor power, then find the bandwidth. Find the capacitor voltage using its impedance at resonance. Enter answers to three significant figures. Define f₁ and f₂ as the lower and upper positive frequencies where resistor power is half its maximum.

展开中文推导

Z(jω) = R + j[ωL − 1/(ωC)]。谐振时虚部为零，串联总阻抗等于 R。

  1. ω₀L = 1/(ω₀C)，所以 ω₀ = 1/√(LC) = 1/√[(0.100)(10.0×10⁻⁶)] = 1000 rad/s。f₀ = ω₀/(2π) ≈ 159 Hz。
  2. Q = ω₀L/R = 1000 × 0.100/20.0 = 5.00。
  3. 电阻功率 P = Vₛ²R/[R² + (ωL − 1/(ωC))²]。峰值为 Vₛ²/R，半功率时 |ωL − 1/(ωC)| = R。解得 ω₁ = [√(R² + 4L/C) − R]/(2L)，ω₂ = [√(R² + 4L/C) + R]/(2L)。相减得 ω₂ − ω₁ = R/L = 200 rad/s。因此带宽 f₂ − f₁ = 200/(2π) ≈ 31.8 Hz。
  4. 谐振时 I = Vₛ/R = 10.0/20.0 = 0.500 A RMS。
  5. |ZC| = 1/(ω₀C) = 100 Ω，故 |VC| = |I| |ZC| = 0.500 × 100 = 50.0 V RMS。其相位为 −90°，与电感电压相反，两者在 KVL 中抵消。
