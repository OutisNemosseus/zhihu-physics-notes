RMS and Heating from a Nonsinusoidal Current

An ideal current source drives a 10.0 Ω resistor with the periodic current shown. The period is T = 4.00 ms. Within each period, i(t) = (3000 A/s)t for 0 ≤ t < 2.00 ms and i(t) = −2.00 A for 2.00 ms ≤ t < 4.00 ms. Time t in this formula is in seconds.

Show your work. Write separate integrals for average current and mean-square current over one complete period. Then use the RMS result to find the resistor heating power. Enter numerical answers to three significant figures.

展开中文推导
  1. f = 1/T = 1/0.004 = 250 Hz。
  2. 平均电流等于带符号面积除以周期。前半周期三角形面积是 ½ × 0.002 × 6 = 0.006 A·s，后半周期面积是 −2 × 0.002 = −0.004 A·s。因此 Iavg = (0.006 − 0.004)/0.004 = 0.500 A。
  3. 均方值为 [∫00.002(3000t)²dt + ∫0.0020.004(−2)²dt]/0.004。前一项积分为 9×10⁶ × (0.002)³/3 = 0.024 A²·s，后一项为 4 × 0.002 = 0.008 A²·s。均方值为 8 A²，所以 IRMS = √8 ≈ 2.83 A。
  4. 平均功率 P = RIRMS² = 10 × 8 = 80.0 W。
  5. 瞬时功率 p(t) = Ri(t)²。电流幅值的峰值为 6 A，波形在跳变前趋近该值，峰值功率为 10 × 6² = 360 W。
