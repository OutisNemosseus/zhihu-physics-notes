Reconnecting a Three-Phase Load

A balanced, positive-sequence (abc) three-phase source supplies the delta load shown. Its line-to-line voltage is Vₐᵦ = 208∠0° V RMS. Each branch has impedance Z = 12 + j9 Ω. Take Iₐᵦ from a to b and Iₐ from the source into the load. Assume ideal lines.

After analyzing the delta connection, reconnect the same three impedances in a balanced wye on the same source, with one impedance from each line to the common star point.

Show your work. On paper, derive Iₐ from the branch currents using KCL and explain how reconnecting the load changes each branch voltage. Round numerical answers to three significant figures. Use principal phase angles.

展开中文推导
  1. Δ 接法中，ab 支路两端就是线电压。阻抗 Z = 15∠36.87° Ω，所以 Iₐᵦ = 208∠0°/Z = 13.87∠−36.87° A，幅值约为 13.9 A。
  2. 正相序下 V꜀ₐ = 208∠120° V，故 I꜀ₐ = 13.87∠83.13° A。a 节点的 KCL 给出 Iₐ = Iₐᵦ − I꜀ₐ。两相量幅值相同、夹角 120°，相减后幅值为 √3 × 13.87 ≈ 24.0 A。
  3. 把公共相量 Iₐᵦ 提出来，Iₐ = Iₐᵦ(1 − 1∠120°) = Iₐᵦ√3∠−30°。因此相角为 −36.87° − 30° ≈ −66.9°。
  4. 每条支路的平均功率为 |Iₐᵦ|² × 12 W。乘以三条支路，PΔ = 3(208/15)² × 12 ≈ 6.92 kW。
  5. 改接 Y 后，每个阻抗跨在一条线和星点之间，电压幅值变为 208/√3 V。Y 接线电流等于支路电流，得 208/(√3 × 15) ≈ 8.01 A。
  6. 每个阻抗上的电压幅值变为原来的 1/√3，电流幅值也变为原来的 1/√3。同一电阻的平均功率随电流平方变化，所以总功率比是 1/3 ≈ 0.333。
