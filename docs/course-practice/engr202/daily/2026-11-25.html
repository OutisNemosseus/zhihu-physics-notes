Recover a Series RL Load from Time-Domain Signals

A passive load consists of an unknown resistor R in series with an ideal inductor L. In sinusoidal steady state, its terminal signals are

v(t) = 40 cos(1000t + 30°) V
i(t) = 4.00 cos(1000t − 30°) A.

Time t is in seconds. The angular frequency is 1000 rad/s and the phase offsets are given in degrees. Use a cosine phasor reference. The current enters the positive voltage terminal, as shown.

Show your work. Convert both signals to RMS phasors, divide to find the load impedance, and calculate complex power using the conjugate of the current phasor. Enter answers to three significant figures.

展开中文推导

时域余弦前的 40 V 和 4.00 A 是峰值。RMS 相量为 V = (40/√2)∠30° V，I = (4/√2)∠−30° A。按图示被动符号约定，S = VI* 表示负载吸收的复功率。

  1. 相量相除时幅值相除、角度相减。Z = V/I = 10∠[30° − (−30°)] = 10∠60° Ω，阻抗相角是 +60.0°。
  2. Z = 10 cos60° + j10 sin60° = 5 + j5√3 Ω，所以 R = 5.00 Ω。
  3. 电感电抗 XL = ωL = 5√3 Ω，故 L = (5√3)/1000 H = 5√3 mH ≈ 8.66 mH。
  4. S = (40/√2)(4/√2)∠[30° − (−30°)] = 80∠60° = 40 + j40√3 VA。因此 P = 40.0 W。也可用 IRMS²R = (4/√2)² × 5 = 40 W 验证。
  5. Q = 40√3 ≈ +69.3 var。正号与感性负载、电流相对电压滞后 60° 相符。

原创题，参考 HW 5 的正弦源与交流功率考点，改为从端口时域测量反推 R 和 L。
