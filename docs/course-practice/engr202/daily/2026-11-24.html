RL Frequency Response with Coil Resistance

An ideal sinusoidal source drives an 80 Ω resistor in series with a coil. Model the coil as an ideal 20 mH inductor in series with a 20 Ω winding resistance. Vout is measured across the entire coil, including its winding resistance. The output is unloaded. Define H(jω) = Vout/Vₛ.

Show your work. Derive H(jω) by voltage division. Calculate its DC and high-frequency limits, then solve for the frequency where the gain magnitude is 1/√2 of its high-frequency limit. Use the source voltage as the phase reference. Enter answers to three significant figures.

展开中文推导

输出跨在线圈的电阻和电感两端，故 H(jω) = (r + jωL)/(R + r + jωL) = (20 + jω·0.020)/(100 + jω·0.020)。高频极限为 1 V/V。

  1. 令 ω = 0，H(0) = 20/100 = 0.200 V/V。
  2. 分母对应的极点角频率为 ωₚ = 100/0.020 = 5000 rad/s，故 fₚ = 5000/(2π) ≈ 796 Hz。
  3. 在极点频率，ωL = 100 Ω。因此 |H| = √(20² + 100²)/√(100² + 100²) = √0.52 ≈ 0.721 V/V。
  4. 同一频率下，∠H = arctan(100/20) − arctan(100/100) ≈ 78.690° − 45° = +33.7°。
  5. 相对高频极限的 −3.01 dB 频率满足 (20² + (ωL)²)/(100² + (ωL)²) = 1/2。整理得 (ωL)² = 100² − 2×20² = 9200，故 f = √9200/(2π×0.020) ≈ 763 Hz。

本电路的分子含有零点。线圈内阻使低频增益大于零，也使极点处的幅值偏离 1/√2。
