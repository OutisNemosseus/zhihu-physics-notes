RC High-Pass Filter with Source Resistance

An ideal source with phasor voltage Vₛ = 5.00∠0° V RMS drives the circuit below. Its series resistance is Rₛ = 100 Ω, the coupling capacitor is C = 1.00 μF, and the load resistor is RL = 400 Ω. Vout is measured across RL with an ideal, nonloading voltmeter.

Show your work. Derive H(jω) = Vout/Vₛ, find the low- and high-frequency limits, and define cutoff as the frequency where |H| is its high-frequency limit divided by √2. Use the exact calculated cutoff frequency in the remaining parts. Enter answers to three significant figures.

展开中文推导

三元件串联，输出跨在负载电阻上。由分压得 H(jω) = RL/[Rₛ + RL + 1/(jωC)] = jωCRL/[1 + jωC(Rₛ + RL)]。低频极限为 0。

  1. 高频时电容阻抗趋近于 0，|H| → RL/(Rₛ + RL) = 400/500 = 0.800 V/V。
  2. 写成 H = 0.8·jx/(1 + jx)，其中 x = ωC(Rₛ + RL)。截止时 x = 1，故 fc = 1/[2πC(Rₛ + RL)] = 1/[2π(10⁻⁶)(500)] ≈ 318 Hz。
  3. 截止处 |H| = 0.8/√2，所以 |Vout| = 5.00 × 0.8/√2 = 4/√2 ≈ 2.83 V RMS。
  4. 截止时 H = 0.8j/(1 + j) = 0.4 + j0.4，相角为 +45.0°。输出超前源电压。
  5. 负载电阻平均功率为 |Vout|²/RL = (4/√2)²/400 = 0.0200 W = 20.0 mW。

交叉验证：截止时 ZC = −j500 Ω，I = 5/(500 − j500) = 0.005 + j0.005 A。Vout = 400I = 2 + j2 V，给出相同幅值、相位和负载功率。
