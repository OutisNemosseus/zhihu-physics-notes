Inverting Active Filter

Assume the op amp is ideal and operates in its linear region. All voltages are measured relative to ground. Derive H(jω) = Vout/Vin on paper and sketch the asymptotic magnitude Bode plot, labeling the corner frequency and the passband level. Show your work. Enter numerical answers to three significant figures.

展开中文推导

理想运放的反相端是虚地，输入支路阻抗 Zin = Rin + 1/(jωC)，反馈阻抗 Zf = Rf。对反相端写 KCL 得 Vin/Zin + Vout/Rf = 0。因此 H(jω) = −3·jωRinC/(1 + jωRinC)。

  1. 高频时电容阻抗趋近于零，H → −Rf/Rin = −3，故通带增益幅值是 3.00 V/V。
  2. 截止时 ωRinC = 1，故 fc = 1/[2π(10 kΩ)(20 nF)] ≈ 796 Hz。
  3. 截止处 |H| = 3/√2，故增益为 20 log₁₀(3/√2) ≈ +6.53 dB。通带平台为 20 log₁₀(3) ≈ +9.54 dB。
  4. 截止时 H = −3j/(1 + j) = −1.5 − j1.5，所以相位是 −135°。
  5. 低频时 |H| ≈ 3f/fc。频率增大 10 倍时增益增大 20 dB。画渐近线时，从低频以 +20 dB/decade 上升，到 796 Hz 后接 +9.54 dB 水平线。

原创题，参考 ENGR 202 HW 8 第 2 题的有源滤波与 Bode 图考点。
