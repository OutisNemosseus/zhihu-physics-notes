Combine Two Parallel Loads by Complex Power

An ideal source Vₛ = 100∠0° V RMS supplies the two parallel loads shown. At the operating frequency, Load 1 absorbs 600 W at a power factor of 0.800 lagging. Load 2 absorbs S₂ = 400 − j150 VA. Assume sinusoidal steady state and ideal wires. Iₛ flows from the source into the loads.

Show your work. First find the reactive power of Load 1. Add the two complex powers, then use the total to find Iₛ. State on paper whether the combined power factor is leading or lagging. Enter numerical answers to three significant figures.

展开中文推导

负载 1 的视在功率幅值 |S₁| = P₁/PF₁ = 600/0.800 = 750 VA。Q₁ = +√(750² − 600²) = +450 var，正号来自 lagging。于是 S₁ = 600 + j450 VA。

  1. 复功率相加，Stotal = S₁ + S₂ = (600 + j450) + (400 − j150) = 1000 + j300 VA。实部为总平均功率 P = 1000 W。
  2. 虚部为总无功功率 Q = +300 var。
  3. 对负载采用被动符号约定，Stotal = VₛIₛ*，所以 Iₛ = (Stotal/Vₛ)* = (1000 − j300)/100 = 10 − j3 A。幅值为 √(10² + 3²) = √109 ≈ 10.4 A RMS。
  4. ∠Iₛ = arctan(−3/10) ≈ −16.7°。电流滞后源电压。
  5. |Stotal| = √(1000² + 300²) ≈ 1044.03 VA，故 PF = 1000/1044.03 ≈ 0.958 lagging。

也可用 KCL 检查。I₁ = (600 − j450)/100 = 6 − j4.5 A，I₂ = (400 + j150)/100 = 4 + j1.5 A，两者相加为 10 − j3 A。

原创题，参考 HW 5 的复功率平衡练习，结合两个并联负载的功率因数与电流相位。
