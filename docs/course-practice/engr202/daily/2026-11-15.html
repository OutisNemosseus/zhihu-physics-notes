Maximum Power with Two Load Constraints

An ideal sinusoidal source has a voltage phasor of 24∠0° V peak. At its operating frequency, the source drives a passive adjustable load ZL through the fixed series impedance shown. The circuit is in sinusoidal steady state.

Show your work. Write the load's average power in terms of its resistance and reactance. Optimize it under each load constraint and justify the optimum. Enter numerical answers in the units shown.

展开中文推导

先把源电压换成 RMS：24/√2 V，所以 |Vs,RMS|² = 288 V²。令负载为 RL + jXL，串联总阻抗为 (8 + RL) + j(6 + XL)。于是负载平均功率 PL = |IRMS|²RL = 288RL/[(8 + RL)² + (6 + XL)²]。

  1. 负载的电抗可调时，先令 6 + XL = 0，让分母最小。此时对 RL 求导，导数为 288(8 − RL)/(8 + RL)³，在 RL = 8 Ω 处由正转负。
  2. 刚才抵消串联电抗所需的负载电抗为 −6 Ω。最佳负载是 8 − j6 Ω，即源串联阻抗的复共轭。
  3. 代入总阻抗 16 Ω，Pmax = 288 × 8/16² = 9 W。
  4. 纯电阻负载固定 XL = 0。将分母展开为 RL² + 16RL + 100，求导后分子为 288(100 − RL²)，分母为原分母的平方。导数在 RL = 10 Ω 处由正转负。
  5. 纯电阻的最大平均功率为 288 × 10/(18² + 6²) = 8 W。
