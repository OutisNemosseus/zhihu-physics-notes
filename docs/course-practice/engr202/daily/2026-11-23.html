Balanced Wye Load with Line Impedance

A balanced positive-sequence three-phase source supplies a balanced wye-connected load. The source line-to-line voltage magnitude is 120√3 V RMS. Choose Vₐₙ = 120∠0° V RMS as the phase reference. Each line has impedance 1 + j1 Ω, and each load phase has impedance 11 + j8 Ω. The source and load star points are joined by an ideal neutral conductor. All values apply at the operating frequency.

Show your work. Write the phase-a KVL equation, calculate the voltage across the load phase, and distinguish the load's average power from the losses in the three lines. Enter numerical answers to three significant figures.

展开中文推导

平衡 Y 接负载中，线电流等于对应支路电流。每相 KVL 为 Vₐₙ = Iₐ(Zline + Zload)。总串联阻抗是 (1 + j1) + (11 + j8) = 12 + j9 = 15∠36.87° Ω。

  1. Iₐ = 120∠0°/(15∠36.87°) = 8∠−36.87° A RMS，幅值为 8.00 A。
  2. 电流相角为 0° − 36.87° ≈ −36.9°。
  3. 负载相电压是 IₐZload，幅值为 8√(11² + 8²) = 8√185 ≈ 109 V RMS。直角坐标验证，Iₐ = 6.4 − j4.8 A，负载相电压为 (6.4 − j4.8)(11 + j8) = 108.8 − j1.6 V。
  4. 三相负载总平均功率为 3|Iₐ|²Re(Zload) = 3 × 64 × 11 = 2112 W，三位有效数字为 2.11 × 10³ W。
  5. 三条线路的平均损耗为 3|Iₐ|²Re(Zline) = 3 × 64 × 1 = 192 W。

功率平衡检查，源输出总平均功率为 3Re(VₐₙIₐ*) = 3 × 120 × 6.4 = 2304 W，等于 2112 + 192 W。

原创题，参考 HW 9 的平衡 Y 负载练习，加入每相线路阻抗和损耗计算。
