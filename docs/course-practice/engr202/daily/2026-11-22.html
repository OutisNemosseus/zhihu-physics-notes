Design an Inverting Active Low-Pass Filter

The op amp in the circuit below is ideal and operates in its linear region. Design the feedback network for a low-frequency gain magnitude of 4.00 V/V and a cutoff frequency of 1.00 kHz. The input resistor is 10.0 kΩ. The output is unloaded.

Show your work. Derive H(jω) = Vout/Vin from the parallel feedback impedance. Solve for Rf and Cf. Use your ideal design values at 2.00 kHz and show the complex-number calculation used to find the output phase. Enter numerical answers to three significant figures.

展开中文推导

反馈电阻与电容并联，Zf = [1/Rf + jωCf]⁻¹ = Rf/(1 + jωRfCf)。由理想反相端的 KCL 得 H(jω) = −Zf/Rin = −(Rf/Rin)/(1 + jωRfCf)。

  1. 低频时 H → −Rf/Rin。增益幅值要求为 4，所以 Rf = 4 × 10.0 kΩ = 40.0 kΩ。
  2. 截止频率 fc = 1/(2πRfCf)，因此 Cf = 1/[2π(1000)(40000)] F ≈ 3.98 nF。
  3. 在 2.00 kHz 时 f/fc = 2，H = −4/(1 + j2) = −0.8 + j1.6。所以 |H| = 4/√5 ≈ 1.79 V/V。
  4. |Vout| = |H| × 0.500 = 2/√5 ≈ 0.894 V RMS。
  5. H 的实部为负、虚部为正，位于第二象限。题目要求主值相角，因此 ∠H = 180° − arctan(2) ≈ +116.6°，三位有效数字为 +117°。写成 −243.4° 表示相同相位，但本题按要求输入 (−180°, +180°] 内的值。
