HW 1 · amplitude convention and first-cycle events · 01

Interpret a sine with a negative coefficient

The voltage is \(v(t)=-6\sin(800\pi t-\pi/3)\;\mathrm V\), with t in seconds.

  1. Find the positive peak amplitude, angular frequency, frequency and period. Convert to a positive-amplitude cosine and give its peak phasor.
  2. Find v(0), the first downward zero crossing, first minimum, and first positive maximum at or after t=0. Give event times in ms.
  3. Sketch one complete period on labeled axes and mark all three events in time order. Explain why the coefficient −6 is not a negative peak magnitude.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(-\sin\theta=\cos(\theta+\pi/2)\), \(f=\omega/(2\pi)\), \(T=1/f\).
中文提示（不含解答）

负号可用半圈相位变化吸收；把表达式统一成正振幅余弦再找时刻。

HW 1 · combine two time shifts · 02

An advance followed by a larger delay

Let \(x(t)=4\cos(1500t+\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\), \(y(t)=x(t+\pi/6000\;\mathrm s)\), and \(z(t)=y(t-\pi/3000\;\mathrm s)\).

  1. Find the period and both stated time shifts in ms and as fractions of the period. Describe each shift as an advance or delay.
  2. Write y(t) and z(t) as positive-amplitude cosines and give all three cosine-reference phasors. Find the single net time shift relating z to x.
  3. Compare x,y,z at t=0 and t=π/3000 s. Draw the three phasors to scale and label their angles.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(T=2\pi/\omega\); replacing t by \(t+\Delta\) adds \(\omega\Delta\) to phase, and replacing t by \(t-\Delta\) subtracts it.
中文提示（不含解答）

先逐次代入时间变量，再化简总相位；第二次移动是作用在 y 上。

HW 1 · complex arithmetic and reciprocal checks · 03

A third-quadrant product and a first-quadrant quotient

Let \(a=-4+j3\) and \(b=2+j5\).

  1. Compute a+b, ab, a/b and 1/b in rectangular form.
  2. Convert a,b,ab and a/b to polar form, with positive magnitudes and phases in (−180°,180°].
  3. Verify (a/b)b=a and b(1/b)=1. Plot a and b with equal axis scales, and use the rectangular signs to check product and quotient quadrants.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(j^2=-1\), \(|u+jv|=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}\), \(\arg z=\operatorname{atan2}(\Im z,\Re z)\), \(1/b=\bar b/|b|^2\).
中文提示（不含解答）

相位要用 atan2 或结合象限判断；先保留分数可以减少舍入误差。

HW 1 · phasor subtraction with different phase references · 04

Subtract a cosine from a shifted sine

The voltage is \(v(t)=9\sin(600t+\pi/3)-4\cos(600t+\pi/3)\;\mathrm V\).

  1. Convert both written components to cosine-reference phasors. Show the subtraction as a rectangular sum, with the minus sign included exactly once.
  2. Find the combined peak magnitude and principal phase, then write one positive-amplitude cosine. Check the original and combined forms at t=0 and t=π/1200 s.
  3. Draw a tip-to-tail diagram with equal real/imaginary scales. Explain why the two written phase constants being equal does not make their phasors point in the same direction.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\sin\theta=\cos(\theta-\pi/2)\), \(V=M(\cos\phi+j\sin\phi)\), \(v(t)=\Re\{Ve^{j\omega t}\}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

相同的书面相位不代表正弦与余弦相量同向；正弦需要额外减去 90°。

HW 1 · use a fixed magnitude and an unknown phase · 05

Choose a phase that leaves a positive imaginary result

Let \(v(t)=6\cos(750t)+10\cos(750t+\phi)\;\mathrm V\), with \(\phi\) restricted to (0°,180°). Choose phi so that the total cosine-reference phasor is purely imaginary with positive imaginary part.

  1. Write the total real and imaginary components in terms of phi. Find phi in degrees to three decimals and explain why the stated interval selects a unique answer.
  2. Find the resulting phasor and express v(t) as a positive-amplitude cosine and as a signed sine. Check v(0) and v(π/1500 s).
  3. Draw the component and total phasors to scale. Verify that the second component retains its specified magnitude after the real component cancels.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V=6+10(\cos\phi+j\sin\phi)\), \(\sin^2\phi+\cos^2\phi=1\). Convert degrees to radians when evaluating trigonometric functions.
中文提示（不含解答）

未知量是相位；实部抵消后，还需要用给定角度范围选择虚部正号。

HW 2 · series RLC with KVL and time checks · 06

An inductive loop produces a negative imaginary current

The source \(v_s(t)=18\cos(1250t-\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\) drives series R=12 Ω, L=16 mH, C=100 μF. Current is clockwise from the source positive terminal. Each element voltage is a passive drop along that direction.

  1. Copy the time-domain circuit and draw its impedance version, including source phasor. Find ZL,ZC,total Z and I in rectangular/polar form.
  2. Write steady-state i(t). Calculate VR,VL,VC and verify KVL. State the current phase relative to the source.
  3. Predict i(0) and i(π/2500 s). For optional LTspice, provide source frequency in Hz and sine-source phase, then compare these phase positions in a settled cycle with units, appropriate scales and two cursor readings.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\ Z_L=j\omega L,\ Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\), \(I=V_s/(R+Z_L+Z_C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

源相位与阻抗相位都要保留；从源相位减去阻抗相位得到电流相位。

HW 2 · staged reduction and node checks · 07

An input inductor precedes a parallel RC block

At ω=1250 rad/s, terminal A connects through L=4.8 mH to node X. R=16 Ω and C=50 μF each connect X to terminal B. B is reference. Apply VAB=17∠0° V peak. Input current points A to X; branch currents point X to B.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit. Reduce the parallel block and then the input impedance ZAB, giving the latter in rectangular and polar form.
  2. Find input current, VX, resistor current and capacitor current, all as complex phasors.
  3. Find the inductor drop from A to X. Check KCL and VAB=VL+VX, and state whether input current leads or lags the applied voltage.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=(1/R+j\omega C)^{-1}\), \(Z_{AB}=j\omega L+Z_p\), \(I=V_{AB}/Z_{AB}\), \(V_X=IZ_p\).
中文提示（不含解答）

输入电感在并联块之外；总输入阻抗与节点电压所对应的阻抗不要混淆。

HW 2 · current-source node with a series RL branch · 08

A cosine-equivalent current drives two unequal branches

The source \(i_s(t)=0.75\sin(1600t+\pi/2)\;\mathrm A\) injects current upward into node A. One A-to-ground branch is R₁=32 Ω. The other is R₂=16 Ω followed by L=10 mH in series. Define vA positive at A, and both branch currents downward.

  1. Copy the circuit and draw the impedance version. Convert the source to a cosine-reference peak phasor; find branch impedances, total admittance and equivalent impedance.
  2. Find VA in rectangular/polar form and write vA(t). Compute both branch-current phasors and verify KCL.
  3. Predict vA(0) and vA(π/3200 s). State the voltage phase relative to the source current; optionally compare both samples after transients settle in a simulation, with reference directions and units.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Y=1/R_1+1/(R_2+j\omega L)\), \(V_A=I_s/Y\), \(I_1=V_A/R_1\), \(I_2=V_A/(R_2+j\omega L)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

串联 RL 分支先求整体阻抗，再求该分支导纳；源的正弦表达式也要转换。

HW 2 · impedance divider and passive signs · 09

Measure the resistor after a series capacitor

The source \(v_s(t)=26\cos(1250t+\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\) connects through C=80 μF to node A. R=24 Ω connects A to ground; the source negative terminal is grounded. Current is clockwise from source through C and R. Output vo=vA−Vground is positive at A.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit. Find input impedance and I, then use an impedance divider to find Vo in rectangular/polar form.
  2. Write vo(t). Calculate the capacitor drop VC along the current direction and verify Vs=VC+Vo. State whether output leads or lags the input.
  3. Predict vo(0) and vo(π/2500 s). Explain why the output amplitude is not 26 V even though the resistor is the only dissipative element in this ideal circuit.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\), \(I=V_s/(R+Z_C)\), \(V_o=V_sR/(R+Z_C)\), \(V_C=IZ_C\).
中文提示（不含解答）

电容仍有非零相量压降；分压的分母是整个串联阻抗。

HW 2 · loaded divider with a third-quadrant output · 10

A sine source supplies a loaded RC divider

The source \(v_s(t)=25\sin(1600t)\;\mathrm V\) connects through R₁=12.5 Ω to node A. R₂=25 Ω and C=25 μF each connect A to ground. Output vo is positive at A. Source current flows through R₁ toward A; load currents are downward.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit with source phasor. Find the parallel load impedance, total input impedance and source current.
  2. Find Vo in rectangular/polar form and write vo(t). Compute the load currents and R₁ drop, then check KCL and KVL.
  3. Predict vo(0) and vo(π/3200 s). For optional LTspice, give frequency in Hz and sine-source phase; compare both values in a late steady-state cycle with reasonable scales, units and cursor readings.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=(1/R_2+j\omega C)^{-1}\), \(I_s=V_s/(R_1+Z_p)\), \(V_o=I_sZ_p\), \(I_R=V_o/R_2\), \(I_C=j\omega CV_o\).
中文提示（不含解答）

输出相量位于第三象限时，主相位应为负角而不是直接取反正切的锐角。
