HW 1 · cycle timing and labeled axes · 01

Order the events of a phase-lagging waveform

A sinusoidal voltage has peak amplitude 6 V, frequency 200 Hz and cosine-reference phase −120°. Use t in seconds.

  1. Find angular frequency, period and the peak phasor. Write v(t) using radians.
  2. Calculate v(0), the first upward zero crossing, first positive maximum, first downward zero crossing and first minimum at or after t = 0. Report times in ms.
  3. Sketch one period on axes labeled in ms and V, marking those events in chronological order.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\omega=2\pi f\), \(T=1/f\). Upward and downward cosine zero crossings occur at phases \(-\pi/2+2k\pi\) and \(\pi/2+2k\pi\).
中文提示（不含解答）

把相位先转成弧度；上升过零点与下降过零点对应不同的余弦角度。

HW 1 · reference conversion and time shift · 02

Advance a negative sine without losing its sign

Let \(x(t)=-4\sin(600t-\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\) and \(y(t)=x(t+\pi/3600\;\mathrm s)\).

  1. Write positive-amplitude cosine expressions and principal-phase peak phasors for x and y.
  2. Find the time advance in ms and as a fraction of the period. State the phase change.
  3. Compare x(0), y(0), x(T/4) and y(T/4). Plot the two phasors with equal real/imaginary scales.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(-\sin\theta=\cos(\theta+\pi/2)\), \(T=2\pi/\omega\). A time advance \(\Delta t\) adds phase \(\omega\Delta t\).
中文提示（不含解答）

负号也可以写成相位变化；先统一成正幅值余弦，再处理提前量。

HW 1 · complex arithmetic and quadrants · 03

Use a quotient to uncover a complex-number relation

Let \(a=-5+j12\) and \(b=2+j3\).

  1. Find a − b, ab and a/b in rectangular form.
  2. Give a, b, ab and a/b in polar form, using positive magnitudes and phases in (−180°,180°].
  3. Check (a/b)b = a and determine whether a = b². Plot a and b on equal-scale complex axes.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(j^2=-1\), \(|u+jv|=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}\), \(\arg z=\operatorname{atan2}(\Im z,\Re z)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

实部为负、虚部为正的数在第二象限；除法可乘分母的共轭进行检查。

HW 1 · subtraction in the phasor plane · 04

Subtract a sine from a lagging cosine

The voltage is \(v(t)=6\cos(900t-\pi/3)-4\sin(900t)\;\mathrm V\).

  1. Convert both signed terms to cosine-reference peak phasors and add them in rectangular form.
  2. Find the total magnitude and principal phase, and write a single positive-amplitude cosine. Check both forms at t = 0 and t = π/1800 s.
  3. Draw the signed component phasors tip-to-tail with equal axis scales. Explain the imaginary sign contributed by −4 sin(900t).
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\sin\theta=\cos(\theta-\pi/2)\), \(v(t)=\Re\{Ve^{j\omega t}\}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

注意负的正弦对应正的虚部；先处理外面的减号，再相加。

HW 1 · reconstruct amplitude and phase · 05

Recover a phasor from two quarter-cycle samples

A zero-offset voltage is \(v(t)=M\cos(1000t+\phi)\;\mathrm V\), with M > 0. Measurements give v(0) = −6 V and v(π/2000 s) = −8 V.

  1. Use the two measurements to determine the rectangular peak phasor, M and principal phase φ.
  2. Write v(t) and predict v(π/1000 s) and v(3π/2000 s). Check all four quarter-cycle samples.
  3. Find the first positive maximum at or after t = 0, in ms, and plot the phasor in the correct quadrant.
Reference / 必要约定与公式If \(V=a+jb\), then \(v(t)=a\cos\omega t-b\sin\omega t\). \(M=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\), \(\phi=\operatorname{atan2}(b,a)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

四分之一周期处的电压等于相量虚部的相反数；最大值对应总相位为整圈。

HW 2 · series RLC and complete phasor circuit · 06

A source phase that makes the series current purely real

The source \(v_s(t)=16\sqrt2\cos(2000t+\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\) drives series R = 8 Ω, L = 6 mH and C = 125 μF. Current is clockwise from the source positive terminal. Element voltages are passive drops in that direction.

  1. Copy the time-domain circuit and draw its impedance version, including the source phasor. Show ZL, ZC and total Z. Find I in rectangular and polar form.
  2. Write steady-state i(t), calculate VR, VL and VC, and check KVL. Compare current phase with source voltage phase.
  3. Predict i(0) and i(π/4000 s). For optional LTspice, give frequency in Hz and sine-source phase; compare these values at corresponding points in a late steady-state cycle, showing units, cursor values and current reference direction.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\ Z_L=j\omega L,\ Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\), \(I=V_s/(R+Z_L+Z_C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

源和总阻抗的相位都要保留；两者相减后电流可能恰好为零相位。

HW 2 · stepwise series-parallel impedance · 07

Reduce a series resistor and two unlike branches

At ω = 1000 rad/s, terminal A connects through R₁ = 10 Ω to node X. One X-to-B branch is L = 20 mH. The other is R₂ = 20 Ω in series with C = 50 μF. Terminal B is reference. Apply VAB = 26∠0° V peak; input current flows A to X and branch currents X to B.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit and show each reduction: series RC branch, parallel block, then total ZAB. Give total impedance in rectangular and polar form.
  2. Find input current and VX. Find both branch-current phasors.
  3. Verify KCL at X and VAB = VR1 + VX. State whether input current leads or lags the applied voltage.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_b=R_2+Z_C\), \(Z_p=(1/Z_L+1/Z_b)^{-1}\), \(Z_{AB}=R_1+Z_p\), \(V_X=IZ_p\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先把 R₂ 与 C 串联，再与 L 并联，最后加上 R₁；不要把三条阻抗直接相加。

HW 2 · current-source node and three branches · 08

Cancel reactive branch currents at one given frequency

The source \(i_s(t)=0.40\sin(2000t)\;\mathrm A\) injects current upward into node A. R = 25 Ω, L = 5 mH and C = 50 μF are separate parallel branches from A to ground. Define vA positive at A and all three branch currents downward.

  1. Copy the time-domain circuit and draw the impedance circuit with the cosine-reference source phasor. Find total admittance and equivalent impedance.
  2. Find VA and vA(t), then IR, IL and IC. Verify KCL and explain which branch currents cancel at this frequency.
  3. Calculate vA(0) and vA(π/4000 s). For optional simulation, compare these two values in a late steady-state cycle with labeled axes and cursor readings, using the stated reference directions.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Y=1/R+1/(j\omega L)+j\omega C\), \(V_A=I_s/Y\), \(I_R=V_A/R\), \(I_L=V_A/(j\omega L)\), \(I_C=j\omega CV_A\).
中文提示（不含解答）

正弦源统一成余弦参考；并联网络相加的是导纳，支路电流不能只加大小。

HW 2 · voltage division and output phase · 09

Take the output across a grounded inductor

The source \(v_s(t)=20\cos(4000t)\;\mathrm V\) connects through R = 12 Ω to node A. L = 4 mH connects from A to ground, which also joins the source negative terminal. Output vo is positive at A relative to ground. Current flows from the source through R and L.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit and find total impedance and current in rectangular/polar form.
  2. Use voltage division to find Vo in rectangular/polar form and write vo(t). Compute VR and verify Vs = VR + Vo.
  3. Predict vo(0) and vo(π/8000 s). Compare the phases of current, source voltage and output voltage.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(I=V_s/(R+j\omega L)\), \(V_o=V_s(j\omega L)/(R+j\omega L)\), \(V_R=IR\).
中文提示（不含解答）

输出跨在电感上，分压的分子必须是 jωL；输出不一定与电流同相。

HW 2 · source conversion, divider and branch checks · 10

Keep a shifted sine through a loaded RC divider

The source \(v_s(t)=15\sin(1000t+\pi)\;\mathrm V\) connects through R₁ = 5 Ω to node A. R₂ = 10 Ω and C = 100 μF each connect from A to ground. Output vo is positive at A. Source current flows through R₁ toward A; both load currents are downward.

  1. Find the cosine-reference source phasor, parallel-load impedance, total input impedance and source-current phasor.
  2. Find Vo in rectangular/polar form and write vo(t). Find load currents and the R₁ drop, checking KCL and KVL.
  3. Predict vo(0) and vo(π/2000 s). Give the frequency in Hz and sine-source phase for optional LTspice; compare the two points after transients settle with correct units, reasonable plot scale and cursor values.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=(1/R_2+j\omega C)^{-1}\), \(I_s=V_s/(R_1+Z_p)\), \(V_o=I_sZ_p\), \(I_R=V_o/R_2\), \(I_C=j\omega CV_o\).
中文提示（不含解答）

正弦相位减去 90° 后再做分压；电容支路的电流相位比该支路电压超前 90°。
