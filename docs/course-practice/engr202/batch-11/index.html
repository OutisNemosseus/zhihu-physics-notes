HW 1 · frequency and phase in compatible units · 01

Find the first events of a phase-leading voltage

A zero-offset sinusoidal voltage has peak amplitude 9 V, frequency 125 Hz, and cosine-reference phase +60°. Use t in seconds.

  1. Find angular frequency and period, and write the voltage as a positive-amplitude cosine with phase in radians. Give its peak phasor.
  2. Find v(0), the first downward zero crossing, first minimum, and first positive maximum at or after t = 0. Report event times in ms.
  3. Sketch one cycle on labeled time/voltage axes and mark those events in chronological order.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\omega=2\pi f\), \(T=1/f\). Cosine phases for a maximum, downward zero and minimum are \(2k\pi\), \(\pi/2+2k\pi\) and \(\pi+2k\pi\), respectively.
中文提示（不含解答）

已有正相位会让某些事件提早出现；求“首次”事件时要选择非负的时间。

HW 1 · phase wrapping and sine-to-cosine conversion · 02

Recognize the same signal before applying a delay

Let \(x(t)=3\cos(400t-7\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\), \(y(t)=3\sin(400t+3\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\), and \(z(t)=x(t-\pi/1600\;\mathrm s)\).

  1. Decide whether x and y are identical for every t. Write cosine-reference peak phasors for x, y and z with principal phases.
  2. Find the delay applied to x in ms and as a fraction of the common period. State its phase change.
  3. Compare x(0), z(0), x(T/4) and z(T/4), and sketch the phasors on equal-scale complex axes.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\sin\theta=\cos(\theta-\pi/2)\), \(T=2\pi/\omega\), delay \(\Delta t\) changes phase by \(-\omega\Delta t\).
中文提示（不含解答）

相位加减整圈不改变波形；统一成余弦参考后，再处理时间延迟。

HW 1 · rectangular and polar arithmetic · 03

Different magnitudes can still cancel product phase

Let \(a=3+j4\) and \(b=6-j8\).

  1. Calculate a + b, ab and a/b in rectangular form.
  2. Convert a, b, ab and a/b to polar form with positive magnitudes and principal phases in (−180°,180°].
  3. Check (a/b)b = a. Explain why ab is a positive real number even though a and b are not conjugates of each other, and plot a and b to scale.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(j^2=-1\), \(|u+jv|=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}\), \(\arg z=\operatorname{atan2}(\Im z,\Re z)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

比较 b 与 a 的共轭之间的比例；乘法相位相加，除法相位相减。

HW 1 · sum after reference conversion · 04

Add a cosine to a shifted sine

The total voltage is \(v(t)=4\cos(1200t)+8\sin(1200t+\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\).

  1. Write both peak phasors with a cosine reference and find their sum in rectangular and polar form.
  2. Write one positive-amplitude cosine for v(t). Verify the original and combined forms at t = 0 and t = π/2400 s.
  3. Draw a tip-to-tail addition plot with equal real/imaginary scales in volts. Explain which sign the shifted sine contributes to the total imaginary component.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\sin\theta=\cos(\theta-\pi/2)\), \(M\angle\phi=M(\cos\phi+j\sin\phi)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先把正弦的相位减去 90°；原式中的加号不代表相量的虚部一定为正。

HW 1 · rectangular cancellation and positive magnitude · 05

Choose an amplitude to remove the sine component

Consider \(v(t)=A\cos(500t+\pi/6)+4\sin(500t)\;\mathrm V\), where the unknown real amplitude A is nonnegative. The desired total is a positive multiple of \(\cos(500t)\), with no sine term.

  1. Express the total cosine-reference phasor in terms of A and find the required A.
  2. Give the resulting total phasor and single-cosine expression. Check v(0) and v(π/1000 s).
  3. Draw the two selected component phasors and their sum. Explain how the cancellation condition differs from setting the total amplitude to zero.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V=A(\cos(\pi/6)+j\sin(\pi/6))-j4\). A positive pure cosine has a positive real phasor.
中文提示（不含解答）

先让相量虚部为零，再检查剩下的实部是否为正。

HW 2 · RLC and KVL · 06

Track three series voltage drops with a lagging source phase

The source \(v_s(t)=30\cos(2000t-\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\) drives series R = 15 Ω, L = 10 mH and C = 100 μF. Current is clockwise from the source positive terminal; element voltages are passive drops along that current.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit. Find source, total-impedance and current phasors in rectangular and polar form.
  2. Write i(t), find VR, VL and VC, and check that they sum to Vs. State whether current leads or lags source voltage and by how much.
  3. Predict i(0) and i(π/4000 s) and check their signs from the time-domain expression.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\ Z_L=j\omega L,\ Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\), \(I=V_s/(R+Z_L+Z_C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

电流的相位是源相位减去总阻抗相位；各元件电压都按同一被动符号约定计算。

HW 2 · series capacitor and parallel RL section · 07

A reactive interior can give a real input impedance

At ω = 1000 rad/s, terminal A connects through C = 50 μF to node X. R = 40 Ω and L = 40 mH each connect from X to terminal B. Apply VAB = 20∠−30° V peak. Define input current from A to X, both branch currents from X to B, and B as the voltage reference.

  1. Find each component impedance, the parallel equivalent ZXB, and total ZAB. Give the total in rectangular and polar form.
  2. Find input current and VX relative to B. Explain whether the source voltage and input current are in phase.
  3. Find both load currents and the capacitor drop from A to X. Verify KCL and VAB = VC + VX.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=(1/R+1/(j\omega L))^{-1}\), \(Z_{AB}=Z_C+Z_p\), \(I=V_{AB}/Z_{AB}\), \(V_X=IZ_p\).
中文提示（不含解答）

输入端的总虚部为零，并不意味着每个内部电压或支路电流都与输入同相。

HW 2 · a resistor in parallel with series RC · 08

Keep the current-source phase in a two-branch node

The source \(i_s(t)=0.60\cos(800t+\pi/2)\;\mathrm A\) injects current upward into node A. One branch from A to ground is R₁ = 20 Ω. A second branch is R₂ = 10 Ω followed by C = 125 μF in series. Define vA positive at A and both branch currents downward.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit, find total admittance and equivalent impedance, and state the source phasor.
  2. Find VA in rectangular/polar form and write vA(t). Find both branch-current phasors and verify KCL.
  3. Evaluate vA(0) and vA(π/1600 s). Explain why the second branch can have a negative real current component without having a negative phasor magnitude.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Y=1/R_1+1/(R_2+Z_C)\), \(V_A=I_s/Y\), \(I_1=V_A/R_1\), \(I_2=V_A/(R_2+Z_C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

支路的串联阻抗要先合并，再与另一支路相加导纳；保留源相量的 +90°。

HW 2 · resistive output divider · 09

Read a grounded resistor output in a three-element loop

The source \(v_s(t)=20\cos(1000t-\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\) drives a series loop in this order: L = 10 mH, C = 50 μF, and R = 10 Ω. The lower end of R and source negative terminal are grounded. Node A is between C and R; vo is measured from A to ground with A positive. Current follows the loop from the positive source terminal through L, C and R.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit and calculate the source phasor, total impedance, and current.
  2. Find Vo by voltage division and write vo(t). Calculate VL and VC, and verify Vs = VL + VC + Vo.
  3. Predict vo(0) and vo(π/2000 s). Compare the phases of source voltage, current and resistor output.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V_o=V_sR/(j\omega L+1/(j\omega C)+R)\), \(I=V_s/Z_{total}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

输出跨在电阻上，分子只用 R；电感和电容仍都在分压的分母里。

HW 2 · final KCL, KVL and time checks · 10

A loaded RL divider driven by an equivalent cosine

The source \(v_s(t)=15\sin(2000t+\pi/2)\;\mathrm V\) connects through R₁ = 6 Ω to node A. R₂ = 12 Ω and L = 6 mH each connect from A to ground. Output vo is positive at A. Source current flows through R₁ toward A, and load currents are downward.

  1. Find the cosine-reference source phasor, parallel-load impedance, total input impedance and source-current phasor.
  2. Find Vo in rectangular/polar form and write vo(t). Calculate both load currents and the R₁ drop; verify KCL and KVL.
  3. Predict vo(0) and vo(π/4000 s). For optional LTspice, give frequency in Hz and sine-source phase. Compare these two points in a late steady-state cycle after transients settle, with labeled units and reference directions.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=(1/R_2+1/(j\omega L))^{-1}\), \(I_s=V_s/(R_1+Z_p)\), \(V_o=I_sZ_p\), \(I_R=V_o/R_2\), \(I_L=V_o/(j\omega L)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先识别给定正弦是否能简化为零相位余弦；并联负载的两条支路共享同一输出电压。
