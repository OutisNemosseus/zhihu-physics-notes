HW 1 · phase conversion and first-cycle events · 01

Read a leading sine using a cosine reference

The voltage is \(v(t)=8\sin(1000\pi t+\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\), with t in seconds.

  1. Find peak amplitude, angular frequency, frequency and period. Write the equivalent positive-amplitude cosine and its peak phasor.
  2. Calculate v(0), the first positive maximum, first downward zero crossing and first minimum at or after t=0. Give event times in ms.
  3. Sketch one period on labeled time/voltage axes and mark the calculated events in chronological order.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\sin\theta=\cos(\theta-\pi/2)\), \(f=\omega/(2\pi)\), \(T=1/f\).
中文提示（不含解答）

正弦转余弦时相位减去 90°；求事件时间时选第一个非负结果。

HW 1 · a time delay followed by a sign reversal · 02

Invert a delayed cosine and keep both phase changes

Let \(x(t)=5\cos(1200t-2\pi/3)\;\mathrm V\) and \(y(t)=-x(t-\pi/3600\;\mathrm s)\).

  1. Find the peak phasor of the delayed signal before the minus sign is applied, then X and Y with positive magnitudes and principal phases.
  2. Find the delay in ms and as a fraction of the period. Account separately for the phase change from the delay and from inversion.
  3. Write y(t) as a positive-amplitude cosine. Compare x(0), y(0), x(T/4), y(T/4), and draw X and Y to scale.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(T=2\pi/\omega\), delay \(\Delta t\) changes phase by \(-\omega\Delta t\), and \(-M\cos\theta=M\cos(\theta+\pi)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

外面的负号不是时间移动；它额外带来半圈相位变化。

HW 1 · rectangular calculations with explicit quadrants · 03

A quotient and a product both enter quadrant two

Let \(a=2-j5\) and \(b=-3+j1\).

  1. Compute a − b, ab, a/b and 1/b in rectangular form.
  2. Convert a, b, ab and a/b to polar form with positive magnitudes and phases in (−180°,180°].
  3. Verify (a/b)b = a and b(1/b) = 1. Plot a and b to scale; explain the quadrants of the product and quotient from their rectangular signs.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(j^2=-1\), \(|u+jv|=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}\), \(\arg z=\operatorname{atan2}(\Im z,\Re z)\), \(1/b=\overline b/|b|^2\).
中文提示（不含解答）

相位主值必须配合复平面象限；乘法和除法最后都可以用直角坐标复核。

HW 1 · a shifted sine added to a quadrature cosine · 04

A small negative real part changes the phase quadrant

The voltage is \(v(t)=7\cos(400t+\pi/2)+3\sin(400t-\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\).

  1. Write the two cosine-reference peak phasors and add them in rectangular form.
  2. Find the total positive magnitude and principal phase, then write v(t) as one cosine. Check the original and combined forms at t=0 and t=π/800 s.
  3. Draw a tip-to-tail plot with equal real/imaginary scales and explain why a one-argument atan of imaginary/real would select the wrong principal quadrant here.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\sin\theta=\cos(\theta-\pi/2)\), \(V=M(\cos\phi+j\sin\phi)\), and \(\phi=\operatorname{atan2}(\Im V,\Re V)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先把正弦相位减去 90°；实部很小也不能忽略其正负号。

HW 1 · real-part cancellation · 05

Choose an amplitude for a purely imaginary total

Consider \(v(t)=A\cos(1000t+\pi/3)+3\cos(1000t+\pi)\;\mathrm V\), where A is a nonnegative real amplitude. Choose A so that the cosine-reference total phasor is purely imaginary with positive imaginary part.

  1. Express the total rectangular phasor in terms of A and determine the required A.
  2. Give the resulting phasor, positive-amplitude cosine expression, and equivalent signed-sine expression. Check v(0) and v(π/2000 s).
  3. Plot both component phasors and their sum to scale. Explain why a positive imaginary phasor gives a negative sine term under the stated cosine convention.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V=A(\cos(\pi/3)+j\sin(\pi/3))-3\), \(v(t)=\Re\{Ve^{j\omega t}\}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

这次要让实部抵消，而不是让虚部抵消；时域重构使用实部取值的约定。

HW 2 · series RLC, phase and KVL · 06

A capacitive loop gives a positive imaginary current

The source \(v_s(t)=24\cos(1000t+\pi/4)\;\mathrm V\) drives series R=12 Ω, L=8 mH and C=50 μF. Current is clockwise from the source positive terminal; element voltages are passive drops in that direction.

  1. Copy the time-domain circuit and draw its impedance circuit, including the source phasor. Find ZL, ZC, total Z and I in rectangular/polar form.
  2. Write steady-state i(t), calculate VR, VL and VC, and verify KVL. State whether current leads or lags the source and by how much.
  3. Predict i(0) and i(π/2000 s). For optional LTspice, give frequency in Hz and sine-source phase, then compare both phase positions after transients settle with units, cursor readings and the stated current direction.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\ Z_L=j\omega L,\ Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\), \(I=V_s/(R+Z_L+Z_C)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

电容与电感先合并带符号的虚部；电流相位等于源相位减去总阻抗相位。

HW 2 · staged series-parallel reduction · 07

Reduce a resistor feeding a resistor-and-RC split

At ω=1000 rad/s, terminal A connects through R₁=4 Ω to node X. One X-to-B branch is R₂=20 Ω; the other is R₃=10 Ω followed by C=100 μF in series. B is reference. Apply VAB=20∠0° V peak. Input current flows A to X; branch currents flow X to B.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit. Show the series RC branch, parallel block and total ZAB reductions, giving total impedance in rectangular and polar form.
  2. Find input current, VX and both branch-current phasors.
  3. Find the R₁ drop and check KCL and VAB=VR1+VX. State whether input current leads or lags the applied voltage.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_b=R_3+Z_C\), \(Z_p=(1/R_2+1/Z_b)^{-1}\), \(Z_{AB}=R_1+Z_p\), \(V_X=IZ_p\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先完成 R₃ 与 C 的串联，再与 R₂ 并联，最后加入 R₁。

HW 2 · a phased current source at one node · 08

Keep all three parallel branch currents

The source \(i_s(t)=0.80\cos(1000t+\pi/2)\;\mathrm A\) injects current upward into node A. Separate parallel branches R=10 Ω, L=20 mH and C=100 μF connect A to ground. Define vA positive at A and all branch currents downward.

  1. Copy the circuit and draw the impedance version with source phasor. Calculate total admittance and equivalent impedance.
  2. Find VA in rectangular/polar form and write vA(t). Compute IR, IL and IC and verify KCL using complex components.
  3. Predict vA(0) and vA(π/2000 s). Explain why adding the three current magnitudes is not a valid KCL check; optionally verify the two voltage samples in a late steady-state simulation cycle.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Y=1/R+1/(j\omega L)+j\omega C\), \(V_A=I_s/Y\), \(I_R=V_A/R\), \(I_L=V_A/(j\omega L)\), \(I_C=j\omega CV_A\).
中文提示（不含解答）

并联相加的是导纳；电感和电容的导纳符号相反，但本题不会完全抵消。

HW 2 · divider and explicit voltage polarity · 09

Reverse the probes across an inductor output

The source \(v_s(t)=30\cos(2000t-\pi/6)\;\mathrm V\) connects through R=10 Ω to node A. L=5 mH connects A to ground; source negative is grounded. Define clockwise current from source through R and L. The output probes are deliberately reversed: vo=Vground−vA, with positive probe at ground and negative probe at A.

  1. Draw the impedance circuit and find I. First calculate the passive inductor drop VL=vA−Vground, then obtain Vo with the stated reversed polarity. Give Vo in rectangular/polar form.
  2. Write vo(t), find the resistor drop VR, and verify the correct KVL relationship Vs=VR−Vo.
  3. Predict vo(0) and vo(π/4000 s). Explain how swapping the output probes changes phase and waveform sign without changing the circuit current.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(I=V_s/(R+j\omega L)\), \(V_L=V_s(j\omega L)/(R+j\omega L)\), \(V_o=-V_L\), \(V_R=IR\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先按被动符号求电感电压，再因探针反接乘以 −1；不要更改电路电流的参考方向。

HW 2 · source conversion, divider and branch checks · 10

A capacitor feeds a parallel RL node

The source \(v_s(t)=20\sin(1000t+\pi/2)\;\mathrm V\) connects through C=50 μF to node A. R=20 Ω and L=20 mH each connect A to ground. Output vo is positive at A. Source current flows through C toward A, and both load currents are downward.

  1. Find the cosine-reference source phasor, load impedance, total input impedance and source current. Draw the impedance circuit with all reference labels.
  2. Find Vo in rectangular/polar form and write vo(t). Compute both load currents and the capacitor drop, then verify KCL and KVL.
  3. Predict vo(0) and vo(π/2000 s). For optional LTspice, give frequency in Hz and sine-source phase; compare both values after transients settle with a reasonable plot scale, units and cursor readings.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_p=(1/R+1/(j\omega L))^{-1}\), \(I_s=V_s/(Z_C+Z_p)\), \(V_o=I_sZ_p\), \(I_R=V_o/R\), \(I_L=V_o/(j\omega L)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

源的正弦表达式可先化成余弦；输出是整个并联负载的电压，不是输入电容的压降。
