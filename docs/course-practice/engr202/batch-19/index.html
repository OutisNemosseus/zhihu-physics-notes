HW 2 · series RLC, current phasor and LTspice verification · 01

Find the current in a series RLC loop

An ideal voltage source drives the series circuit shown: R = 36 Ω, L = 6 mH, and C = 4 μF. The source is \(v_s(t)=150\cos(5000t-\pi/12)\;\mathrm{V}\). Node B is the reference, and i(t) flows clockwise from the source’s positive terminal through R, L, and C. Assume sinusoidal steady state.

Use peak-amplitude, cosine-reference phasors. Time t is in seconds, angular frequency is in rad/s, and angles inside the written cosine functions are in radians. Report phasor angles in degrees with the correct quadrant and include units.

  1. Draw the complete time-domain circuit and its phasor-domain equivalent. Show the calculation of each reactive impedance, the source phasor, the reference node, and the current direction.
  2. Show the total series impedance, then find the current phasor I in rectangular and polar form.
  3. Write the steady-state expression for i(t), including its amplitude, angular frequency, phase, and units.
  4. Verify your expression with an LTspice transient simulation. Include the schematic and a settled-waveform plot with sensible time and vertical scales and visible units. Display cursor readouts at two distinct times in the settled region. For each actual cursor time, substitute that time into your analytical expression and explicitly compare the calculated and simulated values. State the source frequency in Hz and its sine-source phase. Use the current or voltage reference defined in the diagram; document any sign reversal needed for the displayed trace. Keep enough digits during calculation to make the comparison meaningful.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\quad Z_L=j\omega L,\quad Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\). For \(X=a+jb\), \(|X|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\) and \(\arg X=\operatorname{atan2}(b,a)\). A peak phasor \(X=|X|\angle\phi\) gives \(x(t)=|X|\cos(\omega t+\phi)\), with \(\phi\) in radians inside the cosine. Series circuit: \(Z_{\mathrm{eq}}=R+Z_L+Z_C\), \(I=V_s/Z_{\mathrm{eq}}\). For a sine-source entry, \(f=\omega/(2\pi)\) Hz and \(\phi_{\sin}=\phi_{\cos}+90^\circ\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先分别计算电感、电容阻抗，再相加求总阻抗。相量采用峰值余弦参考；由直角坐标转极坐标时检查象限。仿真中要按图中顺时针方向读取电流，并把两个真实游标时刻逐一代入手算式；注意毫秒、秒与角度、弧度的转换。

HW 2 · step-by-step nested series–parallel impedance · 02

Reduce a six-element impedance ladder

Find the equivalent input impedance Zin seen at terminals A–B of the ladder shown. The labeled impedances already apply at one common fixed frequency, so no separate frequency is needed. B is the reference node, and the input-current reference enters terminal A.

The connections are: A to X, −j4 Ω; X to B, j5 Ω; X to Y, 8 Ω; Y to B, −j3 Ω; Y to Z, j7 Ω; and Z to B, 6 Ω. The X–B and Y–B components are shunt branches, not series elements in the top path.

  1. Redraw the circuit after each series or parallel reduction, starting with the right-hand end. For every step, identify which impedances are being combined and show the complex-number calculation.
  2. Continue until only a single impedance remains between A and B. Give Zin in both rectangular and polar form, with Ω and a quadrant-correct angle in degrees. Keep extra digits in intermediate values.
Reference / 必要约定与公式Series: \(Z_s=Z_a+Z_b\). Parallel: \(Z_p=(1/Z_a+1/Z_b)^{-1}=Z_aZ_b/(Z_a+Z_b)\). Complex division: \(z_1/z_2=z_1z_2^*/|z_2|^2\). For \(Z=a+jb\), \(|Z|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\), \(\arg Z=\operatorname{atan2}(b,a)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

从最右端开始，先找没有分支的串联元件，再逐层向左合并。只有两端节点完全相同的两组阻抗才能并联。每次合并后重画节点关系；不要把顶端所有元件直接相加。

HW 2 · current-source RLC network, node voltage and LTspice · 03

Find node voltage from a three-branch current-source circuit

An ideal current source injects \(i_s(t)=640\cos(10000t+\pi/6)\;\mathrm{mA}\) upward from reference node B into node A. Three branches connect A to B: (1) R1 = 12 Ω in series with L = 0.9 mH, (2) R2 = 18 Ω, and (3) C = 6 μF. Node X is the junction between R1 and L. Define vA(t) = v(A) − v(B); all passive branch-current references point from A toward B. Assume sinusoidal steady state.

Use peak-amplitude, cosine-reference phasors. Time t is in seconds, angular frequency is in rad/s, and angles inside the written cosine functions are in radians. Report phasor angles in degrees with the correct quadrant and include units.

  1. Draw the complete time-domain and phasor-domain circuits. Show the source phasor, all element impedances and their calculations, and all reference labels. Convert the source magnitude from mA to A before using impedances in Ω.
  2. Reduce the network or use its equivalent admittance to find VA. Show the intermediate complex arithmetic and report the voltage phasor in rectangular and polar form.
  3. Write the steady-state expression for vA(t) with its amplitude, angular frequency, phase, and units.
  4. Verify your expression with an LTspice transient simulation. Include the schematic and a settled-waveform plot with sensible time and vertical scales and visible units. Display cursor readouts at two distinct times in the settled region. For each actual cursor time, substitute that time into your analytical expression and explicitly compare the calculated and simulated values. State the source frequency in Hz and its sine-source phase. Use the current or voltage reference defined in the diagram; document any sign reversal needed for the displayed trace. Keep enough digits during calculation to make the comparison meaningful.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\quad Z_L=j\omega L,\quad Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\). For \(X=a+jb\), \(|X|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\) and \(\arg X=\operatorname{atan2}(b,a)\). A peak phasor \(X=|X|\angle\phi\) gives \(x(t)=|X|\cos(\omega t+\phi)\), with \(\phi\) in radians inside the cosine. \(Y_{\mathrm{eq}}=1/(R_1+j\omega L)+1/R_2+j\omega C\), \(V_A=I_s/Y_{\mathrm{eq}}\). \(1\;\mathrm{mA}=10^{-3}\;\mathrm{A}\). For a sine-source entry, \(f=\omega/(2\pi)\) Hz and \(\phi_{\sin}=\phi_{\cos}+90^\circ\).
中文提示（不含解答）

R₁ 与 L 先串联合并，再与 R₂、C 两条支路并联。电流源向 A 注入电流，输出是 A 对 B 的电压。源幅值给的是 mA；进入 I×Z 或 I/Y 时先换成 A。仿真应验证节点电压，并将两个游标时刻的读数与手算值对应。

HW 2 · composite impedance voltage divider · 04

Use voltage division across a series RC segment

The ideal source \(v_s(t)=80\cos(5000t)\;\mathrm{V}\) drives the single series loop shown. Starting at the source’s positive terminal, the elements are R1 = 180 Ω, L = 120 mH, R2 = 320 Ω, and C = 400 nF, returning to reference node B. Node A lies between L and R2. Define vo(t) = v(A) − v(B), so the output is across the complete downstream R2–C series segment. The loop current reference is clockwise. Assume sinusoidal steady state.

Use peak-amplitude, cosine-reference phasors. Time t is in seconds, angular frequency is in rad/s, and angles inside the written cosine functions are in radians. Report phasor angles in degrees with the correct quadrant and include units.

  1. Calculate the element impedances and identify the two series impedance groups on either side of the output node. Write the complex voltage-divider equation appropriate to the marked output polarity.
  2. Use voltage division to obtain Vo in rectangular and polar form, showing the complex arithmetic. Then write the steady-state expression for vo(t), with units and the correct phase quadrant.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(Z_R=R,\quad Z_L=j\omega L,\quad Z_C=1/(j\omega C)\). For \(X=a+jb\), \(|X|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\) and \(\arg X=\operatorname{atan2}(b,a)\). A peak phasor \(X=|X|\angle\phi\) gives \(x(t)=|X|\cos(\omega t+\phi)\), with \(\phi\) in radians inside the cosine. For two series impedance groups, \(V_o=V_s Z_{\mathrm{out}}/(Z_{\mathrm{upstream}}+Z_{\mathrm{out}})\). Determine each group from the marked output terminals. \(1\;\mathrm{nF}=10^{-9}\;\mathrm{F}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先确认输出的两个测量节点：A 到 B 包含 R₂ 和 C，不只是电容。分压分子必须是整个输出段阻抗，分母是整条串联回路阻抗。nF 要换成 F；复数除法后再转为时域。
