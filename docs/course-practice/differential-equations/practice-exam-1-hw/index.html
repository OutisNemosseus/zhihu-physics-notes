3. Verify a solution and apply an initial condition

对应 Wk1-1, Q13 · Wk1-1 原作业

For x > 0, verify the proposed family by differentiation and substitution. Then find C and the particular solution satisfying y(1)=4.

\[y=x^2+\frac{C}{x^2},\qquad xy^{\prime}+2y=4x^2\]
查看本题参考答案与步骤（做完再看）

Differentiate the proposed family. Multiplying by x and adding 2y cancels the C terms. The condition gives 1+C=4, hence C=3.

\[y^{\prime}=2x-\frac{2C}{x^3},\quad xy^{\prime}+2y=4x^2,\quad y=x^2+\frac{3}{x^2}\]

9. Nonlinear separable IVP

对应 Wk2-1, Q5 · Wk2-1 原作业

Solve the IVP explicitly. Also give the maximal interval containing x=1 on which this solution solves the DE.

\[\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{2y^2}{\sqrt{x}},\qquad y(1)=\frac{1}{10}\]
查看本题参考答案与步骤（做完再看）

Separate to y to the power -2 dy = 2x to the power -1/2 dx. Integrate to -1/y=4 sqrt(x)+C. At x=1, C=-14. The DE requires x>0; the first singularity to the right is x=49/4.

\[y=\frac{1}{14-4\sqrt{x}},\qquad 0<x<\frac{49}{4}\]

10. Choose the correct square-root branch

对应 Wk2-1, Q4; Wk3-2, Q7 · Wk2-1 原作业 · Wk3-2 原作业

Solve the IVP. State its maximal interval of existence and explain the sign of the square root.

\[\frac{dx}{dt}=\frac{4}{x},\qquad x(0)=-3\]
查看本题参考答案与步骤（做完再看）

Multiply by x: x dx=4 dt. Thus x squared=8t+C and C=9. Choose the negative branch to satisfy x(0)=-3. At t=-9/8, x reaches zero and the DE is undefined.

\[x(t)=-\sqrt{9+8t},\qquad -\frac{9}{8}<t<\infty\]

11. Implicit-power answer

对应 Wk2-1, Q9-10; Wk3-2, Q6 · Wk2-1 原作业 · Wk3-2 原作业

For x > 0, solve the IVP near x=1. Express your answer as y cubed in terms of x.

\[\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{2+3x}{xy^2},\qquad y(1)=2\]
查看本题参考答案与步骤（做完再看）

Multiply by y squared and separate. Integrate, multiply by 3, and substitute x=1, y=2: 8=9+C, so C=-1.

\[\frac{y^3}{3}=2\ln x+3x+C_1,\qquad y^3=6\ln x+9x-1\]

For Question 11, the implicit-power formula specifies the IVP branch near x=1. It is not a classical solution at a point where y=0, because the original DE divides by y squared.

15. Standardize before finding the factor

对应 Wk3-2, Q19; Wk2-2, Q5, Q9 · Wk2-2 原作业 · Wk3-2 原作业

\[xy^{\prime}+y=\frac{4}{x}+6x,\qquad y(1)=5\]
查看本题参考答案与步骤（做完再看）

Divide the equation by x: y'+y/x=4/x squared+6. Since x>0, use the factor x. The original left side is already the derivative of xy. Integrate to xy=4ln x+3x squared+C. The initial condition gives C=2.

\[\mu=x,\qquad y=\frac{4\ln x+3x^2+2}{x},\qquad x>0\]

16. A filling mixing tank

对应 Wk2-2, Q10; Wk3-2, Q4 · Wk2-2 原作业 · Wk3-2 原作业

A 40-gallon tank initially contains 20 gallons of water with 4 pounds of salt. Brine with concentration 1 lb/gal enters at 3 gal/min. Well-mixed liquid leaves at 1 gal/min. Let Q(t) be the pounds of salt after t minutes.
(a) Find V(t) and the time when the tank fills.
(b) Write and solve the IVP for Q(t), valid until the tank fills.
(c) How many pounds of salt are in the tank when full? Give an exact answer and a decimal to two places.

查看本题参考答案与步骤（做完再看）

Volume is 20+2t, so the tank fills at t=10 min. Salt enters at 3 lb/min and leaves at Q/(20+2t) lb/min. An integrating factor is sqrt(20+2t). A particular solution is Q=20+2t.

\[Q^{\prime}=3-\frac{Q}{20+2t},\ Q(0)=4,\qquad Q=20+2t-16\sqrt{\frac{20}{20+2t}}\]
\[Q(10)=40-8\sqrt{2}\approx 28.69\ \mathrm{lb}\]

17. Newton cooling

对应 Wk3-2, Q12 · Wk3-2 原作业

An object starts at 90 degrees C in a room maintained at 20 degrees C. After 10 minutes it is at 60 degrees C.
(a) Write the DE using T'=k(T-20) and determine k.
(b) Find T(t).
(c) How many minutes after the start will T reach 40 degrees C? Give an exact expression and a decimal to two places.

查看本题参考答案与步骤（做完再看）

Solve T-20=70 exp(kt). The 10-minute reading gives exp(10k)=4/7. For 40 degrees, exp(kt)=2/7.

\[k=\frac{\ln(4/7)}{10},\quad T=20+70(4/7)^{t/10},\quad t=\frac{10\ln(2/7)}{\ln(4/7)}\approx 22.39\ \mathrm{min}\]

The model is A'=0.05A-1000, A(0)=8000. Solve and set A=0. The physical account model stops at depletion.

\[A=20000-12000e^{0.05t},\qquad t=20\ln(5/3)\approx 10.22\ \mathrm{years}\]

19. Falling object with quadratic resistance

对应 Wk3-2, Q13 · Wk3-2 原作业

A 10-kg object is moving downward with initial velocity -2 m/s. Take upward as positive and g=10 m/s squared. Resistance acts opposite the motion with magnitude k times speed squared; resistance is 4 N at a speed of 2 m/s.
(a) Find k and write the IVP for v(t) while v is negative.
(b) Solve for v(t).
(c) Find and interpret its terminal velocity; sketch the solution for t ≥ 0.

查看本题参考答案与步骤（做完再看）

From 4=k(2 squared), k=1 kg/m. For downward motion, resistance points upward: 10v'=-100+v squared. The solution stays negative and decreases from -2 toward -10; its graph is decreasing and concave up for t>=0.

\[v^{\prime}=-10+\frac{v^2}{10},\quad v(0)=-2,\quad v=-10\frac{3-2e^{-2t}}{3+2e^{-2t}}\]

Terminal velocity is -10 m/s: constant downward speed 10 m/s. The limiting acceleration is zero; upward drag balances weight.

20. Population models and maximum growth

对应 Wk3-2, Q15-17 · Wk3-2 原作业

(a) For the logistic model below, find both equilibria and use signs of P' to classify them. Find the population and growth rate at maximum growth. (Equilibrium classification is an added application of this practiced model.)
(b) For P'=0.02(P-100)(1-P/600), find the population giving the maximum growth rate on 100 < P < 600.
(c) For P'=0.3P ln(600/P), P > 0, find the population giving the maximum growth rate.

\[P^{\prime}=0.4P\left(1-\frac{P}{600}\right)\]
查看本题参考答案与步骤（做完再看）

(a) Equilibria: 0 and 600. The signs of P' in the intervals P<0, 0600 are negative, positive, negative. Thus 0 is unstable and 600 is asymptotically stable (using the mathematical extension to real P). For physical nonnegative populations, 0 repels positive perturbations. The logistic growth rate is maximal at P=300, with P'=60 population-units per time-unit.
(b) The quadratic growth-rate function has zeros 100 and 600 and opens downward. Its maximum is at their midpoint, P=350.
(c) Differentiate the growth-rate function with respect to P: 0.3[ln(600/P)-1]=0. Thus P=600/e, about 220.73.
