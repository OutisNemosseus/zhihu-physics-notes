Easy · verification and initial data · 01

One equation, two candidates

Consider the differential equation \(y^{\prime}+y=e^{-t}\) and the two candidate functions \(u(t)=(t+2)e^{-t}\) and \(v(t)=(t-1)e^{-t}\).

  1. State the order and whether the equation is linear.
  2. Differentiate each candidate and substitute it into the differential equation. Which candidates satisfy the equation for every real t?
  3. Which candidates, if any, also satisfy the initial condition \(y(0)=2\)? Explain why checking the differential equation alone is not enough to verify an IVP.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\((fg)^{\prime}=f^{\prime}g+fg^{\prime}\). A first-order linear equation has the form \(y^{\prime}+p(t)y=q(t)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

分别检查“满足方程”和“满足初值”；乘积求导时两项都要保留。

Easy · nonautonomous slope field · 02

A moving zero-slope line

For \(y^{\prime}=t-y\), sketch a slope field at all 12 grid points with \(t\in\{0,1,2\}\) and \(y\in\{-1,0,1,2\}\). Use t on the horizontal axis and y on the vertical axis.

  1. Record the slope at each point before drawing its short segment.
  2. Find the line on which the field has horizontal segments. On which side of that line do solutions increase? On which side do they decrease?
  3. Test whether the line itself is a solution curve by comparing its derivative with the slope specified by the equation.
Reference / 必要约定与公式At \((t_0,y_0)\), the segment slope is \(f(t_0,y_0)\) for \(y^{\prime}=f(t,y)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

场中某条线上斜率为零，不代表那条线本身就是解；比较切线斜率。

Medium · separation and maximal interval · 03

A finite-time boundary

Solve the IVP \(y^{\prime}=(t+1)y^2\), \(y(0)=1\).

  1. Separate variables, integrate, and determine the integration constant.
  2. Give the maximal open interval containing t = 0 on which the solution is defined.
  3. Describe what happens to y as t approaches the forward endpoint of that interval.
  4. Identify any constant solution omitted when dividing by \(y^2\), and verify your IVP solution by substitution.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\int y^{-2}\,dy=-y^{-1}+C\). Check possible zero factors before dividing.
中文提示（不含解答）

解出表达式后再找分母为零的位置；选择包含初始时刻的连通区间。

Medium · separation without losing equilibria · 04

Between two equilibrium levels

On the stated domain \(t>-1\), consider \(y^{\prime}=(1-y^2)/(t+1)\), \(y(0)=0\).

  1. Find all constant solutions before separating variables.
  2. Solve the IVP explicitly using logarithms and exponentials; hyperbolic functions are not required.
  3. State the maximal interval allowed by the equation. Show that the IVP solution stays strictly between the two constant solutions at every finite time in that interval.
  4. Find the limits as \(t\to-1^+\) and as \(t\to\infty\).
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\dfrac{1}{1-y^2}=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac{1}{1-y}+\dfrac{1}{1+y}\right)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

部分分式积分后保留绝对值；再用初值所在的区间处理符号。

Medium · variable-coefficient linear IVP · 05

An integrating factor with no real singularity

Solve \(y^{\prime}+\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}y=\dfrac{6t}{1+t^2}\), with \(y(1)=1\).

  1. Find an integrating factor and show the product-derivative equation.
  2. Determine y(t), then check the differential equation and the initial condition.
  3. State the maximal interval containing t = 1. Find y(0) and \(\lim_{t\to\infty}y(t)\).
Reference / 必要约定与公式For \(y^{\prime}+p(t)y=q(t)\), \(\mu(t)=e^{\int p(t)\,dt}\) gives \((\mu y)^{\prime}=\mu q\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先识别分母的导数；区间由原方程系数和解的定义域共同决定。

Medium · integrating factors and periodic forcing · 06

Persistent forcing, fading memory

Solve \(y^{\prime}+2y=5\cos t\), \(y(0)=-1\).

  1. Use an integrating factor and show how the integration constant is fixed.
  2. Separate your solution into a periodic part and a term that approaches zero as \(t\to\infty\).
  3. Does y(t) itself have a finite limit as \(t\to\infty\)? Justify your answer using the periodic part.
  4. Check y(0) and the differential equation directly.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\mu(t)=e^{\int p(t)\,dt}\); integration by parts: \(\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du\).
中文提示（不含解答）

可以对指数乘三角函数连续分部积分；衰减项消失不表示整个解趋于常数。

Medium · learning model and parameter estimation · 07

Learning with a head start

A learning model predicts a score P(t), in points, after t hours of practice. The maximum score is 80 points, and \(P^{\prime}=k(80-P)\) with \(k>0\). The initial score is 20 points; after 4 hours it is 50 points.

  1. Derive P(t) and determine k, including its units.
  2. Find the total practice time needed to reach 72 points. Give an exact expression and a value to two decimal places.
  3. Find the initial improvement rate in points/hour. Does this model reach 80 points at a finite time? Explain from your formula.
Reference / 必要约定与公式If \(D=M-P\), then \(D^{\prime}=-P^{\prime}\). Use natural logarithms when solving an exponential equation.
中文提示（不含解答）

把“距离最高分还差多少”作为新变量；初始分数不是零。

Medium · Newton model with a change of ambient temperature · 08

Cooling in two environments

A sample starts at 90 °C in a room held at 24 °C. After 6 minutes, its temperature is 57 °C. At that instant it is moved into a chamber held at 6 °C. Assume Newton’s model \(T^{\prime}=-k(T-T_a)\) applies, the same positive k applies in both environments, and the sample temperature cannot jump.

  1. Determine k from the first six minutes.
  2. Give T(t) for \(0\le t\le6\) and for \(t\ge6\), with t measured from the original start.
  3. Find the total time from the original start until the sample first reaches 20 °C. Give an exact expression and a value to two decimals.
  4. Compare \(T^{\prime}(6^-)\) and \(T^{\prime}(6^+)\). Explain how T can be continuous even if these two rates differ.
Reference / 必要约定与公式Within a stage with constant ambient temperature \(T_a\), set \(D=T-T_a\) so that \(D^{\prime}=-kD\).
中文提示（不含解答）

第二阶段用交接温度作为初值，经过时间用 t − 6；最后报告总时间。

Synthesis · changing volume, concentration, and a maximum · 09

Salt in a shrinking tank

A tank initially contains 180 L of liquid and 3.6 kg of dissolved salt. Brine with concentration 0.08 kg/L enters at 2 L/min, while perfectly mixed liquid leaves at 5 L/min. Both rates stay constant until the tank empties. Assume no other salt or liquid losses.

  1. Write V(t), the volume in litres, and the physical time interval for the model. Set up the IVP for the salt mass S(t), in kg.
  2. Solve for S(t) and the concentration \(C(t)=S(t)/V(t)\) while the volume is positive.
  3. Find the first time C(t) reaches 0.05 kg/L. Give an exact expression and a value to two decimals.
  4. Does the salt mass initially rise or fall? Find when it reaches its maximum and give that maximum to three decimals. Explain why concentration and total salt mass need not move in the same direction.
  5. Find the one-sided limits of S and C as the tank empties. Explain why the concentration formula cannot be used as the concentration of liquid remaining in the tank at the exact emptying time.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V^{\prime}=q_{\rm in}-q_{\rm out}\); \(S^{\prime}=q_{\rm in}C_{\rm in}-q_{\rm out}S/V\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先跟踪体积，再写流出盐率；质量和浓度是不同变量，模型在空罐前才适用。

Synthesis · logistic growth and semistability · 10

The critical harvest

A managed fish population is modeled continuously by \(P^{\prime}=0.4P(1-P/120)-H\), where t is in months, P is measured in fish, and the constant harvest H is nonnegative in fish/month. Use this model only while \(P\ge0\), and stop an individual trajectory when it first reaches zero.

  1. Find the greatest H for which a nonnegative equilibrium exists. Call it \(H_{\rm crit}\).
  2. Set \(H=H_{\rm crit}\). Find every nonnegative equilibrium and draw a sign diagram on \(P\ge0\). Classify the equilibrium as attracting, repelling, or semistable, specifying the behavior on each side.
  3. For this critical harvest, solve the two IVPs \(P(0)=90\) and \(P(0)=30\). A substitution measuring displacement from the equilibrium may help.
  4. For each initial value, state the physical forward-time interval and describe its outcome. If a trajectory reaches zero, find when; if it does not, find its long-term limit.
  5. Explain why the two outcomes do not contradict the fact that both use the same differential equation.
Reference / 必要约定与公式Equilibria satisfy \(P^{\prime}=0\). An equilibrium is semistable when nearby trajectories approach from one side and move away on the other.
中文提示（不含解答）

先找无收获增长率的最大值；临界参数下检查平衡点两侧，别让鱼群数量继续变成负数。
