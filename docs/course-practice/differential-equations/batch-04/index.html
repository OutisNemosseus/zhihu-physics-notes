Easy · order, linearity, and direct verification · 01

A square root changes the verification

Consider the real differential equation \(y^{\prime}=\sqrt{1-y^2}\), where the square root is the nonnegative one, and the candidate \(y(t)=\sin t\). The independent variable t is dimensionless.

  1. Identify the independent variable, dependent variable, order, and whether the equation is linear in y.
  2. Differentiate the candidate and simplify the right-hand side after substitution. Verify that it solves the equation on \(-\pi/2<t<\pi/2\) and satisfies \(y(0)=0\).
  3. Does the same candidate solve the equation for every real t? Give a specific time at which the two sides disagree.
  4. For a general real solution, what restriction on y is required for the right-hand side to be real? Explain why this restriction alone is not a verification of a candidate.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\sqrt{a^2}=|a|\) for real a; \(\sin^2 t+\cos^2 t=1\). A first-order linear equation has the form \(y^{\prime}+p(t)y=q(t)\).
中文提示（不含解答）

平方根取非负值；化简平方的平方根时，注意绝对值，然后比较等号两侧。

Easy · slope fields and zero-slope curves · 02

A band of downward slopes

For \(y^{\prime}=(y-t)^2-1\), record the slope at all nine points with \(t\in\{0,1,2\}\) and \(y\in\{0,1,2\}\), then sketch a short slope segment at each point. Put t on the horizontal axis and y on the vertical axis; both variables are dimensionless.

  1. Find the two straight lines on which the field has horizontal segments.
  2. Describe the region with negative slopes and the regions with positive slopes.
  3. Test each zero-slope line as a proposed solution by comparing its own derivative with the differential equation.
  4. Can any constant function \(y(t)=c\) solve this equation on a nonempty open time interval? Justify without solving the equation.
Reference / 必要约定与公式A slope-field segment at \((t_0,y_0)\) has slope \(f(t_0,y_0)\) for \(y^{\prime}=f(t,y)\). A constant solution has derivative zero throughout its time interval.
中文提示（不含解答）

先用 y − t 的大小划分区域；检查一条曲线是否为解时，还要计算曲线自己的导数。

Medium · separation and two-sided behavior · 03

Separating a logarithmic feedback law

On the domain \(y>0\), solve \(y^{\prime}=-\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}\,y\ln y\), with \(y(0)=4\). Both t and y are dimensionless.

  1. Check for constant solutions in the stated domain before dividing by factors involving y.
  2. Separate variables and integrate. Keep track of the sign of \(\ln y\) selected by the initial value, and obtain an explicit solution.
  3. Verify the equation and initial condition. State the maximal open time interval containing zero.
  4. Find all real times when y = 2. Determine where the solution increases and decreases, its global maximum, and its limits as \(t\to-\infty\) and \(t\to\infty\).
  5. Does \(y=0\) qualify as an equilibrium of the equation as stated? Explain using its domain.
Reference / 必要约定与公式For \(y>0\) and \(y\ne1\), \(\int\dfrac{dy}{y\ln y}=\ln|\ln y|+C\). Also \(\dfrac{d}{dt}\ln(1+t^2)=\dfrac{2t}{1+t^2}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

可先把 ln y 当成一个整体；在除法过程中排除的常值解，要回到原方程检查。

Medium · separation and a globally defined implicit solution · 04

An implicit answer can be complete

Solve the IVP \(y^{\prime}=\dfrac{t}{1+y^2}\), \(y(0)=1\), with dimensionless t and y. You do not need to use a cubic formula.

  1. Separate variables and obtain an implicit relation between t and y, including the initial condition.
  2. Let \(g(u)=u+u^3/3\). Use its derivative and its limits as \(u\to\pm\infty\) to explain why your relation selects exactly one real y for every real t.
  3. Differentiate the implicit relation to verify the differential equation. State the maximal open time interval containing zero.
  4. Determine where y increases or decreases and find its global minimum. Explain whether \(y(-t)=y(t)\).
  5. Find the first nonnegative time when y = 2, exactly and to three decimals. Explain why y can never equal zero for this IVP.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\int(1+y^2)\,dy=y+y^3/3+C\). A continuous, strictly increasing function whose range is all real numbers takes each real value exactly once.
中文提示（不含解答）

不必强行解三次方程；研究左边关于 y 的单调性，能确定分支与可取值。

Medium · integrating factors and a parameter in the initial data · 05

Choose initial data for a bounded response

For dimensionless \(t>0\), consider the family of IVPs \(y^{\prime}+\dfrac{1}{t}y=\dfrac{e^{-t}}{t}\), \(y(1)=a\), where a is a real constant.

  1. Use an integrating factor to solve in terms of a. Display the product-derivative equation and verify the initial condition.
  2. Find the unique value of a for which the solution has a finite limit as \(t\to0^+\), and compute that limit.
  3. For smaller and larger values of a, determine the corresponding one-sided behavior as \(t\to0^+\).
  4. For the bounded member of the family, find \(\lim_{t\to\infty}y(t)\). Show that \(e^{-t}<y(t)<1\) for every \(t>0\), for example by comparing an integral with rectangles.
  5. State the maximal interval allowed by the original IVP. Does a finite endpoint limit make the original differential equation defined at t = 0?
Reference / 必要约定与公式For \(y^{\prime}+p(t)y=q(t)\), \(\mu(t)=e^{\int p(t)\,dt}\) gives \((\mu y)^{\prime}=\mu q\). Also \(\int_0^t e^{-u}\,du=1-e^{-t}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先保留初值参数；观察趋近零时分子是否留下非零常数，再处理可消去的情况。

Medium · Newton cooling with two unknown parameters · 06

Recover the room temperature from three readings

A sample cools in a room whose unknown constant temperature is A °C. Its temperature is 80 °C initially, 56 °C after 4 minutes, and 44 °C after 8 minutes. Assume \(T^{\prime}=-k(T-A)\), with \(k>0\), \(A<44\) °C, and t measured in minutes. Use this model for \(t\ge0\).

  1. Derive T(t) in terms of A and k.
  2. Use both later readings to determine A and k, including units. Explain why equal observation intervals imply the same ratio of temperature differences from A.
  3. Verify all three measurements using your model.
  4. Find the first time the sample reaches 40 °C, exactly and to two decimals. Find its initial cooling rate, with units.
  5. Does the sample ever reach the room temperature at a finite time? Justify from the formula.
Reference / 必要约定与公式For constant A, setting \(D=T-A\) gives \(D^{\prime}=-kD\). The units of k are inverse time, and temperature differences can be measured in °C.
中文提示（不含解答）

未知环境温度不能直接拿相邻读数相除；先写出相对于同一环境温度的温差。

Medium · linear growth model and a physical stopping time · 07

Growth can lose to a fixed withdrawal

A continuously modeled culture has population P(t), measured in organisms, and obeys \(P^{\prime}=0.08P-24\). Time t is in hours, 0.08 has units h\(^{-1}\), and 24 is a constant removal rate in organisms/hour. Use the model only while \(P\ge0\); stop a trajectory if it first reaches zero. Compare two trials with initial populations \(P(0)=200\) and \(P(0)=500\).

  1. Find the equilibrium population and classify it using the sign of \(P^{\prime}\) on its two sides.
  2. Use a shift by the equilibrium, or an integrating factor, to solve both IVPs.
  3. For the 200-organism trial, find the first time it reaches zero, exactly and to two decimals. State its physical forward-time interval.
  4. For the 500-organism trial, find when it first reaches 1000 organisms, exactly and to two decimals, and describe its long-term behavior under the model.
  5. Calculate both initial net rates. Explain why a positive proportional-growth coefficient does not guarantee that both populations increase.
Reference / 必要约定与公式For \(P^{\prime}=rP-h\) with \(r>0\), the change of variable \(z=P-h/r\) gives \(z^{\prime}=rz\). Use natural logarithms when solving for time.
中文提示（不含解答）

先找净变化率为零的数量，再看初始值位于哪一侧；数量到零后不要继续作物理解释。

Medium · logistic separation above carrying capacity · 08

Logistic recovery from overcrowding

A habitat has carrying capacity 900 organisms. A temporarily overcrowded population is modeled by \(P^{\prime}=0.12P(1-P/900)\), \(P(0)=1500\), where t is in days and 0.12 has units day\(^{-1}\). Interpret the population model for \(t\ge0\).

  1. Find the nonnegative equilibria and classify them using slope signs on \(P\ge0\).
  2. Solve the IVP by separation. Explain how the initial population affects the sign of the constant in the denominator of the explicit solution.
  3. Find the initial rate, and the first time the population reaches 1000 organisms, exactly and to two decimals.
  4. Show that the population stays above 900 at every finite nonnegative time. Find its forward-time limit.
  5. As a separate mathematical question, find the maximal open interval containing zero on which your explicit IVP solution is finite. Describe its behavior at the backward endpoint; do not treat that backward extension as measured population history.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(\dfrac{1}{P(1-P/K)}=\dfrac1P+\dfrac1{K-P}\), for \(P\ne0,K\). Check equilibrium solutions separately before dividing by these factors.
中文提示（不含解答）

初值在承载量上方，部分分式积分时不能提前假设承载量减去种群数量为正。

Synthesis · mixing with evaporation and a changing input · 09

Evaporation removes water but leaves the salt

A tank initially contains 60 L of liquid with 3.0 kg of dissolved salt. Brine enters at 3 L/min, perfectly mixed liquid is drained at 2 L/min, and water evaporates at 1 L/min. Evaporation carries away no salt. During a 20-minute experiment the incoming concentration is \(C_{\rm in}(t)=0.020+0.0010t\) kg/L, with t entered in minutes; the coefficient 0.0010 has units kg/(L·min). Assume ideal mixing, constant volume-flow rates, no other losses, and use this model only for \(0\le t\le20\).

  1. Find the tank volume and set up the IVP for salt mass S(t), in kg. Explain why the evaporation rate does not multiply the tank concentration in the salt-loss term.
  2. Solve for S(t) with an integrating factor, and obtain the tank concentration \(C(t)\).
  3. Find the time and value of the minimum salt mass during the experiment, with time to two decimals and mass to three decimals. Give the corresponding concentration to five decimals.
  4. At t = 20, compute S, C, the incoming concentration, and \(S^{\prime}\), with units. Is the salt mass rising even though the incoming concentration is smaller than the tank concentration? Reconcile your conclusion with the two liquid flow rates.
  5. Compute the total salt entering and the total salt drained during the 20 minutes. Check the salt-mass balance using your final mass. State why this fitted input law should not automatically be extrapolated beyond the experiment.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(V^{\prime}=q_{\rm in}-q_{\rm drain}-q_{\rm evap}\), \(S^{\prime}=q_{\rm in}C_{\rm in}-q_{\rm drain}S/V\), and \(C=S/V\). Total salt in or out is the time integral of its mass-flow rate.
中文提示（不含解答）

先分别列水量平衡与盐量平衡；蒸发影响体积，但这里不会直接带走盐。

Synthesis · loaded RC model and a threshold interval · 10

A voltage pulse with a leakage path

The circuit shown has an ideal voltage source E(t), a series resistor \(R_s=100\) Ω, and a parallel combination of a leakage resistor \(R_\ell=300\) Ω and capacitor \(C=0.020\) F. The capacitor voltage v(t), in volts, is positive at the upper node and initially equals 3 V. Source current \(i_s\) is positive toward that node; leakage current \(i_\ell\) and capacitor current \(i_C\) are positive downward. The ideal source remains connected, permits current in either direction, and is set to 12 V for \(0\le t<4\) seconds and 0 V for \(t\ge4\). Setting an ideal source to 0 V makes its terminals a conducting connection. Capacitor voltage is continuous at t = 4. Apply the differential equation within each stage and use one-sided currents at the switching time.

  1. Use the branch-current formulas to derive a first-order IVP for v(t), then solve in both stages and match the voltage at t = 4.
  2. Find the common time constant of the two stages and the steady voltage that would result if the source stayed at 12 V indefinitely. Explain why that steady voltage differs from the source voltage.
  3. Calculate \(i_s(0^+)\), \(i_\ell(0^+)\), and \(i_C(0^+)\), and check current conservation.
  4. Compute v(4), \(i_s(4^-)\), \(i_s(4^+)\), \(i_C(4^-)\), and \(i_C(4^+)\). Give voltage to three decimals and currents in mA to two decimals. Explain why these current jumps are compatible with a continuous capacitor voltage.
  5. Find the full time interval on which v(t) is at least 6 V, and its duration, with times to three decimals. Find the maximum capacitor charge and when it occurs.
Reference / 必要约定与公式\(i_s=(E-v)/R_s\), \(i_\ell=v/R_\ell\), \(i_C=Cv^{\prime}\), \(i_s=i_\ell+i_C\), and \(Q=Cv\). For \(v^{\prime}+av=b\) with constant \(a>0\), the time constant is \(1/a\).
中文提示（不含解答）

先在上方节点写电流守恒；泄漏电阻一直存在，第二阶段也要保留两个电阻的作用。
