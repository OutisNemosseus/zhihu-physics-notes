6.1

Basic counting

计数基础

7 组题
§6.1 · 2#

乘法原理 · 楼层与办公室

An office building contains 27 floors and has 37 offices on each floor. How many offices are in the building?

§6.1 · 8#

不重复的三字母缩写

How many different three-letter initials with none of the letters repeated can people have?

§6.1 · 12#

不同长度的位串

How many bit strings are there of length six or less, not counting the empty string?

§6.1 · 14#

固定首尾的位串

How many bit strings of length n, where n is a positive integer, start and end with 1s?

§6.1 · 16#

至少包含一个 x

How many strings are there of four lowercase letters that have the letter x in them?

§6.1 · 18#

DNA 序列

How many 5-element DNA sequences satisfy each condition? Use the alphabet {A, C, G, T}.

a) end with A?

b) start with T and end with G?

c) contain only A and T?

d) do not contain C?

§6.1 · 28#

两种车牌格式

How many license plates can be made using either three digits followed by three uppercase English letters or three uppercase English letters followed by three digits?

6.3

Permutations & combinations

排列与组合

7 组题
§6.3 · 10#

候选人排序

There are six different candidates for governor of a state. In how many different orders can the names of the candidates be printed on a ballot?

§6.3 · 12#

位串中的 1 的个数

How many bit strings of length 12 contain

  1. exactly three 1s?
  2. at most three 1s?
  3. at least three 1s?
  4. an equal number of 0s and 1s?
§6.3 · 22c#

相邻字母块

How many permutations of the letters ABCDEFGH contain the strings BA and FGH?

§6.3 · 24#

排队与不相邻限制

How many ways are there for 10 women and six men to stand in a line so that no two men stand next to each other?

Original hint: First position the women and then consider possible positions for the men.

§6.3 · 30#

判断题答案序列

A professor writes 40 discrete mathematics true/false questions. Of the statements in these questions, 17 are true. If the questions can be positioned in any order, how many different answer keys are possible?

§6.3 · 32#

委员会：至少一人

Seven women and nine men are on the faculty in the mathematics department at a school. How many ways are there to select a committee of five members of the department if

  1. at least one woman must be on the committee?
  2. at least one woman and at least one man must be on the committee?
§6.3 · 36#

委员会：人数比较

Suppose that a department contains 10 men and 15 women. How many ways are there to form a committee with six members if it must have more women than men?

§6.4 · 8#
