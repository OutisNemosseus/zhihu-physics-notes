WHW 3 pattern · constructive or nonconstructive proof · 07

Prove that at least one large integer is not a square

Prove that at least one of the two integers \(3\cdot10^{90}+40\) and \(3\cdot10^{90}+41\) is not a perfect square. Is the proof you give constructive or nonconstructive? Justify your classification.

For this question, a constructive proof identifies a particular listed integer and proves that it is not a perfect square; a nonconstructive proof establishes that at least one has this property without identifying which one. A perfect square is the square of a nonnegative integer.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(N\text{ is a perfect square}\iff N=k^2\text{ for some }k\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\).
中文提示（不含解答）

证明后再判断自己的证明是否指出了一个具体符合结论的数；不用十进制近似来替代证明。

WHW 3 pattern · prove or disprove · 08

Test a rational-power claim

Prove or disprove this statement:

For every positive rational number a and every negative rational number b, the number \(a^b\) is rational.

Because \(a>0\), the power \(a^b\) has its usual real-number meaning. Give a proof if the statement is true, or a counterexample with justification if it is false. You may use a familiar irrational number whose irrationality you already know.

Reference / 必要约定与公式\(x\in\mathbb Q\iff x=p/q\text{ for some }p,q\in\mathbb Z,\ q\ne0\).
中文提示（不含解答）

这是对所有允许的 a、b 的断言；检查所用取值是否属于题目给出的范围，并说明结果为什么符合或违反结论。
